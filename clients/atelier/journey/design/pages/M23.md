# M23 · CRITICAL IMPACT · Attention held to the door ⊐

In-room · Leaving (phase 6) · 5–20 sec · From the farewell line to the door closing · **⊐ end-2**, designed as one with M22

Dominant inputs: **non-verbal** and **brand**; BE present (peak-end, affect, social proof); concept sets the collision (the next guest's M5 against this guest's end).

## CURRENT STATE

The line has been said and the nod given (M22). She turns, walks to the door, opens it, steps out onto Rakovski. The next guest may be at the counter or coming through the same door. Her state: "I'll come back" moving to "They were still with me at the door." Next: nothing in the room; the card on her desk, days later (M24).

## DO

1. After the line and the nod, the team member's attention stays with her: eyes at intervals, body loosely oriented to her path, until the door has closed behind her.
2. The next guest is already at the counter: the team member keeps the body angled to the departing guest for the two or three seconds until she is past the counter, then squares to the newcomer with eyes up (their M5). Sequence: line → nod → she passes the counter → eyes up for the next. The newcomer watches the farewell and reads it as what they will receive.
3. Two on the counter: the one who served her holds the attention to the door; the other takes the newcomer's eyes-up. No words in the handoff; an orientation transfer only.
4. If she looks back from the door: eyes meet, a second small nod, no new line, the resting warm face.
5. The door closes; attention releases to the room, unhurried. No remark to a colleague about her.
6. Nobody clears her cup, her table or her chair before the door has closed.
7. To-go at the counter: the same, from the counter to the door.
8. Seated guest leaving while the counter is busy: whoever has the sightline holds the attention; if nobody can, the farewell at the counter as she passes (M22) carries both moments and the eyes follow from there.
9. The room: the music does not rise, no machine starts, the till drawer does not open, in the seconds she is walking out; the door closes softly (dependency).

## SAY

No script. If she says goodbye from the door, the answer is the nod, or her name, nothing more.

Filter test: nothing is said that could hurry, sell or talk past her; the moment is the test passed in silence.

## DON'T

- Don't turn your back to the next order before she is past the counter.
- Don't start the loud tasks (scoop into the water bath, till drawer, grinder) while she can still see your face.
- Don't clear her table or cup until the door has closed.
- Don't stare: attention is intermittent eyes and continuous orientation, not a fixed gaze.
- Don't call a "bye!" after her at the door; don't wave big.
- Don't comment on her after she has gone.
- Don't let the second team member perform a second farewell; a nod only.

## NON-VERBAL

- **Orientation** (NV C.4): the shoulders stay angled to her path and do not square to the next guest until she is past the counter; this is the whole moment.
- **Eyes** (NV C.2): intermittent: at the turn, at the door, once more if she looks back; between, the eyes may drop to the hands; never to the till or the queue. The library's failure mode governs: over-coached intensity reads as fixation.
- **Exit geometry, inverted** (NV A.4): the guest is exiting; the staff's exit is the slow release of attention, not an abrupt turn at the end of the line.
- **Face**: resting warm, intensity 3; unchanged when she is out of sight, because the next guest is watching it.
- **Hands**: a quiet task may continue (closing a lid); a loud one waits.
- **Pace** (NV E.1): the room's pace does not jump when the door closes.
- **Sound** (NV F.2): no rise in music or machine noise in the last seconds.
- **Timing** (NV E.3): from the line to the door closing, five to twenty seconds, read from her position in the room, nothing scheduled.

## BEHAVIORAL ECONOMICS

- **Peak-end** (BE 7): this is the literal end the remembering self samples: the last second of attention, or its absence. A back turned at second three overwrites the farewell line, however well said.
- **Duration neglect** (BE 7): twenty seconds that outweigh the ten minutes.
- **Affect heuristic** (BE 5): the newcomer is thin-slicing the room while this happens; the held attention is their first emotional read, before any word is said to them.
- **Social proof** (BE 11): the next guest sees that leaving here is warm; the ritual proves itself without a line.
- **Decision here**: none for her. One for the team member (turn now or in three seconds), which the design makes by rule so that it is not made by stress.

## RECOVERY

| If … | → |
| --- | --- |
| The newcomer speaks before she is past the counter | Eyes up to the newcomer with a small nod (their M5 within 10 seconds holds); the body stays half to her path for the last second; then square. Eyes to one, shoulders to the other, for two seconds. |
| Three or more waiting and one person on the counter | The held attention becomes eyes only: the body may square to the queue after the line; the eyes still go to the door once. This is a named degradation at peak (operations brief §2: a band that cannot be staffed is discussed, never silently degraded). R-QUEUE. |
| She leaves something behind (the card, an umbrella) | Go to the door or step out, hand it over with her name, no line, back to the counter. The one case where the team member leaves the station. |
| She leaves upset | Attention held the same; no second nod unless she looks back; Menelaos informed the same day. R-UPSET. |
| A regular lingers at the door to talk | Receive it; the length is set by the counter's needs through the body (a half-turn back toward the station), not by a line; the farewell is not repeated. |
| The door slams or sticks | Nothing said; logged for the designer brief revision (soft close). |

## HIGH additions

**Brand-coherence test.** Role Peer: a friend's attention walks you to the door; the Role holds without a word. Connection statement "your day matters to us as much as it does to you" is shown literally by not turning away. Lover 70: present. Innocent 30: nothing performed, nothing added. Holds.

**Story block:** 7, The New World; the card leaves with her.

**Bookended end (S3-06).** The threshold opened with eyes before words (M5); the end closes with eyes after words. The same discipline, in reverse, from the same person.

**The collision with M5.** The counter's structural problem: the end of one guest and the threshold of the next on the same face, in the same three seconds. Resolved by the two-second rule (shoulders to her, eyes to the newcomer) and, at peak, by two on the counter. With one on, the degradation is named above, not hidden.

**Operational dependencies.** Sightline from the counter to the door and the tables (to confirm). Two on the counter at Rakovski peak (summer afternoons; Ring Mall to confirm). The table-clearing rule in the operations brief. The door's soft close (designer brief revision candidate). Music and machine noise managed so nothing rises in the last seconds (v3 §7.7 Sound: minimal machine noise).
