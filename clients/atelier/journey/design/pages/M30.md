# M30 · HIGH IMPACT · Delivery with story cards

Operational · The partner table (phase 8) · per delivery (cadence **to confirm**) · owner: floor (the Lab)

Dominant inputs: **concept** (the Lab, the route) and **brand** (commitment 4); non-verbal (handling, timing); BE (commitment).

## CURRENT STATE

The partner receives the case. "It arrived with its story."

## DO

1. Made fresh that morning at the Lab in small batches (commitment 1); the delivery note states the date and the flavours.
2. Story cards in every delivery, counted to the units plus a margin; 25–40 words, the question → what it is and where it was made → an invitation to pause (v3 §4.8); BG and EN as the partner needs.
3. The case branded with the descriptor "Gelato & Tiramisu"; functional; kraft and no plastics where insulation allows (v3 §7.4).
4. Handed to a named person at the venue, the one line, then leave. No sales talk at the door.
5. B2B tiramisu with the card inside the box; gelato trays with the cards in a sleeve on top.
6. Delivered in the partner's prep window, never during their service (NV E.3).

## SAY

"Fresh this morning. The cards are on top." **Pass.** Only when true.

## DON'T

- A delivery without cards; cards as a loose stack like flyers; "please display our cards" (the agreement already says so); an upsell at the door.

## NON-VERBAL

The case set down, not dropped (NV D.3); the delivery timed to the partner's quiet hour (NV E.3).

## BEHAVIORAL ECONOMICS

Consistency builds the partner's default (BE 6): cards every time, so serving them becomes what happens when nobody decides.

## RECOVERY

| If … | → |
| --- | --- |
| Cards forgotten | A second run the same day, at the Lab's cost. |
| A delivery will be late | The partner told before, not after. |
| A quality issue | Replaced by the Lab; Menelaos informed; never argued. |

**State arc:** Expected → Received → Ready for the table

**Success metric:** Deliveries with cards: 100 %, counted at dispatch; cards per unit delivered on the dispatch sheet.

**Target emotion:** trusted

**Brand pillar:** Mission; standing commitments 1 and 4
