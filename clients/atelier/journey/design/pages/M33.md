# M33 · HIGH IMPACT · The story card on the partner's table (B2B) — brief page

**Draft — book edition v1.0 unsigned (open-facts 39); observation move not held.**

B2B · Phase 8 The partner table · ~10 min for the partner's guest · Partner venues (names, count, tenure: **CONFIRM**, open-facts 19) · Partner-led · markers none · weight provisional; thin

**Brief page, not a design page.** The moment is performed by a third party (the partner's staff and table), so the brand's instrument is a brief and a check, not a DO/SAY/DON'T (handoff cluster 8). This page is the partner brief.

Dominant inputs: **brand** (messages 5, 7; the Vision: the brand found at partner tables) · **concept** (the partner's own service model, unknown per partner). BE and NV are the partner's; the brand asks for one behaviour.

## What the partner is asked to hold

1. **The story card goes on the table** where Atelier gelato or tiramisu is served, one per table or one per dessert as agreed (format and text of the story cards: **CONFIRM**, open-facts 48).
2. **The partner's staff are briefed once** by the Lab (Menelaos or a named Lab person) on what the card is and that it asks a question; they are not asked to say a script. If a partner chooses to ask the question aloud, the words are the brand's, unchanged: "What are we celebrating today?" / „Какво празнуваме днес?" (Bible §7).
3. **The product is served as delivered**: no rebranding as the venue's own "homemade" (guardrails 9), no origin claims beyond the card.
4. **No discount or promotion** uses the Atelier name without the approver's sign-off (guardrails 3, 10).

## The brand's one check

**Is a story card on the table where our gelato or tiramisu is served?** Yes / no, by sight, with a photo for the log.

## Periodic check

Quarterly by default (first round: within the first quarter after the cards exist, 48), by Menelaos or a named Lab person, unannounced, as a guest; logged per partner with the date and the answer. A "no" opens **R-PARTNER**.

## What the brand gives the partner

Morning delivery with the cards on top (M32); a replacement stock of cards on request; the B2B voice in Вие (Bible §6).
