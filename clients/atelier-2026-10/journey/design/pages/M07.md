# M07 · CRITICAL IMPACT · The question ★

In-room · The threshold (phase 2) · ~5 sec plus the beat · Counter, face to face · **★ signature**

Design note: written 2026-11-01 as the first page of the set, out of the working order (peak M15 and end M22–M23 first) for the blind test; the schedule is in `../schedule.md` when the set is written.

Dominant inputs: **brand** and **non-verbal**; BE present; concept light (a counter, a jar, a person).

## CURRENT STATE

She has just been seen (M5): eyes met, a smile before words, her name if she is a regular. She is at the counter, the display in front of her, and she expects to be asked what she wants. Instead she is asked what today meant. She is still near her before-state (busy, a little drained); this is the first thing said to her that is about her and not about the order. Next: the beat (M8), then her answer or none (M9).

## DO

1. The team member who met her eyes at M5 asks the question. No handoff; the person who looked up is the person who asks.
2. Stand squared to her, one step back from the counter edge, hands empty and visible. The scoop is not in the hand.
3. One beat after the eyes meet, before any word about flavours, sizes, the menu or the queue: ask the question, once, at the volume of a conversation with one person.
4. Then the beat (M8): nothing. Hands still, eyes on her, face soft. No repeat, no "for example", no answer supplied. Two or three seconds; longer if she needs it.
5. Whatever comes is received at M9: a win lit up (shared joy, intensity 3, never party energy); "nothing" taken warmly and left alone; the Note still goes with the order.
6. A regular: the name, then the same line. The question is never dropped for someone who has heard it before.
7. A group: the line once, to the group; answers received one at a time; "we" already covers them.
8. A queue: the same line, the same volume, the same form, eyes on this guest, not on the line (the next guest overhears it: M6).
9. The room: the counter low enough for level eyes (v3 §7.2 direction, around 90 cm; the current height is to confirm); the Note jar in view beside the station so the question's promise is visible. Otherwise the moment inherits the room.

## SAY

**LOCKED** — default, every order:

> EN: "What are we celebrating today?"
> BG: „Какво празнуваме днес?"

Registers:

| Register | Line |
| --- | --- |
| Regular, name known | „Елена, какво празнуваме днес?" / "Elena, what are we celebrating today?" |
| Group | The default line, once, to the group |
| To-go, phone-in-hand, in a hurry | The default line, no shorter; the tasting (M11) is what shortens, never the question |
| Ring Mall counter | The default line until Evo decides otherwise (open dependency; diagnosis contradiction 4) |

Filter test (brand-book.md › Filter test: *Is this processing the guest?*): does not hurry, sells nothing, addressed to her. **Pass.** The register in the room is ти / warm "you" (Bible front page).

## DON'T

- Don't ask it after the order, while scooping, or with the payment. Asked late, it is an upsell line.
- Don't say "you": not "What are you celebrating?", not "Special occasion?", not "What's the occasion?" (which tells her she needs one).
- Don't supply answers: no "a birthday, maybe?", no "Friday, right?".
- Don't ask it to the display, the till or the queue. Eyes on her.
- Don't ask it louder for the queue's benefit, or softer to hide it.
- Don't drop it for a regular, a child's order, a to-go order, a long queue, a bad day.
- Don't react to "nothing" with a joke, a second try or a visible drop in warmth.
- Never "Next!", "Anything else?", "Treat yourself", "That's our bestseller" (v3 §8.1; Bible §4).

## NON-VERBAL

- **Geometry** (NV A.2, A.3): across the display, squared to her, one step back from the counter edge, not leaning over it. The counter at level-eye height is the geometry; the team member does not bend down to it.
- **Eyes** (NV C.2): on her for the line and the beat; released only when she begins to answer or looks down at the display.
- **Face**: open; the quick genuine smile from M5 held soft at intensity 2–3; not a grin, not a performance of interest.
- **Hands** (NV C.3): empty and still from the eyes meeting until she has answered or declined. The scoop is picked up after, not during.
- **Tone** (NV B.1): calm, light, sincere; asked as interest, not as a quiz. Falling, not rising, at the end.
- **Volume** (NV B.3): for one person; audible to the next in line because the room is quiet, not because it is aimed at them.
- **Pace** (NV B.2, E.3): a pause before the question (Bible §11), the line itself unhurried (about two seconds), then the beat.
- **Silence** (NV B.4): the beat is part of this moment's execution. The team member holds it; the guest ends it.
- **Timing**: inside the first thirty seconds of her being in the room; after the eyes, before any product word.

## BEHAVIORAL ECONOMICS

- **Thin-slicing** (BE 17; NV 3.2.3): the thin-slice formed at M5 is confirmed or broken by the first sentence she hears. The sentence is about her; the frame "this is for me" holds.
- **Friction design** (BE 10): two seconds of friction added before the order, where the category removes it. The effort signals that the person matters more than throughput, which is the brand's positioning in one gesture.
- **Framing** (BE 9): "we" frames the visit as something shared, not bought; "today" frames an ordinary day as containing a moment. The same gelato, framed as a celebration of her day.
- **Cognitive ease** (BE 1): one short question, the same words every time, no choice inside it. The flavour decision comes after, lighter for having been preceded by something easy.
- **Peak-end** (BE 7): the question opens the arc that the Note (M15) closes. It is the line the remembering self narrates to a friend ("the place where they ask you what you're celebrating").
- **The decision here**: whether to share something. The design makes "no" costless: the beat, no second try, the Note regardless (BE 10, friction removed from declining).

## RECOVERY

| If … | → |
| --- | --- |
| She shrugs or says "nothing" | Received at M9 with warmth intact: "Then let's just choose something good." (filter: pass). No second try. The Note still goes. R-M9. |
| She did not hear (headphones, noise) | Eyes first, then the line once more, same words, same volume. Never louder. |
| She answers with something painful (a loss, a diagnosis, a job gone) | Drop the celebration frame; intensity 2; "I'm sorry. Let's choose something good for today." (filter: pass). No advice, no follow-up questions, the Note still goes. If she asks for anything beyond the order, Menelaos takes over (guardrails 11). R-M9. |
| She is irritated ("just a pistachio") | "Pistachio it is." (filter: pass). No further question; the Note still goes; M22 by name if known. |
| The question was forgotten and the order has begun | Ask it before the Note is handed (M15), never with the payment: the Note needs the question to be a Note. |
| Long queue, the team member starts to skip it | The question stays; the tasting at M11 is what shortens. If this cannot hold at Ring Mall, that is Evo's decision, not the floor's. Open dependency. |

**State arc:** Seen → Asked → Answering, or declining lightly

**Success metric:** On ten sampled orders per shift, the question is asked before the first product word on all ten; the team member's hands are empty when it is asked; the guest speaks or declines before the team member speaks again.

**Target emotion:** noticed

**Brand pillar:** differentiator (core message block 5); value Notice the moment

## HIGH additions

**Brand-coherence test.** Role Peer: the line says "we"; the team member stands beside the question, not above it. Connection statement "Your day matters to us as much as it does to you, so let's celebrate it together": the question is its first half, spoken. Lover 70: attentive, asks before she tells. Innocent 30: simple words, light tone, nothing clever. Holds.

**Story block:** 4, Meeting the Guide ("Someone looks up, smiles and asks, 'What are we celebrating today?' No script, no rush.").

**Operational dependencies.** The same person meets the eyes and asks (no greeter/server split). Counter height to level eyes (current height to confirm). The Note jar visible at every station (v3 §7.2). The Ring Mall queue form (Evo). Name badges with first names (v3 §7.3) so the exchange can become two-way.

**Peak placement test (S3-05), applied here as a check:** if the visit ended at this line, would it feel complete? No. The question is a promise; the Note (M15) keeps it. The signature is correctly not the peak.
