## 4. What the map does with this

- Every weight on the map is marked **provisional, unobserved**. HIGH moments are "brand-led" in the Reasoning Note; none has an observation behind it.
- Two tests are set for the observation move so it changes weights rather than confirming them: (a) **the door**: does anyone hesitate inside the door before a face turns (M05 up, M06 unchanged), or do guests walk to the case without looking for a person (M05 down)? (b) **the mall**: is the question asked in the queue at all, and what do guests do while waiting (M07, M09, M24)?
- The review read tallies by moment code; any moment with unprompted mentions of 5 or more in 50 is re-weighed.
