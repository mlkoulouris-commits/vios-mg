## Diagnostic finding (pattern S1-05)

The skill's rule: if the owner cannot answer at length, stop and say which earlier step is thin. Here the owner has not been asked, which is a different finding: the step that is thin is the **owner interview itself** (diagnosis §3, open-facts 7). The archive's October answers were written from documents and the archive says so; they are not carried here as if spoken. The naming pass that follows is therefore a **check of an existing name**, run on the diagnosis's keyword sources (differentiator keywords, the persona's words, the core message short names), and is re-run against the real answers if they move the keyword set. That is a legitimate use of the skill for a running business with a name; it is not a substitute for the interrogation.
