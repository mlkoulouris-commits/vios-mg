Everything in Stage 1 waits on this hour: the diagnosis signature, the brand book becoming final, the persona format, the proposal. Record the answers in his words, in Bulgarian if he speaks Bulgarian; do not paraphrase.

Part 1 — The four questions (15 min)

# Question Answer
1 What are you trying to build?
2 What stage are you at, and what is the timeline? When do the next ateliers open, and where?
3 Which partners are already engaged? The identity designer's name. An architect or interior partner for the next atelier?
4 What outcome would make this engagement worth the investment?

Part 2 — The owner interview, eleven questions (45 min)

What Brand Book v3 already commits him to is in the right column, for Menelaos only. Do not read it to him; ask the question and write what he says.

# Question Answer v3 already says
1 What do you love about this business, and why? the craft; the moment a guest is noticed
2 What do you dislike or hate, and why? dessert as a transaction; "Next!"; the rushed counter
3 Where do you bring the most value to your guests? a pause in which someone notices you and what you are celebrating
4 The one thing you would change about this industry? service; the category's complaint is people, not product
5 Why is this market a great space to be in? Sofia has no gelato brand with an emotional idea; tiramisu is only now emerging
6 Social media: positives and negatives? real guests, real notes; no posed perfection, no product-only heroes
7 The purpose of your existence, aside from money? happiness is an attitude; everyday moments deserve to be noticed
8 What is important in the way you do business? fresh every day, the Italian way, no shortcuts
9 What do your guests need protecting from? being processed; empty mass-market desserts; joyless "healthy" options
10 The most pressing issues in the world today? nothing on file
11 What are you passionate about? nothing on file

Then: which of the four values (from v3) does the team already live, and which are still aspirational?

Part 3 — Five decisions (20 min)

# Decision The practice recommends Evo decides
1 Stage 1 as a full rerun that re-interrogates the October book, or a revision of Brand Book v3? full rerun, as he asked; he must agree to seeing October's conclusions questioned
2 Giulia: a disclosed AI host with face and voice, or real team on camera with Giulia as the written voice only? real team + written voice; it sits inside the value "Stay human" and needs no disclosure set
3 Editorial tagline: keep "Italian joy crafted daily." or move to "What are we celebrating today?" (lock-up "Happiness is an attitude." stays) move; "joy every day" is Doppio's territory per his own v3 §2.2
4 "Baci" on the Instagram handle and the Google listing: remove now, and which print / QR codes change with it? remove, per v3
5 Identity designer: who, by when; and does he confirm what the brand is indifferent to (Annex B §4)? name a candidate; the practice scores candidates on six criteria
