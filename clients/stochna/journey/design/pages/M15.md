# M15 · CRITICAL IMPACT · The order for the table

In-room · 2–5 min · Phase 4, the first round · Staff-guided, table-decided · Decision anchor (S3-04)

**Dominant inputs:** brand (block 2; Proper choices + Made together) · BE (the decision) · concept (menu and beer list, CONFIRM open fact 6) · non-verbal (one suggestion, step back).

## CURRENT STATE
The table has read the short menu (M13, silent) and is deciding. Some know what they want; one person usually doesn't. Before: menu arrives (M12). After: the wait for the pour (M16, silent).

## DO
1. The server comes when menus go down or someone looks up; not before.
2. Takes beer first, then plates "for the table".
3. **One decision anchor (S3-04):** one pairing suggestion that links a beer and a plate. The same anchor per menu edition, agreed at pre-shift, defended, not changed per server.
4. For anyone unsure about beer: the pour offer (below); the server chooses from the paired list.
5. Repeats the order back once, in short.

## SAY (to lock)
- Anchor: EN "The [beer] goes well with the [plate]." · BG „[Бирата] върви добре с [ястието].“ (names fill in once the menu is on the fact sheet)
- Unsure guest: EN "If you're not sure, I'll pour you something." · BG „Ако не сте сигурни, ще ви налея нещо.“
- Order back: EN "Two [beer], one [beer], bread and [plate] for the table." · BG „Две [бира], една [бира], хляб и [ястие] за масата.“

## DON'T
- No list of every beer; no tasting notes ("notes of", "mouthfeel").
- No upsell, no "would you like to add…".
- No explaining why the menu is short unless asked (then M14).
- No allergen answer from memory: R-ALLERGY.

## NON-VERBAL
Standing at the table end, visible to all (NV A.3). Eyes to whoever is speaking (NV C.2). One gesture to the menu line, not a tour of it (NV C.3). Calm pace; no pen tapping (NV E.1).

## BEHAVIORAL ECONOMICS
BE 8 choice architecture and BE 14 decision fatigue: a short menu plus one anchor. BE 3 anchoring: the first suggestion sets the frame for the table. BE 6 defaults: "I'll pour you something" makes the default a good choice, not the cheapest one. BE 1 cognitive ease.

## RECOVERY
- Guest asks for something not on the menu → M14 line, alternative if there is one (R-M15).
- Table stuck → the anchor, once more, then "I'll come back in a minute" (R-M15).
- Allergy mentioned → R-ALLERGY: the server never answers from memory; the kitchen lead or the manager answers from the allergen sheet (open: allergen policy not on fact sheet).

**State arc:** What should we order? → One suggestion → Everything here is good.
**Success metric:** the table orders in one visit, and at least one person takes the anchor or the pour offer.
**Target emotion:** confident.
**Brand pillar:** Proper choices · Made together · story block 4.

**Brand-coherence test:** Guide alongside: one suggestion, then step back ✓. **Dependencies:** menu names, pairings, prices (open fact 6); allergen sheet (fact sheet CONFIRM).
