# M14 · STANDARD · The Mindful and Aperitivo lines said plainly

**Draft — book edition v1.0 unsigned (open-facts 39); observation move not held.**

In-room · Phase 3 The question · ~5–10 sec · Both counters · markers none · weight provisional · dominant dimension: language (plain, no lecture) · inputs: concept (Mindful: dark chocolate with stevia, vanilla with honey, berries with agave; Aperitivo: tiramisu gelato, limoncello sorbet, prosecco & peach; fact sheet 2026-10-07), brand (guardrails 8, 9)

## CURRENT STATE

She asks about sugar or alcohol, or points at a Mindful or Aperitivo tub. She wants a straight answer.

## DO

1. Mindful: say what sweetens it, as the menu names it (stevia, honey, agave). Nothing about health.
2. Aperitivo: say once that it has alcohol in it.
3. Aperitivo to anyone who may be under age, or to a child via a parent: **R-AGE**; the legal age line: **CONFIRM** (open-facts 14).
4. Then back to choosing.

| Age | — | — | **CONFIRM** (14); not lockable |
| Mindful | "No added sugar. It's sweetened with [stevia / honey / agave]." | „Без добавена захар. Подсладен е със [стевия / мед / агаве]." | **CONFIRM**: not lockable (open-facts 51) |

**Claim flag (guardrails 9).** The fact sheet allows "no added sugar" for the whole Mindful line. Under EU Regulation 1924/2006 (Annex), "no added sugars" may be claimed only where no mono- or disaccharides **or any other food used for its sweetening properties** has been added; honey and agave syrup are foods used for sweetening. The claim may hold for the stevia item and not for the honey and agave items. Until the Lab and Evo confirm the lawful wording (open-facts 51), the team says only what sweetens each one: "It's sweetened with honey." / „Подсладен е с мед." The NEW Aperitivo line is logged in the Stage 3 block.

## DON'T

- No "sugar-free", "healthy", "guilt-free", "diet".
- No lecture on sugar; no joke about the alcohol.
- No Aperitivo served on a guess about age.

Metric: zero "sugar-free" or "healthy" said in a sampled hour.
