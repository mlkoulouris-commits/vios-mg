# Vios Brand

Stage 1 of the Vios Practice: brand strategy and the brand book, then the brand persona. Consulting engagements with a start, an end and the client owner's sign-off. Requires `vios-core`.

1. **vios-brand-diagnosis** — the entry gate: the real business, its meaning, audience persona, competitor audit, differentiator, archetype and Brand Role, positioning. Worksheets, the twelve archetypes and the competitor-audit template are in `skills/vios-brand-diagnosis/references/`.
2. **vios-persona-builder** — the Persona Bible (EN/BG side by side): character, voice spec, behaviour, expression palette, tone by guest mood, KPIs, AI disclosure, error protocol, pilot, versioning.

Outputs land in the client folder (`vios-core:vios-guardrails` › client file convention): `diagnosis.md`, `persona-bible.md`, `brand-book.md` (visual and verbal direction, core messages, story framework, journey, micro-message blocks), `character-sheet/`.

Next stage: `vios-journey` (journey map and design). Daily use: `vios-marketing`.
