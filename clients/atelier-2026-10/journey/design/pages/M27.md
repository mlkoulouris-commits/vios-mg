# M27 · STANDARD · The letter

Digital touchpoint · The card on the desk (phase 7) · weeks–months · Self-led · owner: marketing · dominant dimensions: language, tone

## CURRENT STATE

She gave an address freely; a letter arrives. "They remembered."

## DO

1. The set per v3 §7.5: a three-letter welcome series, a monthly letter, a quiet birthday note, a first-visit anniversary. 150–200 words, letter-like, in Вие (Bible front page), signed by the atelier, not by Giulia in anyone's name.
2. The capture route (**to confirm**): there is no sign-up mechanism on file. Candidates for Menelaos: a card by the till, a line on the receipt, the website. Never a pop-up that gates content; never pre-ticked (guardrails 4); GDPR basis documented (guardrails 12).
3. Each letter plays one story block (most often 7) and anchors one core message; the monthly letter carries one real guest moment with permission, one creation with its true story, the question.
4. The birthday and anniversary notes ask for nothing: no code, no offer. "It's been a while. We'd love to hear what you're celebrating." is the register (v3 §5.4).
5. Unsubscribe in one click, no guilt line.

## DON'T

- "Don't miss", "exclusive offer", "only this week"; a newsletter voice; a birthday discount; sending without a documented basis.

**Success metric:** Capture route live with a documented basis; letters sent on the set cadence; unsubscribe one click. Filter: the register line, **pass**.
