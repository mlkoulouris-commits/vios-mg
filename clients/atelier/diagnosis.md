## 4. Meaning check: purpose, vision, mission, values

Taken from Brand Book v3 as the founder's prior statement; each tested against the worksheet rule, with the practice's finding.

- **Purpose (W1).** "To give everyone who believes everyday moments matter a pause to notice and celebrate one, crafted with care, every day." Commitment: to anyone who believes their everyday moments matter. What: a pause. How: Italian craft and personal attention. Resources and frequency: gelato and tiramisu, a team trained to notice people, every guest every day. **Finding: passes.** It names a commitment, an action and a frequency, and it is already performed (the question and the Note exist, printed, in the jar). The test it has not yet passed is the Ring Mall counter, where the archive records no evidence the ritual runs (contradiction 1).
- **Vision (W2).** "To be the name people think of when an everyday moment deserves celebrating, found in our own ateliers and at the tables of partners we choose." **Finding: believable and 5–10 years; slightly under-scary.** "The name people think of" is a position, not a transformation; the practice will ask in Stage 1 whether Evo's own version is bigger. It carries the B2B line as a first-class part of the brand, which the business's numbers (53 % of Lab sales external) justify.
- **Mission (W3).** "Every day, we craft fresh gelato and tiramisu the Italian way, with no shortcuts, and make sure each one reaches people with its story, so their moment is noticed and celebrated." Standing commitments: fresh daily in small batches; authentic Italian technique; every guest greeted personally; nothing leaves without its story; partners only if they serve that story. **Finding: passes.** Present tense, action-based, and each commitment is checkable on the floor.
- **Values (W4).** Choose happiness (never fake it) · Bring the attitude (hire for attitude, train skill) · Notice the moment (ask what you're celebrating) · Stay human (eye contact, names, no scripts; no kiosks). **Finding: each carries a behaviour; passes the form test.** Which are lived and which are aspirational is unknown (open-facts 8): "Stay human" has a hard case the business has already decided against it once (the AI ambassador for content, contradiction 2), so it is the value most in need of the founder's word.

Gate: purpose is neither empty nor performed. Proceed.

## 5. Audience persona and archetype (W5)

Built from the brand book's persona, the Instagram baseline and the review count; **not yet from review mining or 10–20 competitor profiles** (open-facts 16, 17), so the persona is the founder's prior, to be checked rather than assumed.

**Elena**, 34, creative director, lives and works in central Sofia, personal income in the €2.5–3.2k/month band, partnered, no children. Walks home along Rakovski with her day still on her shoulders. Psychographics: specialty coffee, design and interiors, Instagram as a visual diary not a feed to perform on; clothes from COS-type labels; values quality over quantity and being treated as a person. **Pain:** mass desserts feel empty; "healthy" feels joyless; cafés rush her; her small wins pass unmarked. **Need:** to feel her everyday moments matter. **Before:** busy, a little drained. **After:** calm, seen, lighter. **Quotes (from the book, to be replaced by real ones from reviews and guest conversations):** "I'd rather have one great thing than three average ones." · "If they remember my name, I'll come back."

**Audience archetype: Creator 70 / Lover 30.** Drive: originality, self-expression, real quality. Fear: the mediocre, the duplicated, being processed as one of many. Situational desire at the counter: to be noticed as herself and to have something real in her hands in under a minute.

What the Instagram baseline adds: the two best-performing pieces of Aug–Oct 2026 were real moments with people and places (a gelato cart at an event, reach 1,491, 19 shares; a reel with 79 shares), not product shots (16–25 interactions). The audience already votes for the persona the book describes; the account was not giving it to them.

These five gaps are exactly where the purpose already stands, which is the sign the positioning is credible rather than wished.

## 7. Differentiator and positioning (W7–W8)

**Experience keywords:** noticed · celebrated · unhurried · fresh today · true story · together.

**Differentiator (the one thing):** every order opens with "What are we celebrating today?" and closes with an Atelier Note drawn by chance. It adds value because it turns a 60-second transaction into a moment the guest can carry out of the door, and it costs no theatre, only people, so it travels to a mall counter and a partner's table. **Onliness:** the only gelato and tiramisu atelier where every order becomes a small celebration of your day.

**Positioning (one sentence):** We help people in Sofia who believe their everyday moments matter but feel processed and rushed wherever they go, to experience a short pause in which their moment is noticed and celebrated, unlike gelato shops that sell product and tiramisu bars that sell novelty; our solution is fresh Italian gelato and tiramisu handed over with a question and a note.

Reads as one claim; `vios-messages-and-story` can take it.

## 8. Brand archetype and Brand Role (W10)

1. **Audience archetype:** Creator 70 / Lover 30; drive: real quality and self-expression; fear: the mediocre, the fake, being processed.
2. **What she wants in her situation:** "You see me, and you celebrate with me." Not advice, not inspiration, not protection: company for a minute.
3. **Brand archetype: Lover 70 / Innocent 30.** Lover serves the want directly (closeness, affection, red-carpet treatment for an ordinary day; fear of invisibility is the mirror of her fear of being processed). Innocent supplies the lightness and honesty (simple, optimistic, no deceit) that keeps the Lover from becoming heavy or sensual, which a gelato counter cannot carry. **Guardrail: Innocent stays light, never cute.** Rejected pairs: Caregiver (makes the guest a patient; the brand is beside her, not above), Jester (party energy breaks the pause), Creator as primary (would make the brand the hero of its own craft; the guest is the hero).
4. **Brand Role: Peer.** Role statement: "Your day matters to us as much as it does to you, so let's celebrate it together." The ritual only works side by side ("what are *we* celebrating"); Guide or Inspiration would put the brand above the counter.

Expression: a warm peer in her early thirties, linen in cream, sage and caramel, a notebook of guests' names; uses "we" and "today"; never "Next!", never "our bestseller". Whether this expression is carried by real team members only, or also by a disclosed AI figure on screen, is contradiction 2 and a founder decision.

## 9. Contradictions found and what must change upstream

1. **The ritual and the queue.** The value "Notice the moment" names a long queue as its hard case; Ring Mall is ~34 % of kilos and nothing on file says whether the question is asked there. If it is not, the mall counter is a second brand expression and needs its own map and pages; if it is shortened, the queue learns it is optional. Founder's answer required before Stage 2 (open-facts 9).
2. **"Stay human" vs the AI ambassador.** On 2026-10-30 the partner decided that Giulia becomes a disclosed AI host for content; Brand Book v3 says "never automated or faceless". The archive records that Evo was to be informed, not that he agreed. A value the founder signed cannot be footnoted by a partner's decision; it is re-opened here as a founder decision (open-facts 26). The practice's position: either answer is workable under guardrails 6, but the brand book must say which, in the values section, not in a Bible footnote.
3. **"Baci" is still the public name.** The book removed it; the Instagram handle and the Google listing still carry it, and so do the QR codes and print that point at them. Discovery and profile check are built on the wrong name until decided (open-facts 23, 24).
4. **Claims in the current feed the fact sheet cannot back.** "#glutenfree" on most posts without the Lab's written line; "special promotional price" and "15 % off for students" without a reference price; exclamation marks and emoji strings against the book's own voice rule. None is a brand problem; all are guardrails 1, 3 and 9 problems that the marketing loop fixes on day one, and they say the account and the book have never met.
5. **"Joy" in the editorial tagline.** The book names "joy every day" as Doppio's and says joy alone is not ownable; the editorial line adopted 2026-11-01 is "Italian joy crafted daily." Raised, not decided (open-facts 30); `vios-name-and-tagline` judges it.
6. **The diagnosis has no ledger.** Every number above is a brand-book number or an eighteen-month Lab total; the shop's own daily rhythm is unknown. The POS pull (open-facts 15) and the review read (16) come before the brand book, or Stage 1 designs for a persona the till has never seen.
7. **The founder's voice is absent.** Every statement attributed to Evo in this file is from a document. Structural, not cosmetic: a brand book re-run on derived answers is a revision pass with a new cover.
