# M01 · HIGH IMPACT · A real guest's moment seen online

**Draft — book edition v1.0 unsigned (open-facts 39); observation move not held.**

Digital touchpoint · Phase 1 The walk home · days–weeks · Both counters · Self-led · markers none · weight provisional

Dominant inputs: **brand** (messages 1, 5, 9; pillar Guest moments 35 %) · **BE** (social proof of real people, not numbers) · **concept** (Instagram primary; persona format open-facts 26; Metricool baseline 2026-10-30). NV not used (digital), except the faces in frame (Bible §9).

## CURRENT STATE

She scrolls, tired, suspicious of cute. She sees one real person's real day and is asked the same question back. Baseline: 0 of 24 posts Aug–Oct built on a guest moment (n=24); the two best pieces were real people.

## DO

1. **Marketing**: one real guest moment per piece, with written permission on file before it is drafted (guardrails 2, 7; `consents/`).
2. The real room and the real food in frame (guardrails 5); no product-only hero.
3. One question back to the reader at most; no exclamation marks, no emoji (Bible front page).
4. 30–50 words; BG first or alone, EN second, never machine-translated.
5. QA gate before scheduling (`vios-consistency-qa`), Menelaos signs (guardrails 10).

## SAY

Caption voice, not locked. Pattern (batch A line 7, a sample, not a real guest): "[Name] [her moment, one fact]. Today was [what she chose]. What's yours?" / „[Име] [моментът ѝ]. Днес беше [какво избра]. А твоят ден?" Filter test on every caption.

## DON'T

- No staged or composited moment; no stock smile; no AI food or room (guardrails 5).
- No hype, "best", "artisanal"; no offer.
- No face or voice of Giulia before open-facts 26 resolves.
- No guest post reused without permission.

## BEHAVIORAL ECONOMICS

BE 11 social proof, in the brand's form: one real person, not a count of followers. BE 4 availability: a real moment is easy to imagine as hers.

## RECOVERY

A post published wrong → Menelaos pulls it within 2 hours; correction signed by a named person; logged (Bible §14).
