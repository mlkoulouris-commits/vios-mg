## Decisions the pages take that Menelaos must confirm before training

1. The team member draws the Note, not the guest (M15).
2. The Note is handed before payment, with the order (M15).
3. "Small wins count." is used as a farewell only after the guest shared something (M22).
4. At peak with one person on the counter, the end-2 attention degrades to eyes only; this is named, not hidden (M23).
5. A first-timer gets no greeting line at the threshold; the look is the acknowledgement and the question is the first sentence (M05). "Good to see you again." is locked for known guests only.
6. The tasting anchor on indecision is one flavour tied to the guest's answer or to today's batch, named at pre-shift; never a bestseller (M11).
7. "Thanks for waiting." is said only past a five-minute wait, as a fact; no "sorry for the wait" (M05).
8. The Atelier Note carries no call to action: no hashtag, no QR, no offer; the name and descriptor small on the back only (M24). The 25 printed cards are run through the filter test as a batch before the next print.
9. No loyalty mechanism (stamps, points, "fifth visit"); the return is the guest's decision (M28).
10. A venue that wants the product without the story cards is declined (M29); the cards clause is in every agreement.
11. Aperitivo gelato: named as alcoholic in one line before the spoon; no taste or sale to anyone who looks under 18 until the legal age and ID practice are confirmed (M13). The age itself is a legal fact to confirm, not a brand choice.
12. The review ask, if used at all, is one unrewarded line by the till or on the receipt (M26); the second line of M12 (the tiramisu collection) is stated as what is there, never asked as a question.
13. The email capture route does not exist; Menelaos chooses one (card by the till, receipt line, website) with a documented GDPR basis before any letter is sent (M27).
