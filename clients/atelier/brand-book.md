## Filter test (Part 5; derived 2026-11-01 by `vios-filter-test` from v3 §2.4, §3.2–3.3, §5.4, §8.1 and the Bible's never-list)

**Is this processing the guest?** — does it hurry her, sell to her, or talk past her?

Pass when the answer is no.

**Trait and why.** The category's default is the transaction: the guest is an order, served well and fast and forgotten. Atelier refuses it. The positioning says "unlike gelaterias that serve you transactionally"; the persona's fear is "being processed"; the owner hates "dessert as a transaction, 'Next!', the rushed counter"; the Role is Peer, beside the person. One trait, one refusal: the guest is never an order. The three markers make two people answer the same way. *Hurry*: speed words, "Next", "Anything else?", a choice pushed, a goodbye cut short. *Sell*: bestseller, upsell, "treat yourself", a price pushed, a "last chance". *Talk past*: a line for the crowd rather than this person — hype, self-praise, exclamation energy, a form-letter welcome, a slogan where a sentence to her belongs.

**Passes.**
- "Pistachio again, or something new today?" — to this guest, from memory; no push.
- "Made this morning." — a plain fact given to someone choosing; nothing sold, nothing hyped.

**Fails.**
- "That's our bestseller." — sells, and says nothing about her.
- "We're SO excited to celebrate YOU today!!!" — talks past her: performed warmth for a crowd, even though it says "you".

**Scope.** The test applies to any line a guest, a partner or a team member will read or hear: scripts, captions, replies, Notes, story cards, emails, signage text, the operations brief's examples. The tagline and the name are brand assets judged by the brand book, not by the test. Quality is judged separately; a line can pass and still be weak.
