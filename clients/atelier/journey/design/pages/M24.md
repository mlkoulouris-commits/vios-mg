# M24 · HIGH IMPACT · The card at her desk

Object · The card on the desk (phase 7) · days–weeks · Self-led · owner: floor (the card in the jar), marketing (the card set)

Dominant inputs: **brand** (the Note as signature asset) and **BE** (endowment, the peak's long tail); concept (print); non-verbal through the object only.

## CURRENT STATE

At home or at work, the card where she sees it. "That was written for me."

## DO

1. The card does the work alone: 10–15 words, handwriting-style face, brand voice, one of the 25 approved cards; themes joy, celebration, attitude, gratitude, connection, seasonal (v3 §5.4).
2. Stock that survives a desk (NV D.1); the words on the front; the name and descriptor small on the back (print set **to check**).
3. No call to action on the card: no hashtag, no QR, no offer. A note with a CTA is an advertisement; the brand book makes the Note the signature asset, not a flyer. **Decision for Menelaos** (schedule, item 8).
4. The set refreshed by season with Menelaos's approval; old cards finish in the jar; every card in a new set filter-tested before print (batches file).
5. Nothing is sent to her afterwards. The brand is silent until she acts (M25) or returns (M28).

## SAY

The card text, from the approved set only. The 25 current cards have not yet been run through the filter test as a batch: **to do** before the next print.

## DON'T

- "Follow us", "Show this card for 10 % off", a QR to a promotion.
- A quotation from a famous person; "Have a great day!"; anything generic (v3 §5.4 "never generic quotes, exclamation marks, or invented stories").
- A card that reads as an ad.

## BEHAVIORAL ECONOMICS

Endowment (BE 12): hers since M15. Peak-end (BE 7): the card re-triggers the peak every time it is seen. Framing (BE 9): the card says "today"; re-read, it names a new today.

## RECOVERY

| If … | → |
| --- | --- |
| A card is wrong (misprint, a claim that fails the truth check) | Pulled from all jars the same day. |
| A guest objects to a card's text | Received; Menelaos informed; logged for the next set. |

**State arc:** Kept → Seen again → Remembered

**Success metric:** Google reviews that mention the note (Bible KPI, raw count); the card set filter-tested before each print.

**Target emotion:** kept

**Brand pillar:** differentiator; story block 7 (The New World: "The card sits on her desk")
