# M17 · STANDARD · Packed to go

In-room · Marking it (phase 4) · ~15 sec · Counter · owner: floor · dominant dimension: accessories

## CURRENT STATE

She is taking it with her. "It's coming with me."

## DO

1. Kraft cup with lid, or the clear-top box, into a kraft carry bag with cotton handles (v3 §7.4); a spoon in the bag.
2. The Note is already in her hand (M15); if she would rather it in the bag, it goes in on top, visible when the bag opens. For a box bought to give away, the Note inside the box with the line from M15.
3. The bag handed across the counter at her hand height, not set on the floor side.
4. Nothing glossy, no plastic, no synthetic ribbon (v3 §7.4).
5. The farewell (M22) follows as she turns, a beat after the bag.

## DON'T

- The Note dropped in the bag unspoken; a promotional insert in the bag; a sticker with a hashtag.

**Success metric:** On sampled to-go orders, the Note visible on top or already in her hand; nothing in the bag but the order, the spoon and the Note. Filter: not applicable.
