## Open slots, from the floor

**Three briefs the team can shoot on a phone when the moment happens.** Send to Vios the same day; we caption and post within 24 hours.

1. **A guest stops in front of a work.** Film from behind, 5 seconds, no face. Caption comes from us.
2. **The reading on 28 Oct.** Three frames: the room filling, the reader, the bar after. Faces only with consent; otherwise backs and hands.
3. **Something unplanned and true.** Rain on the window at Golden Hour, a regular's usual order, the artist visiting. One clip, 10 seconds, nothing staged.

## Scheduling and QA

**Nothing schedules until the approver is named.** Then every post goes: draft (vios-persona-voice) → QA (vios-consistency-qa) → approver signs → scheduled through Metricool.

- Posting times: no Metricool history yet, so month one runs on the venue's decision moments: Monday posts at 12:00 (people planning the week), Thursday posts at 17:00 (people choosing the weekend). Treat as a hypothesis; Metricool's best-time data replaces it from December.
- No AI persona posts this month, so no platform AI label needed. The first persona clip waits for the character sheet and the pilot.
- Desi's art lines are quoted verbatim or not at all.
