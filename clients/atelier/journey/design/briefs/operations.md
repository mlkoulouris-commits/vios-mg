**Operating principle: position assigns the role in the moment.** Whoever faces the guest is the Counter for her; the ritual stays with one face from the question to the goodbye (choreography § 1.1). Whoever is free is the Second.

## 4. Service-flow assumptions the design makes

> **A1 — One face per guest.** The Counter who asks the question hands the card and says the goodbye. *Reason:* the ritual reads as a person, not a process. *Protects:* M09, M18, M25.

> **A2 — Pick to hand-over under 60 seconds.** *Reason:* the persona wants something real in her hands in under a minute; tasting and choosing must fit inside it. *Protects:* M13, M17.

> **A3 — The card before the till, every order.** The till waits a beat. *Reason:* the till cannot end the peak. *Protects:* M18, M19.

> **A4 — Two on at the assumed peak bands.** *Reason:* the end pair (M25 + M26) and the next threshold (M06) collide on one face; with two on, both hold. *Protects:* M06, M25, M26. **To replace with data (15, 47).**

> **A5 — Eyes-down work outside open hours or by the Second.** Prep, stock counts, deliveries, phone. *Reason:* the 3-second look-up needs a face toward the door. *Protects:* M06, M07.

> **A6 — The beat costs about two seconds per order.** *Reason:* the question needs silence to be a question. *Protects:* M10. At an assumed peak of a guest every minute, this is under 4 % of the time; checked against the POS bands when they arrive (15).

> **A7 — Card stock never runs out in a day.** A reserve at each counter, reorder before the reserve is touched. *Reason:* the peak object cannot be missing. *Protects:* M18. Stock and reorder rule: **CONFIRM** (53).

## 6. Training assumptions

- **Locked scripts every team member learns, word for word, BG and EN:** the question and its registers (M09); the three answer lines (M11); the card line (M18); the goodbye (M25); "Thanks for waiting." (M07); the known-guest line (M06); the regular line and the four origin lines (M12); "Want to taste two together?" (M13, BG form pending 52); the Aperitivo line (M14); the two allergen lines (M16); the chair line (M21); the recovery lines in `../recovery.md`.
- **Knowledge:** the eight classics and their origins (fact sheet only); the Mindful and Aperitivo lines and what may and may not be said (M14; open-facts 51); the allergen rule (no matrix, no claim); escalation topics and who to get.
- **Drills:** the 3-second look-up; the beat (counted); the blind draw and the edge hand-over; the end composition with two and with one (choreography § 1.2); the over-reaction cap.
- **Pre-shift briefing (5 minutes):** the question said aloud once by each team member; the jar and reserve checked; case gaps; who is Counter and who is Second at each band; any guest owed a message. Whether a briefing exists today: **CONFIRM** (60).
- **Shift handover note:** jar stock, open "I'll check" items, guests promised a call (choreography H8).
- Built in full by `vios-journey:vios-training-programme` (Stage 4).
