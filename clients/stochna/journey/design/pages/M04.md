# M04 · HIGH IMPACT · Booking the table

Digital · 1–3 min · Phase 1, deciding · Self-led · Owner: marketing (Memento)

**Dominant inputs:** brand (Table is the centre) · BE (no dark patterns, guardrails 4) · concept (`book.stochna.com`, Memento, BG/EN) · persona (silent; brand voice, unsigned).

## CURRENT STATE
They've decided. Booking for a group. Next: the reminder (M05).

## DO
1. Booking asks only date, time, people, name, contact.
2. Confirmation restates the plan: day, time, number of people, the address, how to change.
3. Formal register (Вие); unsigned; no ambassador.
4. Groups of 8+ go to the events contact (open fact 14).

## SAY (confirmation)
EN "Thursday, 20:00, six people. We're at [address], 2 km from the centre. To change it: [link]." · BG „Четвъртък, 20:00, шест души. Намираме се на [адрес], на 2 км от центъра. За промяна: [линк].“

## DON'T
- No pre-ticked newsletter box; no countdown; no "only 2 tables left" (guardrails 1, 4).
- No upsell at booking.

## BEHAVIORAL ECONOMICS
BE 10 friction design: remove friction from booking; restating the plan lowers anxiety (BE 1).

## RECOVERY
- Slot full → next free time shown plainly (R-FULL wording).
- Booking error → a named person writes the same day.

**State arc:** Let's go → The plan restated → Done. Thursday, six of us.
**Success metric:** booking completion rate (n shown); share by `?src=`.
**Target emotion:** settled. **Brand pillar:** Table is the centre.
