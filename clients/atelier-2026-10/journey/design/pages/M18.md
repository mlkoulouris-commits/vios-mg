# M18 · SILENT · Reading the card

In-room · The ten minutes (phase 5) · ~10 sec · Counter edge, the table, or the pavement · ○

## CURRENT STATE

The card is in her hand (M15). She reads it. The surprise lands, in private.

## DO

Nothing. M15 did the work: the blind draw, the line, the gaze to her hands and then her eyes, the release. The team member's eyes are already off her by the time she looks down: to the till (M16), the bag (M17), or the next guest's eyes-up (M5). Face stays resting warm; nobody watches her read.

Where the team member is while not acting: at the station, attention deliberately elsewhere, within reach if she speaks.

## DON'T

What would break the silence:

- "Which one did you get?", "Oh, that's a good one", "Read it out".
- Watching her face for a reaction; a colleague leaning in.
- Explaining the card ("they're all different", "we write them ourselves").
- Handing the receipt into the same moment.
- Asking for a photo, a tag, a post.

**Why silent (Reasoning Note §9):** pattern S2-03, the silence after the peak. A witness turns the surprise into a performance she must react to. The card reads as written for her only if nobody is checking that it did.

**Success metric:** On sampled orders, no word is said to the guest between the Note line and the price; the team member's eyes are elsewhere when she reads.
