# M07 · HIGH IMPACT · The queue acknowledged (RM)

**Draft — book edition v1.0 unsigned (open-facts 39); observation move not held.**

In-room · Phase 2 The threshold · 1–10 min waiting, ~2 sec acknowledged · Ring Mall (also Rakovski when a line forms) · markers none · weight provisional; candidate HIGH if the observation shows the question dropped (Reasoning Note § 5) · written for "the same ritual, in a queue" (handoff B1; open-facts 9)

Dominant inputs: **brand** (value 11's hard case; Bible hard moment "the Ring Mall queue") · **non-verbal** (eyes, the design of waits) · **concept** (one or two on the counter, the mall flow; format and queue behaviour: open-facts 11).

## CURRENT STATE

She has joined a line in the mall, with bags or children. She counts the people ahead and decides whether it is worth it. She expects to be processed fast or not at all.

## DO

1. **Counter**: each person who joins the line gets a look and a small nod within a few seconds, between orders, never over a guest's answer (M06 busy rule).
2. The line faces the case: she reads it while waiting (M08 happens here).
3. At her turn: the queue register, then the question (M09), whole.
4. If a second team member is free: the second looks up and nods to the line; never takes orders from the back of the line.
5. No one is asked to decide in advance.

## SAY

**LOCKED** (Bible hard moments; not new):

| | EN | BG |
| --- | --- | --- |
| At her turn | "Thanks for waiting. What are we celebrating today?" | „Благодаря, че изчака. Какво празнуваме днес?" |

Filter test: **pass** (batch boundary case: a fact, no apology, no hurry).

## DON'T

- No "sorry for the wait, we're slammed".
- No "have your choice ready", "who's next?", or calling orders down the line.
- No shortening the question for the line.
- No speeding up the guest at the counter to clear the line.

## NON-VERBAL

Eyes to each new arrival, briefly (NV C.2); the wait made visible and occupied by the case (NV E.2); voice for the guest at the counter, not raised for the line (NV B.3).

## BEHAVIORAL ECONOMICS

BE 10 friction design: the wait is the one friction kept, and it is not hidden; acknowledging it lowers its felt cost. NV E.2: occupied time (reading the case) feels shorter than empty time.

## RECOVERY

If the line grows past what the counter can hold with the ritual intact → **R-QUEUE** (and **R-ALONE** when one is on).
