# Floor audit — the sampled hour

**Draft — book edition v1.0 unsigned (open-facts 39); observation move not held.**

Assessment instrument for the assessment loop. Operational register; behaviours in quotation marks. One sampled hour per counter per week (`cadence-calendar.md`), from the ops brief § 7 standards. Run by Menelaos or a signed-off team member who is not serving. Diagnostic, not punitive: results feed the pre-shift rotation and weekly coaching, never blame.

## Method

- Pick a band (rotate: late morning, afternoon, golden hour, closing hour at Rakovski; weekday and weekend afternoon at Ring Mall).
- Watch from where guests do not feel watched: the end of the counter, the till side, a table at Rakovski. Observation is watching, not asking guests.
- Count every order in the hour. Tick each behaviour per order: seen / missed / not applicable.
- No names on the sheet in front of the team. Names go only to the one-to-one coaching log.

Plus, daily: room check passed (x of n checks); cards left behind at close (count).

## Reporting

**Show x of n.** With fewer than 30 orders in the hour (the usual case at a counter), report **raw numbers only**, headed "too small to call" (guardrails shared numbers). Trends are read over four weeks per counter, never from one hour.

## Bands and actions

Per standard 1–7, over the last four sampled hours at that counter:

| Band | Reading | Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Keep** | seen in every order observed, or missed once in all four hours | standing slot drops one turn in the pre-shift rotation |
| **Watch** | missed in two or three orders across the four hours | named in the weekly team review as "one thing to keep an eye on" |
| **Fix** | missed in four or more orders across the four hours, or any miss on standards 2 or 5 in two consecutive hours | the next two pre-shifts take this moment as the standing slot; the weekly coaching topic for whoever was on; re-audited next week |

Standards 8–10 (counts): any count above zero is reviewed. Any claim miss (10) is corrected at the next pre-shift as a fact. An unverified allergen answer is an incident for Menelaos the same day.

An audit hour with one person alone at a peak band records "R-ALONE" beside standard 7: the dropped watch is the designed degradation, a staffing fact, not a miss (ops brief § 2).

## Where results go

Weekly team review (20 min, ops brief § 8): the four-week band per standard per counter, one thing to keep, one to fix. Monthly design review with Evo: the bands, the recoveries logged, any page that does not survive the floor (it goes back to Stage 3 as a revision, versioned on the page).
