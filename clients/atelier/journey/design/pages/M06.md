# M06 · CRITICAL IMPACT · Someone looks up

**Draft — book edition v1.0 unsigned (open-facts 39); observation move not held.**

In-room · Phase 2 The threshold · ~3–10 sec · Both counters · markers none · weight provisional, brand-led (observation test set: does she hesitate inside the door before a face turns, `observation.md` § 4a)

Dominant inputs: **brand** (message 2 Not an order, value 12 Stay human, story block 4) · **non-verbal** (eye behaviour, posture, timing, the room behind the face). Concept consulted: one or two on the counter, the case between them, the queue at the mall. BE consulted: thin-slicing, affect.

## CURRENT STATE

She has stepped in from Rakovski (M05) or joined the line at Ring Mall (M07), braced for "Next!". She has not ordered anything. She is looking for a face. What she decides in these seconds, person or process, colours every moment after it.

## DO

1. **Counter**, whoever faces the door or the line: within **3 seconds** of her stepping in (Rakovski) or reaching the line (Ring Mall), eyes up to her face. This is the locked behaviour; it comes before any word.
2. Smile before words: quick, genuine, intensity 3 of 5.
3. **Free:** if the counter is free, a plain hello in her language within the next breath. **Busy** with another guest: eyes and a small nod only; no words over the guest being served; the words come when her turn comes.
4. **Known guest:** the locked line, with her name (SAY). Only if sure of the name.
5. Hands: off the till and the screen while looking up; if scooping for someone else, keep scooping, lift only the eyes.
6. Then stand back from the case. She reads it (M08) without help.
7. **Room (Rakovski):** the counter is the first thing that moves in the room; the case is lit, the jar on the counter, sound low enough to talk over (M05). **Ring Mall:** the line can see the counter's face, not its back (open-facts 11).

## SAY

Unknown guest: **not locked** (handoff D latitude). A plain hello in her language: "Hi." / „Здравей." / „Добър ден." Nothing more.

Known guest, **LOCKED**, **NEW** (Stage 3; built from Bible §5 "Good to see you"):

| | EN | BG |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Known guest | "[Name]. Good to see you." | „[Име]. Радвам се да те видя." |

Filter test: hurry no · sell no · no one in particular no → **pass**. Logged in `archive/filter-test-batches.md`, Stage 3 block.

## DON'T

- Don't speak before you look.
- Don't greet from the till, the screen or the scoop.
- Don't use a welcome script ("Welcome to Atelier del Gelato!"), "Next", or "What can I get you?".
- Don't name a flavour, the offer of the day, or the card.
- Don't call across the room to a guest still at the door.
- Don't guess a name.

## NON-VERBAL

Eyes: up to her face within 3 seconds, held for the hello (NV C.2). Posture: upright, open, shoulders toward the door or line (NV C.1, C.4). Face: smile reaches the eyes (Bible §9 welcome row). Voice: warm, normal speaking volume, not projected across the room (NV B.1, B.3). Timing: look, then smile, then words; the look never waits for her to speak first (NV E.3). Distance: the counter stays between; no stepping out to greet (NV A.1). Room: sound, light, scent at standard (M05; NV F.1–F.3).

## BEHAVIORAL ECONOMICS

- **BE 17 thin-slicing** (contested tier; used as a design heuristic): the first seconds anchor the visit; the face is what this brand puts in them, not a sign.
- **BE 5 affect**: a felt "someone saw me" carries into how the case, the price and the wait are judged.
- **BE 1 cognitive ease**: no question yet, no choice yet. Nothing asked of her.

## RECOVERY

- If nobody looked up within the standard and she is hovering or reading the room → the nearest team member looks up now, smile, hello, no apology (**R-M06**).
- If both team members are busy at the back → the first back turns and looks; "One minute." is allowed (**R-M06**, **R-ALONE**).
- At Ring Mall with a line → **R-QUEUE**.
- At closing time → **R-CLOSE**.
