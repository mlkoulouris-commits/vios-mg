# Guest interviews — <Client>

Eight to ten conversations of about fifteen minutes with guests of the venue, held on the floor or by phone by the account manager or the owner with this guide. Input to `vios-brand:vios-brand-diagnosis` step 4 (Guest evidence). The five questions are fixed so the answers can be counted; follow-ups are free. The limits of asking (section 3.1) apply: guests report their remembering self; what they say they would do is not evidence, what they say they remember is.

**Consent and data.** Each guest is told who is asking and why, that nothing is published, and agrees before the first question; the consent line (date, first name or initials, channel) and any recording live in Drive › consents, never in git. This file holds the anonymised summary only: no names, no contact details, no recording links.

## The five questions (ask in the guest's language)

| # | EN | BG |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Why did you come here today, and why here rather than somewhere else? | Защо дойдохте тук днес, и защо тук, а не другаде? |
| 2 | What is the one thing you will remember from this visit? | Кое е единственото нещо, което ще запомните от това посещение? |
| 3 | Was there a moment that felt awkward, slow or unclear? Where? | Имаше ли момент, който беше неловък, бавен или неясен? Къде? |
| 4 | How would you describe this place to a friend in one sentence? | Как бихте описали това място на приятел с едно изречение? |
| 5 | What would make you come back sooner, or bring someone? | Какво би ви накарало да се върнете по-скоро, или да доведете някого? |

Closing: first visit or regular · occasion · party size · how they found the venue. Nothing else is asked.

## Reading (five lines at most)

What the guests agree on, where they contradict the owner's picture (step 2 of the diagnosis), what is a candidate HIGH moment, what is a candidate core message. Counts beside every claim; n < 8 is reported as raw answers, "too few to call".
