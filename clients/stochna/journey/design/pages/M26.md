# M26 · STANDARD · Room sound and light (standing)

Environmental · whole visit · Phase 3–6 · Room-led · Standing condition (sensory frame; operations brief § 5)

## CURRENT STATE
The condition for the evening and the peak: the table can hear itself.

## DO
1. Sound held at a level where a table of six talks without raising voices (target set at observation; music policy open fact 11).
2. Light warm and steady; no change of mood at a set hour.
3. One person per shift owns the level.

## DON'T
- No DJ, no live music competing with conversation, no screens (book value 3).
- No turning the volume up as the room fills.

**Metric:** at observation, no table leans in to hear each other. **Pillar:** Table is the centre.
