# Training architecture — Atelier del Gelato (Stage 4)

**Draft — book edition v1.0 unsigned (open-facts 39); observation move not held.**

Date 2026-11-01. `vios-journey:vios-training-programme`, Moves 1, 3 and 7. Operational register. Approved by Evo and Menelaos before any material is finalised (open-facts 67); until then every file in `training/` is a draft on a draft design set.

## 1. What this programme is

A **running venue with a small team**: two counters (Rakovski 149, Ring Mall) and the Lab, walk-in, no bookings. No opening date; the design goes live on a date still open (open-facts 61). The house form for a running venue applies: one **Journey Training Plan** taking one trainee at a time through the whole journey, the **floor playbook** and the **floor-ready sign-off checklist** as the minimum set, with the rest of the standard architecture added only where the team will use it. Every omission is recorded in § 4.

**Who it trains**

| Who | What they receive | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Every counter team member, at both counters, current team and every joiner | the five-session plan, three shadow shifts, sign-off; the role card, the playbook, the wall chart | ops brief § 1 (Counter, Second: one positional role) |
| The Lead (Menelaos, or a shift lead he names) | the management stack; trains and signs off | ops brief § 1; trainer to confirm (open-facts 62) |
| The Lab team | the Lab card (the counter's "I'll check" answers, deliveries, card stock) | ops brief § 1; names and route (open-facts 60, 69) |
| Evo (founder) | the full stack, in the founder handoff | Stage 4 § 2.4.6 |

No team member is named in this programme: the roster is not on file (open-facts 60). Roles are positional (ops brief § 1): whoever faces the guest is the **Counter** for her; whoever is free is the **Second**. One workbook and one role card serve both, because the role is the position, not the person.

## 5. Cadence chosen

**Onboarding cadence (running venue)**, not the pre-opening sequence: the shop and the counter are open, the team exists, and there is no opening date. The plan runs in the winter low (Stage 4 window 2027-01-04 → 01-15 proposed in the intake timeline; go-live open, 61) so the team is trained before the spring. Detail: `cadence-calendar.md`.

**Length:** five sessions, about **5¼ hours** in all (60 · 90 · 45 · 60 · 60 minutes), held over one to two weeks, then **three shadow shifts** (Rakovski golden hour, Rakovski closing hour, Ring Mall weekend afternoon), then sign-off. Session lengths are the practice's proposal for a counter team; the trainer confirms them against shift times (63).

**Sign-off criteria:** the checklist in `management/sign-off-checklist.md`, signed and dated by trainer and trainee: foundations; every HIGH moment in every register; the service moments; the four silences verified under a clock; the knowledge block; three shadow shifts. A trainee who does not pass gets targeted coaching on the failed lines and is signed off against the list, never on a feeling.
