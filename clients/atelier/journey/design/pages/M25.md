# M25 · HIGH IMPACT · Her moment shared online

Digital touchpoint · The card on the desk (phase 7) · hours–days · Self-led · owner: marketing

Dominant inputs: **brand** (Giulia's written voice) and **BE**; concept (the account, consents); non-verbal not applicable.

## CURRENT STATE

She posts the note or the cup, tags the atelier or uses #MyAtelierMoment. "They noticed again."

## DO

1. Reply within 24 hours, one line, by name, in Giulia's written voice; no emoji unless mirroring one she used; no exclamation. ти on social; Вие if she wrote in Вие.
2. The reply answers her; it never claims her. Story block 7: she is noticing for herself.
3. A repost or a feature only after written permission by DM, kept in `consents/` with the date; then it becomes an M1 post in her own words.
4. Never ask her to tag, review or share; never reward.
5. A complaint: facts only, handed to Menelaos. Illness, allergy, a named staff member, legal or press: no draft, escalate (guardrails 11).
6. Giulia never replies in Evo's or Menelaos's name.

## SAY

Patterns, filter-tested:

| Case | Line |
| --- | --- |
| A note posted | "Good to see your note made it home, [name]." |
| A win posted | "Small wins count, [name]." |
| Permission ask (DM) | "May we share your photo on our page, in your words? A yes here is enough; we keep it on file." |

All **pass**: to her, no hurry, nothing sold; the permission ask is one ask, no reward.

## DON'T

- "Thanks for sharing!! 😍"; "Tag a friend"; "Use code…"; "Sorry you feel that way".
- Reposting without permission; a minor in frame without a guardian's release.
- Answering a complaint in Giulia's voice beyond the facts.

## BEHAVIORAL ECONOMICS

Social proof is hers to give, not the brand's to engineer. Friction preserved (BE 10) on the permission ask: one ask, no incentive (guardrails 2). Reciprocity is not used.

## RECOVERY

| If … | → |
| --- | --- |
| She declines the repost | "Of course." Nothing more. |
| Her post names a team member critically | Guardrails 11; Menelaos; no reply drafted. |
| Her post misstates a fact (an origin, "sugar-free") | No public correction of a guest; a kind DM with the fact, signed by the account, if it matters for claims. |

**State arc:** Posting → Answered → Noticed again

**Success metric:** Replies within 24 h ≥ 90 %, n shown (Bible KPI); a permission on file for every featured moment.

**Target emotion:** noticed again

**Brand pillar:** Notice the moment; story block 7
