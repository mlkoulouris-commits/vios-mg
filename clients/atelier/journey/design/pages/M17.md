## CURRENT STATE

She has chosen (M12, M13). She watches the hands. Next is the card (M18). She wants something real in her hands in under a minute.

## DO

1. **Counter**: scoop in her sight, plainly, the portion as sold; cup or cone as she asked.
2. Tiramisu: fetched from behind, already portioned, handed with its name ("L'Originale"). No assembly at the counter.
3. Hand it over at her chest height, handle or base toward her (NV D.3); then straight to the jar (M18).
4. While scooping: quiet. If she speaks, answer; do not narrate the craft.
5. Standard: under a minute from her pick to the order in her hand.

## SAY

Nothing is locked. If she asks: "Made this morning in our Lab." / „Направено тази сутрин в лабораторията ни." (Bible hard moments; fact sheet claims row). One true origin only from the fact sheet (M12).

## DON'T

- No theatre: no tossing, no sculpting, no show for the line.
- No "alla minute", no "our master gelatiere", no "hand-crafted with passion" (batch B line 9).
- No adding toppings, sizes or extras she did not ask for.
- No eyes on the line behind her while handing over.

## NON-VERBAL

Hands visible, clean, economical (NV C.3); the object handed, never pushed across (NV D.3). Face: quiet pride, half-smile (Bible §9 "showing a flavour", intensity 2).

## BEHAVIORAL ECONOMICS

BE 5 affect: plain, competent hands read as care; theatre reads as the brand as hero. No decision for her (BE 1).

## RECOVERY

If the wrong flavour, a dropped scoop or a broken cone → **R-WRONG**. If a flavour runs out mid-order → **R-SOLDOUT**.
