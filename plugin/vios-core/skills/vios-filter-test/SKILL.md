# Vios Filter Test

One question, derived from character, that transfers brand judgement from the practice to the operation. Scope: lines addressed to a guest, a partner or a team member (scripts, captions, replies, signage, emails, internal notes). The name, the tagline and the lockup are judged by the brand book's own tests, not by the filter test; say so in the output when a tagline would fail it. "Is this effusive?" "Is this performed?" "Is this anxious?" It tests character fit, not quality; both are required. Shared instrument: `vios-brand-book` builds it into Part 5, `vios-journey-design` runs every script through it, `vios-training-programme` prints it on the wall chart and in the playbook, `vios-consistency-qa` uses it beside row 2, `vios-persona-voice` holds it as the pre-write filter.

## Read first

- `../../references/section-3-4-filter-test.md`, verbatim: what it is and is not, the four derivation moves, the three modes, five failure modes, three worked examples.
- `[client]/brand-book.md` and `persona-bible.md`: the Personality Expression dimensions, the archetypal mix, the never-list, the is/is-not pairs. The trait comes from there, never from this skill's examples.

## Derive (four moves)

1. **One dominant trait.** The trait that most separates the brand from its category; usually a refusal (effusion, theatre, urgency, familiarity, performance) or, failing that, the positive it embraces (restraint, plainness, patience). One trait only; a two-trait question stops working.
2. **Phrase the question** in the brand's own vocabulary, negative form preferred because the fault is easier to see than the virtue: "is this fancy?" for a plain brand, "is this rushed?" for a considered one. Add one or two concrete markers so two people answer the same way: "is this anxious — does it apologise, hedge or seek reassurance?"
3. **Sharpen** against two batches: ten lines the brand would say (all must pass) and ten it would not (category copy, a competitor's, a parody; all must fail). Rewrite until both batches hold. Record the batches; they are the regression test for the annual review.
4. **Build it in**: brand book (with rationale and two or three worked passes and fails), operating handbook, floor playbook, wall chart, QA checklist.

## Apply (three modes)

- **Pre-write**: the writer holds the question; the better discipline.
- **Post-write**: seconds per piece; the safety net.
- **External audit**: partner, agency and manager-written content; fails go back with the test attached. Most drift comes from outside, unstopped.

## Failure modes to check at review

Too lenient (names a trait the category shares) · too strict (tests a word, not the trait) · ambiguous (trait named, not marked) · outlived the brand (review yearly on the standing rotation, log revisions) · replaced the brand book (present it beside its source section).

## Output

`[client]/brand-book.md`, in a section headed "Filter test" (inside Part 5 when the file has parts; under Messages and story when it is the live summary): the question, the trait and why, two passes and two fails with one-line reasoning, the two sharpening batches in `[client]/archive/filter-test-batches.md`, the review date. One line at the end: the question, and where it has been placed.

## Example (placeholders)

Brand: the Quiet Refuser. Trait: refusal of effusion. Test: **Is this effusive?** Pass: "Welcome — sit anywhere." Fail: "Welcome to our wonderful restaurant! We're so excited to have you!!" Markers: exclamation marks, self-praise of the venue, performed warmth.
