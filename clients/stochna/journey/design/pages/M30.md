# M30 · CRITICAL IMPACT · The bill, when asked ⊐ end-1

In-room · 2–5 min · Phase 6, leaving · Guest-led · **End, designed as one with M31** (see `M31.md`; shared design in `choreography.md` › The end pair)

**Dominant inputs:** brand (block 5; "nothing is requested") · BE (the end, the payment pain) · concept (payment route, CONFIRM open fact 8) · non-verbal (no hovering).

## CURRENT STATE
Full, talked out, glad. The table asks for the bill. Before: the evening and the cleared plates (M24–M25). After: the farewell (M31). The last risk: the evening turning into a transaction.

## DO
1. The bill comes only when asked, by anyone at the table.
2. The server brings it within 3 minutes, places it at the centre of the table (not in front of one person), and leaves.
3. Splitting: accepted without comment, in whatever way the table wants (CONFIRM: what Barsy and its replacement can do).
4. Payment: at the table or the counter, per open fact 8. Card machine carried, not waved across the table.
5. **Nothing else on the tray.** No review card, no QR card, no loyalty card, no survey. The Razberry card question (open fact 23) is decided before this page locks.

## SAY (to lock)
- On asking: EN "Of course." · BG „Разбира се.“
- On placing: nothing, or EN "No rush." · BG „Няма бързане.“

## DON'T
- No bill before it is asked for, even at closing (closing time is said separately, once).
- No "how was everything?" at the bill.
- No tip prompt on the machine beyond what the machine forces (CONFIRM).
- No review ask, spoken or printed.

## NON-VERBAL
Bill placed, hand withdrawn, server turns away within two seconds (NV D.3, C.4). Waits out of the table's sightline while they sort it (NV A.4). Same calm pace as the rest of the evening (NV E.1).

## BEHAVIORAL ECONOMICS
BE 7 peak-end: the end is half of what is remembered; a transactional exit discounts the peak. BE 13 mental accounting: paying is the moment of loss; keep it short, central, unwatched, so it is not the last feeling. BE 10 friction design: remove friction from paying, add none (no forms, no asks).

## RECOVERY
- Bill slow → server says EN "Coming now" · BG „Идва веднага“ once and brings it within a minute (R-M30).
- Bill wrong → corrected at the counter, brought back, "Fixed." · „Оправено.“ No debate at the table (R-M30).
- Card machine fails → second machine or the counter; never ask for cash first (R-M30).
- Complaint at the bill → R-UPSET; compensation only by the manager, afterwards, privately.

**State arc:** That was good → Settled without fuss → That was a great evening.
**Success metric:** from "the bill, please" to paid in under 8 minutes, with no second approach from staff.
**Target emotion:** content.
**Brand pillar:** Worth the trip · Human, not performed · story block 7.

**Brand-coherence test:** Guide closes without turning it into a transaction ✓. **Dependencies:** payment route (open fact 8); POS split-bill capability (Barsy replacement); the review-card decision (open fact 23).
