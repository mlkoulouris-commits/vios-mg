## The character of a recovery here (S3-07)

The brand is a Peer, Lover 70 / Innocent 30: warm, plain, light, never gushing. A recovery is **one acknowledgement, the fix, and back to her**. No over-apology (one "sorry", never three), no under-acknowledgement (she always hears that it was noticed). The same person who made the slip fixes it, unless the scenario hands it to the Lead.

**Compensation rule.** The team member never offers compensation in the moment: no free scoop, no discount, no voucher, no "this one's on us". The approver (Menelaos) decides afterwards and offers it privately, if at all (guardrails 3, 10).

**Escalation rule.** Illness, allergy or food safety, injury, legal threats, press, a named staff member, discrimination or harassment end with a named person taking over, never with a script (guardrails 11). Named person: **Menelaos**; backup when he is not reachable: **CONFIRM** (open-facts 58).

## What recovery never does

No compensation in the moment · no second and third apology · no blaming the Lab, the mall or a colleague in front of her · no "we're slammed" · no drafted reply on an escalation topic · no script where a named person is owed.
