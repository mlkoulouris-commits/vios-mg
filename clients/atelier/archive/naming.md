**Recommendation.** Two-line system kept: **lock-up "Happiness is an attitude."** with the descriptors; **editorial line "What are we celebrating today?"** for story-facing use (feed, story cards, email headers, the B2B story card), replacing "Italian joy crafted daily." If Evo wants "Italian" to stay visible, it belongs in the descriptor or the mission line ("the Italian way"), as craft, not as "joy". The decision is his (open-facts 30, updated with this recommendation). Note on the filter test (`brand-book.md` › Filter test): both taglines are brand assets judged here, not by the filter test; the editorial line is, unusually, also a line the brand says to a guest, and it passes the filter test in that use.
