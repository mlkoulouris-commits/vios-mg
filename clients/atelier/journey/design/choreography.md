## 1. Continuities

### 1.1 The cluster around the peak: M09–M19, the ritual

The peak (M18, the Note) is protected by the nine moments before it, designed as one sequence so that the card lands on a guest who has already been asked, heard and served plainly.

| Moment | What it does for the peak |
| --- | --- |
| M09 the question ★ | opens what the card closes; the card is "for your moment" only because she was asked what it is |
| M10 the beat ○ | lets the question be a question; a filled beat turns the card into a gimmick later |
| M11 the answer | whatever she said, including "nothing", is accepted, so the card cannot read as a reward for a good answer |
| M12, M13 choosing, tasting | kept light (a method, two spoons, one fact) so she arrives at the card unhurried and unsold to |
| M14–M16 facts | said once, plainly; no defence, so no friction is carried into the card |
| M17 scooped | plain hands; the craft does not compete with the card |
| **M18 the Note ◉** | blind draw in her sight, handed with eye contact, before the till |
| M19 payment | after the card, always; the till cannot end the moment |

Rule for the whole cluster: **one face**. The Counter who asks the question hands the card. If a Second scoops or works the till, the question and the card stay with the Counter (§ 3, H1).

### 1.2 The end and the next threshold: M25 + M26 + M06, one composition

On a counter one face ends a visit and opens the next in the same seconds. Designed as one move, in this order:

1. Leaver turns to go → Counter stops the hands, faces her, "Until the next one, [name]." (M25)
2. Eyes stay with her to the door or into the mall flow (M26).
3. **Split rule:** the newcomer gets a look and a nod only after the leaver has turned her back; then M06 in full.
4. **Two on:** the one who served holds M25–M26; the other gives the newcomer M06. Agreed by a look.
5. **One alone at a peak band (R-ALONE):** the goodbye words and eyes are kept; the watch to the door is dropped first; the newcomer's look comes straight after the leaver turns.

It bookends the threshold (S3-06): the same face, the same plainness, the same brevity at M06 and M25.

### 1.3 Eyes before words

At every point where the brand speaks to her (M06, M09, M18, M25), the eyes reach her face first and the words follow. No line is said from the till, the screen, the scoop or over a shoulder.

### 1.4 One line, a beat, then the hands

After every locked line or gesture (the question, the card, the goodbye), one beat of stillness before the hands move to the next task. Nothing is said in the beat.

### 1.5 Where the team stands when not with a guest

Behind the case, facing the door (Rakovski) or the flow (Ring Mall), body toward the guest side; quiet tasks only (a scoop wiped, a label straightened). Eyes-down work (prep, stock counts, phone, the screen) is done before 11:00, after close, or by the Second at the back while the Counter faces out. Never both backs to the door.

### 1.6 One voice from the caption to the goodbye

The same few words everywhere: "we", "today", the question, the card, no exclamation, no "next", ти at the counter and online, Вие in letters and B2B (Bible front page). A guest who read a caption hears the same person at the counter.
