# M24 · STANDARD · The pause at the mall (RM)

**Draft — book edition v1.0 unsigned (open-facts 39); observation move not held.**

In-room (mall) · Phase 5 The pause · 2–5 min standing · Ring Mall only · markers none · weight provisional · dominant dimension: volume (low; absent on purpose) · inputs: concept (seating at or near the counter: **CONFIRM**, open-facts 11), brand (message 3)

## CURRENT STATE

She stands beside the counter or on the mall's seating, eating in the flow, card in one hand. The line is already being served.

## DO

1. Nothing toward her; she is left alone.
2. Serve the next guest at a voice meant for that guest only, so she is not talked over.
3. When she turns to go: M25 and M26 as at Rakovski.

## DON'T

- No calling the next order over her.
- No moving her on to make room ("can you step aside?") unless she blocks a safety route; then once, plainly.
- No comment on the card.

Metric: no order called over a guest who is still eating at the counter (sampled Saturday hour).
