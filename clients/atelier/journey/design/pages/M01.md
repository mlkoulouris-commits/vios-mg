# M01 · HIGH IMPACT · A moment seen online

Digital touchpoint · Before: the blur (phase 1) · Self-led · owner: marketing

Dominant inputs: **brand** and **BE**; concept (the account, the release process); non-verbal not applicable.

## CURRENT STATE

Evening scroll, or a pause at her desk between two briefs. She sees a real guest at the counter holding a note, with the real reason they were celebrating, and a question back to her. "That could be my day."

## DO

1. Guest-moments pillar (35 % of posts, v3 §7.5): one real guest, signed release in `consents/`, their own words, the Note legible, natural light, mid-moment. Never a placeholder, never staged.
2. Caption in Giulia's written voice, 30–50 words, BG first then EN: the moment → one true detail (flavour, origin, "made this morning" when true) → the question back. Anchors core message block 5 and plays story blocks 1–2.
3. Hashtags #MyAtelierMoment #HappinessIsAnAttitude; #AIcreated / #AIсъздадено whenever Giulia appears or speaks on camera; the bio line in place on both accounts (Bible §13).
4. Alt text describing the real scene.
5. `vios-persona-voice` writes, `vios-consistency-qa` gates, `signed_off_at` filled before anything is scheduled (guardrails 10).
6. Comments answered by name, one line, within 24 h (M25 rules).

## SAY

Caption pattern (v3 §4.8), one example, filter-tested: "Today [name] celebrated [the real reason]. We chose Bronte pistachio together, made this morning, and one note from the jar. Small wins count. What are we celebrating today?" **Pass**: to the reader, no hurry, nothing sold.

## DON'T

- No product-only hero, no stock smile, no staged moment, no pastel kitsch (v3 §6.4).
- No emoji, no exclamation marks, no "best", no "last chance", no discount.
- No guest without a release; no minor without a guardian's release (guardrails 2, 7).
- No Giulia clip without the on-screen label from frame one and the platform AI label (guardrails 6).
- No "Baci", no alla-minute marks.

## BEHAVIORAL ECONOMICS

Social proof (BE 11): a real peer's moment, not a claim. Framing (BE 9): dessert framed as a celebration of a day. Affect heuristic (BE 5): this post is her pre-visit thin-slice; warmth here is read into the room later.

## RECOVERY

| If … | → |
| --- | --- |
| The featured guest asks to be taken down | Removed within 2 hours, no argument, a one-line thanks by DM. |
| A fact in the caption is wrong | Pulled within 2 hours; correction signed by Menelaos, never by Giulia; row in `qa-log.csv` (Bible §14). |
| A comment raises illness, allergy, a named staff member, legal or press | No reply drafted; Menelaos with the facts (guardrails 11). |

**State arc:** Scrolling → Recognising → Considering

**Success metric:** Guest-moment posts ≥ 35 % of the month, each with a release on file; saves and shares against the account's own history, n shown.

**Target emotion:** curious

**Brand pillar:** differentiator (block 5); Guest moments pillar
