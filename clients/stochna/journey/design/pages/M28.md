# M28 · HIGH IMPACT · Something goes wrong (if)

In-room · any · Phase 4–5 · Conditional · See `../recovery.md`

**Dominant inputs:** brand ("We got that wrong. Here's what we're doing instead.") · non-verbal · concept · BE.

## CURRENT STATE
Wrong dish, a long wait, a spill. Next: the evening carries on.

## DO
1. Own it at once, in one line.
2. Fix it; say what's happening and when.
3. No compensation from the server; the manager decides later (guardrails 3, 10).
4. Escalation topics → R-ALLERGY, R-ESC.

## SAY (to lock)
EN "We got that wrong. Here's the right one." · BG „Сбъркахме. Ето правилната.“

## DON'T
- No repeated apology; no blaming the kitchen.
- No free round as a reflex.

## NON-VERBAL
Calm, normal pace (NV B.2); face matches the words (NV C.2).

## BEHAVIORAL ECONOMICS
BE 7 peak-end: a fast, plain fix rarely dents the memory; a slow, fussy one does.

## RECOVERY
R-WRONG, R-UPSET, R-ALLERGY, R-ESC (`../recovery.md`).

**State arc:** That's not right → Owned and fixed → They fixed it and the evening carried on.
**Success metric:** fixed within 5 min, with one apology only.
**Target emotion:** reassured. **Brand pillar:** Human, not performed.
