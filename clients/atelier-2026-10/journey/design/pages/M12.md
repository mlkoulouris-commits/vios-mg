# M12 · STANDARD · Gelato, tiramisu, or both

In-room · Choosing together (phase 3) · ~5 sec · Counter · owner: floor · dominant dimension: language

## CURRENT STATE

She has chosen a gelato, or is looking at the tiramisu collection. "I know how this works."

## DO

1. Gelato first, then tiramisu, or together if she prefers (v3 §7.6). One offer, as a fact about what is there: "The tiramisu collection is here too: L'Originale, pistachio, hazelnut, blueberry, mango." Said once, only if she has not already looked at it.
2. Sizes named plainly when asked: single, sharing, celebration box of twelve. Gelato cups 100, 200, 500 ml.
3. Coffee: espresso, affogato, pairings, offered only if she sits or asks.
4. If she takes both, the Note still comes once per order, with the whole order (M15).

## DON'T

- "Anything else?", "And a tiramisu with that?", "Would you like to make it a sharing?", "Just one scoop?".
- A second offer after a no.

**Success metric:** On sampled orders, at most one offer of the second line, phrased as what is there, never as a question that invites a no. Filter: a fact, **pass**.
