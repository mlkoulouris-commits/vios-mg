# M24 · CRITICAL IMPACT · The next round, unhurried ◉

In-room · 60–100 min into the evening · Phase 5, the evening · Staff-offered, guest-decided · **Peak**

**Dominant inputs:** brand (block 4, Table is the centre) · BE (decision: stay or move on) · non-verbal (timing, distance) · concept (what is coming from the kitchen).

## CURRENT STATE
The table is mid-conversation, first glasses near empty, plates between courses. It has not decided to stay; it is about to, or about to drift. Before: the first round and plates (M15–M21). After: plates cleared without pressure (M25).

## DO
1. The server reads the table from their station (M23): glasses a third full or less, a pause in conversation, someone looking round. All three = ready; one alone = not yet.
2. Approach from the side of the person who last ordered, never from behind; stop at arm's length, standing.
3. Offer **once**, linked to what the kitchen is sending next. One beer named, not the list.
4. If yes: the beer arrives within 4 minutes (CONFIRM bar timing, observation). If no or "later": nod, leave; no second offer for at least 15 minutes and only if the table signals.
5. Room: sound and light unchanged by the server's approach (M26; music policy open fact 11).

## SAY (to lock)
- EN: "There's a darker one that goes with what's coming next. Want to try it?"
- BG: „Има една по-тъмна, която върви с това, което идва. Искате ли да я опитате?“
- If no: EN "Sure." · BG „Разбира се.“ — and nothing else.

NEW lines at Stage 3 (BG): batch 3, `archive/filter-test-batches.md`.

## DON'T
- No "another round?" or "same again?" (sells volume, not the pairing).
- No offer while someone is mid-sentence.
- No second offer after a no; no "last orders" until the real time, said once.
- No offering a beer that isn't paired with what's coming.

## NON-VERBAL
Side approach (NV A.2), standing at the table's edge not leaning in (NV A.3). Eyes to the person who answers, then to the table (NV C.2). Low volume, matched to the table, not the room (NV B.3). One beat of silence after the offer (NV B.4). Timing on the table's signal, not the clock (NV E.3).

## BEHAVIORAL ECONOMICS
BE 7 peak-end: this is the moment the remembering self keeps; the decision to stay is made here without feeling like a decision. BE 6 defaults: the default is that the table stays; nothing signals the evening ending. BE 8 choice architecture: one option, linked, instead of the list (BE 14 decision fatigue). No pressure device (guardrails BE five lines).

## RECOVERY
- If the offer interrupts → stop mid-line, "Later," step away (R-M24).
- If the table looks tired of being asked → no more offers this table; the table orders when it wants (R-M24).
- If the beer is out → name the other paired one; never "sorry, we're out of everything good" (R-WRONG).
- Over-served signs → R-OVER, the manager decides.
