## Signals to bring to the practice in the first month

Bring any of these to the weekly check-in, with x of n or raw numbers:

- the question shortened, skipped or said as a slogan in more than one order of a sampled hour;
- the card handed after the till, bagged or chosen;
- the goodbye said to the till or the next guest;
- a line the team will not say naturally after a week (it fails the read-aloud test on the floor and goes back to the page);
- a band where the ritual cannot hold with the people on (R-ALONE logged more than once a week);
- any escalation (R-ESC) or complaint (R-UPSET), with the facts;
- the jar running out (R-NOTES);
- guests hesitating at the door before a face turns, or not answering the question at all (the observation tests, `journey/observation.md` § 4).

## If the operation drifts from the design

1. Name the moment and the behaviour, in floor words.
2. It goes to the next two pre-shifts as the standing slot and to the coaching topic for whoever was on.
3. If the behaviour is right and the guest response is wrong, the page is wrong: it goes back to Stage 3 for a revision, versioned on the page (`[vX.Y]`), and the training files follow.
4. A change to a locked line is new copy: filter-tested, signed by Menelaos, logged.
