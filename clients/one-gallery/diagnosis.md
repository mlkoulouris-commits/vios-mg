# ONE Gallery — Brand Diagnosis

26 Oct 2026 · Vios Marketing · Live doc: https://claude.ai/artifact/2SaD1sC8qiXGXyRpUv13dF

## Verdict

**Meaning present.** The purpose is real and acted on every day: the Gallery Series, one welcome, exhibition openings. The brand strategy (Brand Book v2.2) holds. What doesn't yet match it is the live presence: website, Google listing, two Instagram accounts. Marketing's main job is to make guests see the art as the centre, not the backdrop.

## Audience

**Primary: Dilyana, 34**, communications manager, Sofia centre. Explorer 70 / Lover 30. Her city feels like fragments; she wants a place with soul where she belongs without performing it.

- Before: bored, compromising, hesitant about art ("I don't know enough to go alone")
- After: proud, belonging, curious, connected ("This is where we meet now")
- Lives next to: Aesop, Monocle, COS, Aperol, independent cafés

**Second: collectors and the art community.** Collectors, artists, curators, press. Served through channels (openings, the curator's voice, email), never through a different welcome on the floor.

**Gap:** the brand-affinity audit is still unvalidated. Guest reviews confirm the welcome and calm, but say nothing yet about Dilyana's taste profile. To check with real guests.

**The story within the story:** art is mentioned as something that is "usually there". No review names an artist, a work or the Gallery Series. One guest finds the cocktail choice small.

The house nights are one-offs (confirmed), but they show up in reviews. Keep them rare and never lead content with them.

## Differentiator and positioning

**Confirmed, with one sharper proof point.**

Differentiator (v2.2): contemporary art at the centre, with coffee in the morning and aperitivo in the evening around it. One place, one welcome, all day.

Positioning (v2.2): we help curious Sofia adults who live the city in fragments, and never felt at home in a gallery or a hotel bar, to experience contemporary art as part of daily life. Unlike standalone galleries, hotel bars, cafés or aperitivo rooms, we put art at the centre with coffee and aperitivo around it.

**New proof point from the competitor refresh:** the exhibition is open 10:00–00:00, every day. Galleries in this set close at 18:00 and at weekends. "All day" is literally true, and it's the easiest claim for a guest to check.

## Archetype and Brand Role

Confirmed from v2.2.

- **Brand archetype:** Caregiver 70 (core) / Sage 30 (influencer)
- **Audience archetype:** Explorer 70 / Lover 30
- **Brand Role:** Guide. "I know this terrain and I'll show you in without testing you at the door."
- **Two voices, one character:** the team's "we" for hospitality; the curator's signed voice for the art

Reviews back the Caregiver core (warmth comes up most). The Sage layer is not yet visible to guests: no review mentions an artist or a work.

## Contradictions and upstream fixes

**Six gaps between the brand book and what guests see.** None is strategic; all are fixable before content starts.

| # | What guests see | Brand book says | Fix |

| 1 | Website footer: gallery Mon–Fri 12:00–19:00 | Exhibition open whenever the café is | One set of hours on site and Google: daily 10:00–00:00 |
