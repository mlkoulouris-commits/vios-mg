# M26 · STANDARD · The review

Digital touchpoint · The card on the desk (phase 7) · days · Self-led · owner: marketing · dominant dimension: language

## CURRENT STATE

She writes a review, or is asked once. "I said it; they heard it."

## DO

1. Every guest may be asked once, never rewarded, never gated on satisfaction (guardrails 2). The ask, where used, is one line on the receipt or by the till: "If you'd like to tell others about today, our Google page is here." with the link; no incentive, no "5 stars".
2. Replies drafted for Menelaos in Giulia's written voice, by name, one or two lines, thanks and one true detail; signed by Menelaos, never by Giulia (Bible §1).
3. A critical review: facts only, no defence, "I'm sorry. Menelaos will write to you today."; illness, allergy, injury, a named staff member, legal, press: no draft, escalate with the facts (guardrails 11).
4. The 4.9 across about 1,130 reviews is real equity (v3 §5.2); it is never quoted in copy as "best rated".

## DON'T

- "Leave us a review and get…"; asking only happy guests; copy-paste replies; arguing; replying to a health complaint.

**Success metric:** Reviews mentioning the question, the note or being noticed (Bible KPI, raw count); every reply signed by Menelaos; zero incentivised asks. Filter: the ask line, **pass** (one ask, no push).
