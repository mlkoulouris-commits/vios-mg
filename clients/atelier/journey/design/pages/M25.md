# M25 · CRITICAL IMPACT · The goodbye, by name ⊐ end-1

**Draft — book edition v1.0 unsigned (open-facts 39); observation move not held.**

In-room · Phase 6 The door · ~3–5 sec · Both counters · **⊐ end-1** (pair M25 + M26, designed as one; the shared design is in `choreography.md` § 1.2; see M26) · weight provisional, brand-led

Dominant inputs: **brand** (Bible farewell, story block 7, message 7) · **non-verbal** (orientation, voice, exit geometry). BE consulted: peak-end, framing. Concept consulted: the counter, the next guest, the till.

## CURRENT STATE

She is leaving: after the till at the mall, after the pause at Rakovski. She has the card. The next guest may already be at the counter. These are the last words anyone at the Atelier says to her; what she felt at the card is confirmed or lost here.

## DO

1. **The team member who served her** says the goodbye. If she paused at Rakovski and a colleague is nearer the door, the nearer one says it; never both.
2. When she turns to go (stands, picks up her bag, steps back from the counter): stop what the hands are doing. Scoop down, hands still.
3. Turn shoulders and face to her, not to the till or the queue.
4. Say the line, by name if known.
5. Hold her eyes until she turns away. Then M26.
6. A guest who leaves without looking: say it anyway, at the same volume, to her back. Not louder.

## SAY

**LOCKED** (Bible §7; not new):

| | EN | BG |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Name known | "Until the next one, [name]." | „До следващия, [име]." |
| Name not known | "Until the next one." | „До следващия." |

Registers: **closing time** — the same line; never "we're closing" as the last words. **Child with a parent** — the same line, eyes to the child first, then the parent. **After a hard-day answer (M11)** — the same line, softer; nothing added.

Filter test: hurry no · sell no (it does not ask her to come back; it says there will be a next one) · no one in particular no → **pass**. The name is used once, here, never twice.

## DON'T

- Don't say it to the till, the screen, or over your shoulder.
- Don't say it to the next guest's face while she is still leaving.
- Don't add "have a nice day", "see you soon", "come back", or a sales line.
- Don't ask for a review here (M28 lives elsewhere).
- Don't use a name you are not sure of.

## NON-VERBAL

Orientation: shoulders square to her (NV C.4). Hands still and visible, not on the scoop or the till (NV C.3). Eyes: on hers for the line, held until she turns (NV C.2). Voice: the warmth of the question, a notch quieter, falling at the end (NV B.1, B.3). Exit geometry: the team member does not move toward the next guest until M26 is done (NV A.4). Echoes M06: same face, same plainness, same brevity (S3-06).

## BEHAVIORAL ECONOMICS

- **BE 7 peak-end** (evidence tier): the end is half of what is kept. A goodbye said to someone else tells her, last, that she was the order.
- **BE 9 framing**: "until the next one" frames the visit as one in a series, not a transaction closed. It is a statement, not a request (guardrails 1).
- No decision for her here.

## RECOVERY

- If the goodbye went to the till or to the next guest and she is still in sight → say it again, to her, plainly, once (**R-M25**).
- If she is already out → nothing after her; the shift lead notes it for the pre-shift.
- If a name was wrong → "Sorry, [right name]." once, then the line (**R-M25**).

## HIGH page additions

- **Brand-coherence test.** Peer: an equal still with her at the end, not a server turning to the next order → **holds**. Connection ("let's celebrate it together"): the last words are to her → **holds**.
- **Story block:** 7, the new world (she leaves with a place where her days get noticed).
- **Operational dependencies:** staffing per daypart (47) and the R-ALONE rule; how names are learned and kept (45); the next guest held for a breath (M26, `choreography.md` § 1.2).
