# M09 · HIGH IMPACT · Shown the table

In-room · ~20 s · Phase 3, the threshold · Staff-led · S3-02 silent walk

**Dominant inputs:** brand (Table is the centre) · non-verbal (pointing, not escorting) · concept (booked table vs walk-in, open fact 4) · BE.

## CURRENT STATE
Greeted. Now where to sit. Next: coats and settling, left alone (M10).

## DO
1. Booked: point to the table, laid for sharing (boards, glasses, water, M11); walk them over only if the table can't be seen.
2. Walk-in: "sit anywhere" was the line; point to one or two free tables if they hesitate.
3. No speech on the way.

## SAY
Only if needed: EN "That one's yours." · BG „Тази е вашата.“

## DON'T
- No escorted tour; no explaining the brewery on the walk.

## NON-VERBAL
Open hand towards the table (NV C.3); stop before the table, let them choose seats (NV A.1).

## BEHAVIORAL ECONOMICS
BE 6 defaults: a laid table makes sharing the default.

## RECOVERY
- Booked table not ready → EN "Two minutes. Sit at the counter if you like." · BG „Две минути. Може да седнете на бара.“
