## 2. Story and point of view (no invented biography)

Giulia believes happiness is an attitude and small wins count. She thinks most places treat people as orders, and that noticing someone costs nothing and changes their day. On gelato: made this morning, true origins, nothing hyped. On tiramisu: a collection made fresh behind the scenes, never theatre. On the neighbourhood: Rakovski is where people walk home with their day still on their shoulders; the atelier is the ten minutes that sets it down. On B2B partners: only those who will ask the question at their own tables.

BG: Джулия вярва, че щастието е нагласа и малките победи се броят. Смята, че повечето места гледат на хората като на поръчки, а да забележиш някого не струва нищо и променя деня му.

## 4. Traits

| Is | Is not |
| --- | --- |
| Warm | distant or corporate |
| A peer beside the guest | a teacher or a show above her |
| Light and optimistic | cute, loud or childish |
| Attentive to the person | generic or scripted |
| Calm and unhurried | rushed or salesy |
| Honest: true stories, plain facts | invented origins or hype |
| Crafted with care, quietly | process theatre |
| Human, in person | automated or faceless |

**Never-trait:** pushy. **Never says:** "Next!", "Anything else?", "That's our bestseller.", "Last chance", "Treat yourself", "You deserve it", "The best gelato in Sofia". **Never does:** stage a guest moment, post a product-only hero, use a stock smile, answer a complaint about illness or allergy herself, use "Baci" or any alla-minute mark.

## 5. Voice and tone rules

Voice traits (v3 §5.4): warm · attentive · light · calm · honest. Short sentences. Uses "we" and "today".

| Do | Don't | EN example | BG example |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Greet like a regular | greet like a brand | "Good to see you again." | „Радваме се да те видим пак." |
| Be specific to the person | be generic | "Pistachio again, or something new today?" | „Пак шамфъстък или нещо ново днес?" |
| Stay light | be cute or loud | "Small wins count." | „Малките победи се броят." |
| Give time | create urgency | "Take your time." | „Не бързай." |
| State the plain fact | hype | "Made this morning." | „Направено тази сутрин." |

Content lengths: caption 30–50 words · flavour story 50–75 · email 150–200 · story card 25–40 · Atelier Note 10–15.

## 7. Signature phrases and habits

- Farewell: "Until the next celebration." · "Small wins count." — „До следващото празнуване." · „Малките победи се броят."
- Habit: the Atelier Note is drawn by chance from the jar, never chosen; names are used once known; eye contact before words.

## 14. Error protocol and retirement

Pull within 2 hours by the approver → correction signed by Menelaos or Evo, never by Giulia → row in `qa-log.csv` → fix the Bible or the brief → two serious errors in a month pause persona content. Retire the format if no KPI moves after two content cycles, if guests react badly, or if a rule changes; old content stays as is.

## 15. Pilot before scaling

3–5 pieces (two guest-moment posts, one Creations post with a true origin, one Reel of the question being asked, one Atelier Note story) shown to 3–5 real guests matching Elena before daily posting or any paid spend. Questions: does it feel like the place? did you notice the question and the Note? would you share your own moment? A "no" on the first means rewrite the voice, not the script.
