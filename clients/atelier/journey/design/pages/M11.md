# M11 · CRITICAL IMPACT · The answer received, or "nothing" accepted

**Draft — book edition v1.0 unsigned (open-facts 39); observation move not held.**

In-room · Phase 3 The question · ~5–20 sec · Both counters · markers none · weight provisional, brand-led

Dominant inputs: **brand** (story block 5, value 11 Notice the moment, message 4) · **BE** (no persuasion after a "no") · **non-verbal** (listening, face, voice). Concept consulted: the case, the line behind her.

## CURRENT STATE

She has been asked (M09) and given a beat (M10). She has said it out loud ("I passed my test", "Friday"), or said "nothing", or shrugged. Saying it felt small, almost silly. The real risk for her is the generic friendly again; this is where it is answered or confirmed.

## DO

1. **Counter**: listen to the end of what she says. Eyes on her, hands still.
2. **She answered:** give one sentence back that uses her own word ("Your test. Then something that tastes like that."). If no sentence comes naturally, the locked line.
3. **"Nothing", a shrug, or "I just want ice cream":** the locked line, lightly, at once. The same scoop, the same card follow.
4. **A heavy answer** (a hard day, a loss): the heavy-answer register, quietly. No celebration words after it.
5. **She tells more:** listen. No second question about her answer; no advice; no story of your own.
6. Then move to choosing (M12), at her pace.
7. A colleague nearby does not join in or comment.

The Bible carries three forms of the "nothing" line (§5 "Nothing's fine too. Then just something good.", §7 "Then just something good.", hard moments "Then just something good for today."); §7 is locked here as the signature list; the approver confirms (open-facts 52).

Free (not locked): the one sentence back with her word, in her language, under ten words, no exclamation.

Filter test: all three lines hurry no · sell no · no one in particular no → **pass**. The NEW line is logged in `archive/filter-test-batches.md`, Stage 3 block.

## DON'T

- Don't push after "nothing" ("Oh come on, there must be something").
- Don't over-react: no "Wow!", no "Congratulations!!", no clapping, no calling a colleague over.
- Don't laugh at the answer, or turn it into a joke.
- Don't ask a follow-up question, or give advice.
- Don't say "Happiness is an attitude" as a reply (Bible §7 habits).
- Don't repeat her answer to the next guest or the room.

## NON-VERBAL

Listening: still, eyes on her, a small nod (NV C.2, C.3). Face: shared joy for a good answer, smile reaching the eyes, **capped at 3 of 5** (Bible §9; party energy is on the never-list); neutral and attentive for a heavy answer. Voice: matches her register a notch below her volume; never louder than her (NV B.1, B.3). Pace: no rush to the scoop (NV B.2, E.1).

## BEHAVIORAL ECONOMICS

- **No persuasion after a "no"** (house doctrine, guardrails BE five lines): "nothing" is an answer, not an objection to overcome.
- **BE 12 loss aversion**, applied to the guest's exposure: what she risks here is feeling foolish; an over-reaction costs her more than a quiet one gains.
- **BE 7 peak-end**: not the peak, but a bad reception here colours the card; a good one makes it hers.

## RECOVERY

- If the answer was laughed at, missed, or met with a push → **R-M11**: the same team member, at once, plainly, "Sorry, I missed that. [her word]." then the locked line; no second apology.
- If she reacts as if the question was intrusive → the "nothing" line, and move on.
- If what she says touches illness, injury, a complaint about a staff member, or discrimination → no comment beyond the heavy-answer line; if it is a complaint, **R-UPSET** / **R-ESC**.

## HIGH page additions

- **Brand-coherence test.** Peer: accepts the answer as it is, beside her; never a host performing delight or a seller pushing → **holds**. Connection ("let's celebrate it together"): any answer, including none, is celebrated in the same way → **holds**.
- **Story block:** 5, the challenge ("saying it out loud feels small, almost silly").
- **Operational dependencies:** training on the over-reaction cap (ops brief § 6); a line behind her does not shorten the listening (R-QUEUE).
