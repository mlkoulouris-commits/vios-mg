# M14 · STANDARD · Scooped and boxed

In-room · Marking it (phase 4) · ~20 sec · Counter · owner: floor · dominant dimensions: appearance, accessories

## CURRENT STATE

She has said the flavour. She watches. "Made with care, not performed."

## DO

1. Scoop from the display, clean spatula, the tray left tidy; the flavour handwritten on the kraft cup (v3 §7.4); a wooden or bamboo spoon with it.
2. Tiramisu from the collection in its clear-top kraft box, layers showing; the box closed without flourish.
3. Nothing said about the scooping. If she asks, one fact ("Made this morning in our Lab.").
4. Eyes to the work while scooping, up to her when it is done; the cup or box set down in front of her, not handed over her head (NV D.3).
5. No theatre: no spatula flip, no "watch this", no staged slowness. Alla minute and in-front preparation are retired (v3 §7.1).

## DON'T

- Commentary on the craft; a performance for the queue; a sloppy tray; a plastic spoon; a printed label where the hand should write.

**Success metric:** On sampled orders, the flavour handwritten on the cup, the tray tidy after the scoop, no commentary. Filter: not applicable (no line).
