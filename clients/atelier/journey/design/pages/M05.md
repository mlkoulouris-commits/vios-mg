Dominant inputs: **non-verbal** (environmental cues: sound, scent, light, surface) · **brand** (message 3 The pause; the affinity set: restraint). Concept consulted: the room as it is (facts open: 27, 44). BE consulted: affect, cognitive ease, thin-slicing.

## CURRENT STATE

She has decided on the street (M03) and opens the door. In two seconds, before any face, the room tells her whether this is a shop floor or a pause. Next: someone looks up (M06).

## DO

The room's standard, checked at open (11:00) and on the hour by the counter (R-ROOM):

1. **Case:** full, lit, labels straight, no gaps; eight classics in the same places every day.
2. **Jar:** on the counter, cards visible through the glass, in sight from the door (position: open-facts 27, 54).
3. **Sound:** low enough for two people to talk across the counter at a normal voice; nothing with a driving beat. What plays, at what level, who controls it: **CONFIRM** (open-facts 44).
4. **Scent and air:** the room smells of the case and the coffee, nothing added that competes; no cleaning smell during open hours. Scent at the door today: **CONFIRM** (44).
5. **Path and chair:** a clear path from the door to the case; at least one chair free and visible (tables: **CONFIRM**, 27).

## SAY

Nothing. The room speaks first.

## DON'T

- No promotional signs, price boards with "offer", or "best gelato" claims (guardrails 1, 3, 9).
- No music up to fill an empty room.
- No mop, boxes or delivery crates in the path in open hours.
- No team member with their back to the door at the case.

## NON-VERBAL

Environmental cues carry the moment (NV F.1 light, F.2 sound, F.3 scent, F.4 surface): warm, even light on the case; sound under conversation; the jar and the case as the only objects asking to be looked at. The first surface she touches is the door; it opens easily (NV D.1).

## BEHAVIORAL ECONOMICS

BE 5 affect and BE 1 cognitive ease: a quiet, legible room is read as "a pause" before any word. BE 17 thin-slicing (contested tier, design heuristic).

## RECOVERY

If the room is wrong at open or mid-shift (case gap, jar empty, sound up, path blocked) → **R-ROOM**.
