# M26 · CRITICAL IMPACT · Attention held until she is out ⊐ end-2

**Draft — book edition v1.0 unsigned (open-facts 39); observation move not held.**

In-room · Phase 6 The door · ~3–8 sec (Rakovski: counter to door; Ring Mall: counter to the mall flow) · Both counters · **⊐ end-2** (pair M25 + M26, designed as one; the shared design is in `choreography.md` § 1.2; see M25) · weight provisional, brand-led

Dominant inputs: **non-verbal** (eye behaviour, exit geometry, synchronisation) · **brand** (value 12 Stay human, message 12). Concept consulted: staffing per counter, the next guest's arrival. BE consulted: peak-end.

## CURRENT STATE

The goodbye has been said (M25). She is walking to the door or into the mall. Behind her, the next guest is at the counter or in the queue. At a counter the same face ends one visit and opens the next in the same seconds; this page decides who gets which second.

## DO

1. After the line, eyes stay on her as she walks away, until she reaches the door (Rakovski) or turns into the flow (Ring Mall).
2. Body stays as it was for M25: no step to the till, no hands back to the case, until she is out.
3. **The split rule (counter variant).** The leaver gets the last second; the newcomer gets the first look straight after. If the newcomer is already at the counter: a short look and a nod to the newcomer is allowed **once the leaver has turned her back**, not before.
4. **Two on the counter:** the one who served her holds M26; the other gives the newcomer M06. Agree it with a look, not a word.
5. **One alone at a peak band (R-ALONE):** keep the goodbye words and eyes until she turns; then go to the newcomer at once. What is dropped is the watch to the door, never the goodbye.
6. If she looks back: a small nod or a small smile. No wave, no second line.
7. Then M06 for the next guest. The newcomer never hears the leaver's goodbye said toward them.

## SAY

Nothing. M25 carried the words.

## DON'T

- Don't turn to the next guest while she is still facing you.
- Don't say "next" or "who's next?" at all (Bible never-words).
- Don't call after her, wave, or add a line.
- Don't start the next order's question over her goodbye.
- Don't fill the gap with the till; the till waits two seconds.

## NON-VERBAL

Eyes: on her back to the door (NV C.2). Exit geometry: she leaves the space; the team member stays (NV A.4). Synchronisation: the newcomer's first look follows the leaver's last second, not overlaps it (NV E.4). Posture still, warm resting face (Bible §9 sign-off, intensity 3; guardrails NV stand-in "warm last frame"). The newcomer reads this too: a team member who watches the last guest out is the thin-slice the newcomer gets (BE 17, contested tier).

## BEHAVIORAL ECONOMICS

- **BE 7 peak-end** (evidence tier): the end is a sequence on a counter; without M26 the M25 words land on the next guest's face.
- **BE 17 thin-slicing** (contested; used as a design heuristic): the newcomer's first seconds include watching how the last guest was let go.
- No decision for her.

## RECOVERY

- If eyes went to the newcomer before she turned → nothing to her; the newcomer still gets M06 in full; the lead notes it (**R-M26**).
- If the counter is alone and a queue builds while she leaves → **R-ALONE** then **R-QUEUE**; the goodbye is never cut to save seconds.
- If she comes back in (forgot something) → M06 again, by name, no comment.

- **Story block:** 7, the new world.
