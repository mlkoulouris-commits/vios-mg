# Floor playbook — Stochna Kitchen Brewery

DRAFT 0.1 · 2026-11-02 · lives at the bar · lines not locked yet (pull final wording from `journey/design/pages/` when locked) · BG staff edition due (open fact 31).

## Page 1 — Three rules and one exit

1. **Nothing is asked of the guest.** No review, card, survey.
2. **Offer once.**
3. **Look up. Nod. Keep going.**

**Not yours — get the manager:** allergy · illness · injury · legal threat · press · harassment · a complaint about a named colleague.
„Ще повикам [име].“ / "I'll get [name]."

## The evening, in order

| | Moment | Say (BG / EN) | Then |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| M07 | Door opens | — | look up, nod, keep working |
| M08 | Greeting | „Здравейте. Седнете където искате.“ / "Hi. Sit anywhere." · booked: „На какво име?“ / "Under what name?" | point |
| M09 | Table | „Тази е вашата.“ / "That one's yours." (only if needed) | leave them to settle |
| M12 | Menu | „Заповядайте. Връщам се след малко.“ / "Here you go. Back in a few minutes." | leave |
| M15 | Order | „[Бирата] върви добре с [ястието].“ · „Ако не сте сигурни, ще ви налея нещо.“ | repeat the order once |
| M17 ★ | Beer + bread | „Бирата е варена тук. Хлябът е изпечен тук…“ (fallback: „Варена тук. Изпечен тук. Вървят заедно.“) | gone |
| M19 | Plates | „[Ястие]. Върви с [бирата].“ (only if they look up) | gone |
| M24 ◉ | Next round | „Има една по-тъмна, която върви с това, което идва. Искате ли да я опитате?“ · no → „Разбира се.“ | no second offer |
| M25 | Clearing | if asked: „Стойте колкото искате. Не броим.“ | glasses stay |
| M30 ⊐ | Bill | „Разбира се.“ | nothing on the tray |
| M31 ⊐ | Farewell | „До следващия път.“ / "See you next time." | back to work |

## When it goes wrong

| Code | Sign | Say | Who |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| R-M07 | someone waiting at the door | „Здравейте — седнете където искате.“ | anyone |
| R-FULL | full, walk-in | „Тази вечер сме пълни. В четвъртък има място.“ (a day that really has room) | door |
| R-WRONG | wrong dish or beer, long wait | „Сбъркахме. Ето правилната.“ | server |
| R-M30 | bill wrong, machine down | „Оправено.“ · second machine | server, then manager |
| R-UPSET | guest unhappy | listen once · „Ще повикам [име].“ | manager |
| R-OVER | too much to drink | stop offering, bring water | manager |
| R-ALLERGY | any allergy word | „Ще помоля кухнята да отговори.“ | kitchen lead / manager |
| R-ESC | injury, illness, legal, press, harassment | — | manager, now |

**Never give anything free.** The manager decides later.

## If a guest asks about the online host

„Да, [Име] е нашият AI домакин онлайн. Бирата, храната и залата са истински.“ / "Yes, [Name] is our AI host online. The beer, food and room are real."

## Leave them alone

Coats · reading the menu · waiting for the pour · first sip · the conversation · the walk out.

## Before you improvise

"Would someone who never explains themselves say or do this at their own table?"
