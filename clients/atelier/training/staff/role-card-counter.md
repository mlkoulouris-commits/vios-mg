# Role card — the Counter (and the Second)

**Draft — book edition v1.0 unsigned (open-facts 39); observation move not held.**

Two laminated panels, pocket size. Whoever faces the guest is the Counter. Nothing else goes on this card (S4-04). Lines marked † wait for Menelaos's signature; laminate after the read-aloud test and the BG instructions (open-facts 64).

---

## Panel 1 — The ritual

**Look first. Then speak. You asked, you hand the card, you say goodbye.**

**1. She comes in — 3 seconds, eyes up.** Smile. Hello. Busy? Eyes and a nod.
Name, for sure: "[Name]. Good to see you." / „[Име]. Радвам се да те видя." †

**2. She reads the case — say nothing.** Half a step back.

**3. She looks up — ★ ask it, whole, every order.**
"What are we celebrating today?" / „Какво празнуваме днес?"
After a wait: "Thanks for waiting." / „Благодаря, че изчака." — then the question.

**4. Then nothing.** Eyes on her. Hands still.

**5. Her answer is the answer.**
Her word back, or: "Then something that tastes like that." / „Тогава нещо с вкус на това."
"Nothing": "Then just something good." / „Тогава просто нещо хубаво."
Hard day: "I'm sorry. Let's find you something good." / „Съжалявам. Да ти намерим нещо хубаво." †

**6. Order in her hand → jar → ◉ draw blind → edges, face to her.**
"A card for your moment. It's drawn by chance." / „Картичка за твоя момент. Теглим ги на късмет."
Let go. Half step back. **Then the till.**

**7. She turns to go — ⊐ hands still, face her.**
"Until the next one, [name]." / „До следващия, [име]."

**8. Eyes with her to the door. Then the next guest.**

Never: "Next" · "Anything else?" · "bestseller" · "everyone takes it" · "sugar-free".

## Panel 2 — When it goes wrong

**One sorry. Fix it. Back to her. Never a freebie.**

| Trigger | Do |
| --- | --- |
| Nobody looked up | Look up now. Hello. No sorry. |
| Forgot the question | Ask it now, before the card. |
| Missed her answer | "Sorry, I missed that." / „Извинявай, не те чух." † Her word. The line. |
| Forgot the card | Before she pays: now. Gone? Let her go. Log it. |
| "No thanks" to the card | Nod. Back in the jar. |
| Jar empty | Reserve. None? Say it once. No substitute. Tell the Lead. |
| A line | A look per face. Question whole. Card always. |
| Alone at a rush | Drop the watch to the door first. Never the question, the card, the beat, the goodbye. |
| Allergen question | Not on the matrix? "I'll check with the Lab and tell you exactly." / „Ще проверя с лабораторията и ще ти кажа точно." Can't check? Don't serve it. |
| Might be under 18 | No Aperitivo. A classic, plainly. |
| Upset guest | Calm. Short. "I'm sorry. Menelaos will write to you today." / „Съжалявам. Менелаос ще Ви пише днес." |

**STOP — get the Lead:** illness · allergic reaction · injury · choking · legal · press · a complaint about a team member · discrimination · harassment.
**Reaction, fall, choking: 112 first.**
