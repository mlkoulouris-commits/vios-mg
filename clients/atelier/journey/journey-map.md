## How to read

One table per phase. Columns: code · moment · weight · brand intention (one sentence, with the pillar it serves) · role-in-moment (how a Peer shows up here) · dominant expression dimension · customer target state (her inner sentence, in quotes) · marker. HIGH rows carry fuller text; LOW rows keep the intention to twelve words or fewer, the full text is in `archive/journey-inventory.md`; silent rows carry only code, name and ○, the reason is in `reasoning-note.md`. Venue tags in the moment name: **RK** Rakovski only, **RM** Ring Mall only, **B2B** the partner line; untagged moments run at both counters.

**Weight tiers (33 moments):** HIGH **6** (18 %) · MEDIUM **14** (42 %) · LOW **9** (27 %) · silent **4** (12 %). Shares are of all 33.

**Markers:** ★ signature (the differentiator made visible) · ◉ peak · ⊐ end (two moments, end-1 and end-2, designed as one) · ○ silent.

## The arc

| Phase | Name | Duration | What Elena is doing |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | The walk home | days–weeks online; ~5 sec on the street | sees a real moment online; checks it is real; walks past on Rakovski or through the mall and decides in seconds |
| 2 | The threshold | ~10 sec | steps in; looks for a face; at the mall, joins a queue |
| 3 | The question | 1–3 min | is asked; answers or says "nothing"; chooses, tastes, asks about sugar, alcohol, price, allergens |
| 4 | Marked | ~1 min | watches the scoop; receives a card; pays; or chooses a box for someone |
| 5 | The pause | 5–15 min (RM: 2–5 min standing) | sits if she can; reads the card; eats; is left alone |
| 6 | The door | ~30 sec | leaves; hears her name; is watched out |
| 7 | The card on the desk | days–weeks | keeps the card; posts it; reviews; comes back; reads a letter |
| 8 | The partner table | weeks (B2B); ~10 min for the partner's guest | a venue owner is chosen; the Lab delivers; the card asks the question far from Rakovski |

Peak ◉ **M18 The Note drawn and handed** (phase 4). Signature ★ **M09 "What are we celebrating today?"** (phase 3). End ⊐ **M25 The goodbye, by name + M26 Attention held until she is out** (phase 6, designed as one).

## What passes to Stage 3

### The HIGH moments

| Code | Moment | Why HIGH | Observed? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| M06 | Someone looks up | The threshold thin-slice: the first seconds decide "person or process", which is the persona's whole fear; the brand's own logic puts the threshold on a face, not on a room, because the differentiator "needs no theatre, only people". | no, brand-led |
| M09 | "What are we celebrating today?" ★ | The differentiator made visible; the moment of decision for her (answer or not) and the moment the mall counter is tested. | no, brand-led; the mall unverified (9) |
| M11 | The answer received | Story block 5 lives here: the risk of the generic friendly is answered or confirmed; "nothing" accepted is the value "Notice the moment" in practice. | no, brand-led |
| M18 | The Note drawn and handed ◉ | The peak: the one thing she carries out of the door; peak-end says the visit is remembered by this and by the end. | no, brand-led; who draws, when (28) |
| M25 | The goodbye, by name ⊐ | The end, by default HIGH (peak-end); the only moment after the card where a person speaks to her. | no, brand-led |
| M26 | Attention held until she is out ⊐ | The end's second half: on a counter the end competes with the next arrival in the same seconds; without this the end never happens. | no, brand-led; staffing (47) |

### The silent moments

| Code | Moment | Why silent |
| --- | --- | --- |
| M08 | Reading the case | Private activity: the choice is hers; a flavour named unasked takes it from her (message 4). |
| M10 | The beat after the question | Between two HIGH moments (pattern S2-03): the question needs a second to land; filled, it becomes a slogan. The Bible's own rule: "then a beat of silence". |
| M22 | Reading the card | Private: the card is hers; a comment on which one she drew makes the peak a performance. |
| M23 | Eating, talking | Private activity and the longest phase; duration neglect says it adds nothing to memory and hovering subtracts (message 3). |

### Coherence test

| Question | Yes/No | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Arc specific to this customer? | **yes** | "The walk home" (the street and the decision in seconds, no booking), "The question" as its own phase, "The card on the desk" and "The partner table" are phases a generic gelato arc does not have; a tiramisu bar's arc has no question phase. |
| All moments named? | **yes, with gaps marked** | 33 moments; routine ones (payment, the price, allergens, the chair) mapped per pattern S2-04. The mall counter's pause and seating (M24) and the B2B phase are mapped thin and say so. |
| Real hierarchy? | **yes** | 6 HIGH of 33 (18 %); the end as two moments spends two of them, defended in the Reasoning Note. |
| Every placement from the brand book? | **yes** | Each intention names its message or story block; the coherence table in the Reasoning Note lists them. Two placements rest on facts not yet on file (M18 who draws; M07/M09 at the mall) and say so. |
| Silences read as choices? | **yes** | Four, each with a reason; Stage 3 receives them as instructions. |
