# The Vios branding worksheets, verbatim

Source: Drive › BRANDING RESOURCES (UDMCC1 course set). Ask these questions in these words, in this order. The skill's steps paraphrase them; when the two differ, these win. Worksheet 9 does not exist in the course; numbering jumps 8 → 10.

## Worksheet 1 — Purpose
Aside from commercial intent, why do we do what we do?
- Who or what are we making a commitment to?
- What are we going to do?
- How are we going to do it?
- What resources will we use? How often will we take action?

## Worksheet 2 — Vision
What does our brand aspire to be? Where does it aspire to go? What does it aspire to achieve?

## Worksheet 3 — Mission
What is our brand committed to, in order to achieve our vision of the future?

## Worksheet 4 — Core values
Core values, each with a value statement (a verb or a behaviour, not a single word).

## Worksheet 5 — Audience persona
Demographics: persona name, target group, age, gender, occupation, education, personal income, household income, marital status, children, employees, location.
Psychographics: car model, activities / interests, sports, music, values, clothing brands, social platforms, news sources, entertainment, publications.
Emotional insights: pain points, challenges, needs, wants / desires, before-state emotions, after-state emotions, beliefs (persona quotes).
Personality insights: drive, desires, fears, characteristics; archetype from the twelve (Outlaw, Hero, Caregiver, Creator, Magician, Lover, Jester, Everyman, Ruler, Innocent, Sage, Explorer).

## Worksheet 6 — Competitor audit (spreadsheet)
One sheet per competitor, scored 1–10 per dimension (8+ green, 7 yellow, under 7 red):
- Brand message: tagline / intro message, slogan, underlying core message
- Positioning: claimed difference, value proposition, uniqueness, defensibility, how it is communicated, features / benefits highlighted most
- Personality: defined archetype?, personality description, who it appeals to, voice / language, visual execution
- Brand identity: logo, colours, images, typography, packaging
- Website / UX: design, UX, mobile design and UX, photography style, blog content and frequency, contact methods, support hours
- Reviews
Finish with two lists: Ideas (their strengths worth adopting) and Opportunities (their gaps). Template: `competitor-audit-template.md` beside this file.

## Worksheet 7 — Differentiator
What is the one thing we do differently from our competitors, that adds value to our audience and enhances their experience?
- Experience keywords
- Differentiator (what will we do differently and what will that mean to our audience)

## Worksheet 8 — Positioning statement (template)
WE HELP [target audience] WHO [pain point(s)] TO ACHIEVE / EXPERIENCE [key benefit] UNLIKE [competitive alternative] OUR SOLUTION [differentiator statement]. Then the full statement in one paragraph.

## Worksheet 10 — Brand personality workbook
1. Audience persona: archetype, drive, desires, fears, characteristics, before-state and after-state emotions.
2. Situational desires (what do they want in their current circumstance) and personality desires.
3. Your brand archetype: select primary and secondary from the twelve.
4. Role toward the audience: Guide ("I can guide you"), Inspiration ("You can be great"), Peer ("You are the same as me"), Defender ("I can protect you"), Leader ("We can be…"), Other. Write the role statement.
5. Brand personality expression: physical appearance, dress / style, accessories / distinctions, language / vocabulary, tone of voice, volume / sound, attitudes, beliefs.
6. Mood board: collect images to bring the visual representation of the brand to life.

## Brand personality questionnaire (owner interview)
Ask one at a time, record the answer verbatim.
1. What do you love and why?
2. What do you dislike / hate and why?
3. Where do you bring the most value to your audience?
4. What is the one thing you would change about your industry above all else, and why?
5. Why is your market a great space to be in?
6. What positives and negatives do you believe social media has brought about in our society?
7. What is the purpose of your existence?
8. What is important to you in the way you do business?
9. What does your audience need to be protected against?
10. What do you believe are the most pressing issues in the world today?
11. What are you passionate about?
