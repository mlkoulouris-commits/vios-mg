## Variants folded into moments (not separate codes)

- **Regular** at M5 ("Good to see you again."), M10 ("Pistachio again, or something new today?"), M28.
- **Celebration box buyer** at M9–M11: same welcome, more time, no upsell language; "Who are we celebrating? We'll choose the twelve together."
- **Excited group** at M9: joins the energy, stays calm.
- **Upset guest** at any moment: calm, short, no defence, hand to Menelaos; illness, allergy, injury, staff, legal: escalate, no draft (guardrails 11).
- **To-go order**: M20–M21 skipped; the end (M22–M23) happens at the hand-over of the bag.
- **Ring Mall counter**: same inventory; M6 and M11 under queue pressure; form to confirm with Evo.
