# Vios Guardrails

One place for the lines we don't cross and the numbers we share, so every skill says the same thing. Other Vios skills link here by hard-line number (e.g. "guardrails 6") and keep one sentence each instead of restating.

## Hard lines (any breach = BLOCK in `vios-consistency-qa`, except a true fact worded as pressure, which is FIX per the QA check "Pressure / scarcity"; QA checks are cited by name, never by row number, so the table can be reordered)

1. **No invented scarcity or pressure.** *True, plain, once = allowed; false, unverifiable or pressure = never.* "Menu runs 14–20 Nov" is a fact stated once. "Last chance", "hurry", timers, "only 2 left" that isn't counted, are pressure. A corrected line must not be a softer nudge either.
2. **No fake proof.** Reviews, testimonials and UGC are real, verbatim, with written permission; never composited or incentivised without saying so. Review requests: ask every guest, never reward, never gate on satisfaction. Gifted or paid partnerships labelled on every post.
3. **No fake price anchors.** Only the actual current price. For a reduction, the reference is the lowest price charged in the previous 30 days (EU price-indication rule, transposed in BG/GR; the law covers goods, Vios applies it to menu and set-menu prices too). No other "was" price.
4. **No dark patterns** in booking, sign-up or unsubscribe flows.
5. **Food truth.** Dish, portion, plating, the room and the exterior are shown as they are. AI may enhance a real photo; it never generates food, the room or the street.
6. **AI disclosure.** Any synthetic face or voice: on-screen label from the persona's first frame, platform AI label on where one exists. EU AI Act Art. 50 transparency duties apply from 2 Aug 2026; check the current status of the Digital Omnibus deferral for machine-readable marking before relying on any grace period (status to be confirmed by the account manager before release and re-checked quarterly; until confirmed, treat as "no grace period"). Block when a label that exists is missing. If the scheduling or upload route can't set the platform AI label, the post goes native or is verified after publish; QA logs "label ✓ confirmed live".

   Full disclosure set for any AI or AI-scaled persona: (a) a permanent bio line on every account the persona appears on ("X is our AI host; the food/drinks/room are real"); (b) on-screen label from the first frame + platform AI label; (c) caption hashtag on every persona post (#AIcreated / #AIсъздадено / Greek equivalent); (d) paid partnerships carry dual disclosure, paid label + AI label; (e) every agency, freelancer or voice-actor contract states the disclosure duty and who is liable if it is missed. An AI persona never speaks or signs in a real person's name. When an AI post is wrong: approver pulls it within 2 hours, a named person signs the correction, log it, fix the Bible or prompt; two serious errors in a month pause persona content.
7. **Likeness and voice consent.** Any real person's face or voice (owner, staff, guests, a persona modelled on someone): written, paid where relevant, time-limited consent on file before cloning, filming for ads, or generation. The consent states whether the persona/voice is retired or continues when they leave, and who decides. No minors in frame without a guardian's release.
8. **Alcohol.** Where the venue sells alcohol: 18+ targeting (21+ where platform policy requires), no link to driving, sport, performance or youth, no health claims, per Meta/Google alcohol policy and BG/GR advertising law.
9. **Claims and rights.** Allergen, "organic", "vegan", "gluten-free", "local", "homemade" verified against the fact sheet. Music: licensed or commercial-library only (business accounts lose personal-library audio; unlicensed tracks get muted or removed).
10. **Human sign-off.** Nothing publishes, spends or is sent to a client without a named approver. Skills draft; they never publish.
11. **Escalate, don't draft.** Replies touching illness, allergy or food safety, injury, legal threats, press, a named staff member, discrimination or harassment go to the approver with the facts. A fluent draft here can create liability or read as dismissive.
12. **Data.** Customer lists in ads only with a documented GDPR basis. Client revenue and cover data stays private; sharing is the account manager's decision.

## Behavioural economics, the five-line version

one message · one CTA or none · no pressure device · no persuasion after a "no" · plain words, no superlatives. The full library (System 1 and System 2, the two selves, the seventeen principles from cognitive ease to thin-slicing, the limits of asking customers) is in this plugin: `../../references/section-3-1-behavioural-economics.md` (BE, read by `vios-be-lens`) and `../../references/section-3-2-non-verbal-communication.md` (non-verbal, read by `vios-nonverbal-lens`).

The marketing-side devices (one CTA, the tone ladder neutral/suggestive/directive, restating the plan in confirmations, shareable moments as the success signal) are `vios-be-lens` checks built on the library, not library entries: cite them as checks, and cite principles by their section 3.1 name. The five lines above are enough to run any skill; the references are for the lenses and the journey stages.

## Evidence and house doctrine

The practice's rules come from three kinds of source, and every lens, skill and client document says which kind it is leaning on, so that a client, a designer or a new team member can tell a finding from a choice:

- **Evidence**: findings that have held up across studies and that the practice would defend to a sceptic. Peak-end and duration neglect, the two selves, cognitive ease, loss aversion and the reference point, choice overload, defaults. Cite the section 3.1 entry; where a client asks, the primary study is named in that entry.
- **Contested**: ideas the practice uses as working heuristics because they produce good design decisions, while the science behind them is disputed or has failed to replicate in part. Thin-slicing and first-impression speed, facial expression as a universal readout of emotion (the basis of the non-verbal lens), priming effects, most of the "persuasion" literature. Use them as design heuristics; never present them to a client as proven mechanisms, and never build a measurement claim on them.
- **House doctrine**: the practice's own choices, held because of who the brand wants to be, not because a study says so. The refusal of pressure devices, no persuasion after a "no", plain words, silent moments, the filter test, the three-to-seven HIGH rule, the 70/30 archetype split, the tone ladder, the reporting thresholds (n ≥ 30, 50 results per cell). These are commitments. A client may be told they cost click-through; they are not negotiated per post.

When a skill states a mechanism ("viewers read the face before the words"), it is in the second group unless the library entry says otherwise. When a number is quoted, it is in the third group unless a dated source is beside it.

## Messages and micro-stories (owned here)

The brand book's message architecture (VIOS Practice, Stage 1 §2.1.2.10–11) is the source every piece of copy, video, calendar slot and reply draws from. It has three layers, and `brand-book.md` carries them per client:

1. **Core messages.** Primary (why should I care: audience, pain, key benefit, alternative, differentiator) and secondary (why it matters: purpose, vision, mission, values). Each is a statement of what the guest should understand, never finished copy. **Integrity rule:** a piece reinforces at least one core message and contradicts none; otherwise it does not belong.
2. **Story framework.** Seven blocks, guest as hero, brand as guide: existing world · obstacle · call to action · meeting the guide · the challenge · the transformation · the new world. The brand is never the hero.
3. **Journey.** The guest's touchpoints in order, online and in the venue, with the guest's state and tension at each, the designed moment to show, who owns it (marketing or floor), one touchpoint marked as the **peak**, one as the **signature** (the differentiator made visible; usually a different moment from the peak) and the **end** (VIOS Practice Stages 2–3; `vios-journey:vios-journey-map` produces it, the account manager fills the table by hand until a map exists).

   Canonical touchpoint names, so every skill says the same thing: discovery · profile check · booking · reminder · arrival · ordering · making · the evening · departure · after-visit · review. A client may drop or rename, never invent a twelfth without saying so. Walk-in retail drops booking and reminder; a B2B line adds "partner table" and "delivery" as named touchpoints; a gallery or event venue adds "the exhibition" or "the event" as its own phase. A full journey map (`vios-journey:vios-journey-map`, which owns the moment count and the HIGH/MEDIUM/LOW rules) has many more moments than touchpoints; the touchpoint table maps each touchpoint to its moment codes, it does not replace the map.
4. **Micro-message blocks.** One per journey touchpoint. Each states the guest's state and tension, the understanding to create, verbal and non-verbal expression, success signal and failure modes.

**Micro-story rule.** Every post, story, caption, script, ad, reply and booking message is a micro-instance of the frame: it names its anchor core message and the story block it plays (most social content sits in blocks 1–4 and 6; departure and after-visit copy in 7). Touchpoint copy is written from the matching micro-message block's "understanding to create" and "verbal expression", and is checked against its failure modes. One piece plays one block; the arc is covered across the month (`vios-content-calendar`), never inside one post. A micro-story shows the hero's moment; it does not narrate the brand's story.

## Changing the method (owned here)

The skills are the method; a change to a skill is a change to the practice. Three rules:

1. **Doctrine changes go through the owner.** A hard line, a shared number, a weighting rule, a tier in the evidence table or a stage's moves changes only on the owner's explicit word, in a commit whose message names the change, with a line in the dry-run or harvest note so the manuscript's next version carries it. Wording, examples and references can change without that.
2. **One writer at a time** in the method repository. Before any edit or package, check that no other session is working in it; two sessions editing skills in one afternoon is how a method drifts without anyone deciding.
3. **Evals before a package.** The skills with an `evals/` folder (the list in `docs/evals.md`) carry `evals/cases.json`; a package is cut only after a run that passes, logged in `evals/runs.md`. `release.py check` guards the structure, a session runs the cases.

## Shared numbers (owned here)

- **Reporting n**: show *n* beside every percentage. If either side is < 30, show raw numbers and say "too small to call". An owner will act on "doubled" when it's 3 vs 6.
- **Early reads**: ad-cell decisions (the `vios-ads-audit` winner rule) are early reads that decide whether to keep spending, which is reversible. Reports and client messages still use n ≥ 30 per side; an early read with n < 30 per side is shown as raw numbers, headed "early read, too small to call", never as a percentage lift.
- **Benchmarks**: the client's own history. Industry numbers only when sourced and dated. Never invented, including posting times.
- **The venue's money**: definitions of revenue, covers, check, mix, daypart, labour %, COGS %, the quadrant and the cost of a moment, and which Memento page each comes from, are in `../../references/numbers-page.md`; every skill that quotes one of them uses that definition.
- **Worked examples**: the client behind each house form or example a general skill shows is named only in `plugin/vios-core/references/worked-examples.md` (entries E1 …); a general skill never carries a client name inline.
- **Outcome KPIs** (the proposal's "What we aim to move" table, `status.json` › `kpis`): at most four, each with a monthly source and the baseline page's figure as "today"; a target is the founder's number or one labelled step, never a guarantee; reported every month against the baseline with *n*; the practice claims **contribution**, never causation, unless an offer-day lift against the same-weekday baseline supports it, and says "not provable" when it is not.
- Minimum sample, learning phase, planned-test protection and fatigue thresholds: `vios-ads-audit`.

## Client file convention

Every client has a folder (`[client]/`) with:

| File | Holds |
| --- | --- |
| `diagnosis.md` | Output of `vios-brand-diagnosis`: purpose, audience persona, positioning, archetype, Brand Role |
| `persona-bible.md` | From `vios-persona-builder`; front page names the **approver**, the **address register** (BG ти/Вие, GR εσύ/εσείς, per channel; default informal on social, formal in booking messages), **emoji policy**, **alcohol: yes/no**, **AI disclosure rule**, **version number and next review date**; sections include is/is-not pairs, tone sliders, word rules, hard moments, the ten-line checklist, tone by guest mood, objective and KPIs, error protocol and pilot (the brand book's Part 5 is assembled from these) |
| `brand-book.md` | Colours, type, logo rules, image style (or a link to the design system); **Messages and story**: core messages (primary + secondary), the seven story blocks, the **journey** (touchpoints in order, guest state, designed moment, owner, peak), the micro-message blocks per touchpoint, with the brand book version they come from |
| `fact-sheet.md` | Hours, address, prices, menu names, allergens, claims we may make, booking channels, venue rhythm (open days, slow days, peak days, events) |
| `accounts.md` | Meta ad account / page / pixel ids, Google Ads customer id, Metricool brand id, POS and booking system |
| `ads-plan.md` | Monthly budget by cold/warm/guest, target cost per booking, offline attribution method, planned test dates, accepted Ad Strength notes (e.g. venue-name pin) |
| `character-sheet/` | Locked reference stills, expression palette stills, voice spec |
| `consents/` | Persona-source, staff, guest and minor-guardian releases; UGC permissions; each with date and expiry |
| `calendar-sources.md` | Where holiday / name-day / event dates come from (dated sources, never memory) |
| `qa-log.csv` | `date, client, asset, platform, language, attempt, verdict, failed_checks (joined with ;), approver, signed_off_by, signed_off_at` — scheduling and upload require `signed_off_at` filled |
| `data/YYYY-MM/` | Raw pulls with source and pulled-at in the filename |
| `data/review-mining.md` · `data/guest-interviews.md` · `data/mystery-visit-YYYY-MM-DD.md` | Guest evidence (templates in `templates/client/data/`): the last 200 reviews coded into themes with counts; 8–10 fixed-question interviews, anonymised summary only (consents and recordings in Drive); one scored unannounced visit on the house journey frame. `vios-brand-diagnosis` step 4 does not pass without at least the review mining; the map's Move 2b and the baseline read them too. Guest names never in git; hard-line 11 topics counted, never quoted |

| `data/baseline-YYYY-MM.md` | The month-0 measurement (`vios-engage:vios-engagement-setup` step 7, template `templates/client/data/baseline.md`): venue, bookings, reviews, social, paid, each row with source and n, the three months before the practice started; made once, never recomputed; every monthly report carries a "Baseline" column from it; `status.json` › `baseline` says measured or partial |
| `journey/` | Stage 2 and 3 outputs (`vios-journey`): `intake-stage-2.md`, `observation.md` (shadowed services and review read, dated; the re-weighting date), `journey-map.md`, `reasoning-note.md`, `handoff-to-design.md`, `design/` (pages, choreography, recovery, briefs) |
| `training/` | Stage 4 outputs (`vios-journey:vios-training-programme`): architecture, registers, cadence calendar, staff and management stacks, handoffs; **proof**: `certification.csv` (one row per person per role card, 10 checks, manager signs; initials only), `behaviour-kpis.md` (two counted behaviours per HIGH moment, four weekly counts after go-live), the thirty-day register audit as a `data/mystery-visit-*.md` sheet with its score in `status.json` › stages › 4 Training › `audit` |
| `open-facts.md` | The engagement's chase list: every "to confirm" from any stage as a row (fact · what it blocks · who holds it · asked on · due); worked by the account manager |
| `archive/` | Stage 1–4 working documents that stay out of the deliverable: filled worksheets, `deep-interrogation.md` (the interrogation answers, from `vios-name-and-tagline`), `naming.md` (long list, scores), `filter-test-batches.md` (the pass/fail batches from `vios-filter-test`), `harvest-stage-1.md` |
| `brand-book-vN.md` | The assembled brand book per edition (`vios-brand-book`); `brand-book.md` stays the live summary the operating skills read |

Skills' "Load first" sections point here. If a file is missing, say so and use the stand-in below; don't improvise.
