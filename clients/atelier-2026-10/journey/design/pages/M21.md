# M21 · STANDARD · The celebration board

In-room · The ten minutes (phase 5) · optional · The room · owner: floor · dominant dimensions: accessories, beliefs

## CURRENT STATE

If the board exists (v3 §7.2, "optional"; **to confirm**), she may add her moment. "Mine can go up there too."

## DO

1. A board in the room with guests' moments in their own hand, cards and a pencil beside it; nothing printed by the brand on it except the question at the top.
2. Never prompted. No "would you like to add yours?"; the board invites by existing.
3. Only what guests wrote themselves; nothing staged by the team; names only if the guest wrote them.
4. Curated weekly: full board, old cards archived (kept, not binned); anything unkind or naming a third person removed the same day.
5. A photo of the board for social only with the writers' permission or with no name legible (guardrails 2, 12).

## DON'T

- A prompt, a prize, a hashtag on the board; team-written filler cards; a board left to fade.

**Success metric:** If the board exists: cards added per week (raw count); nothing on it written by staff. Filter: the printed question only, **pass**.
