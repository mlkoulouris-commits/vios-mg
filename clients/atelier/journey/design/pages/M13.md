# M13 · STANDARD · The Aperitivo line

In-room · Choosing together (phase 3) · ~5 sec · Counter · owner: floor · dominant dimension: language

## CURRENT STATE

She picks limoncello sorbet, prosecco and peach, or tiramisu gelato. "They told me straight."

## DO

1. Named as alcoholic in one plain line before the spoon: "That one has limoncello in it." / „В този има лимончело." Once; no joke, no wink.
2. The legal serving age and the ID practice for alcoholic gelato: **CONFIRM** with Menelaos before this page is locked; until then, no Aperitivo taste or sale to anyone who looks under 18, and the line "That line is 18 and over." if asked why (schedule, decision 11; the age itself is the legal fact to confirm, not a brand choice).
3. Never with a child's order, never suggested to a visibly pregnant guest, never as the anchor (M11).
4. In content: 18+ targeting on any paid post featuring it; no link to driving, sport, youth or health (guardrails 8; Bible front page).

## DON'T

- "It's basically nothing", "just a bit of booze"; selling it as a treat; "you deserve a drink".
- Serving it without the line.

**Success metric:** On sampled Aperitivo orders, the alcohol line is said before the taste; no Aperitivo in a child's order. Filter: a fact, **pass**.
