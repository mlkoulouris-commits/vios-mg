# ONE Gallery — Brand Book, quick reference

Source: Brand Book v2.2 (brand-book-v2.2.pdf, 33 pages). This file is the lookup; the PDF is the authority.

## Non-negotiables
- Art · Coffee · Aperitivo as one integrated identity. Anything that reads "gallery only" or "café only" is a brand violation.
- The exhibition leads content; the bar follows (§11.5).
- Two voices, one character: the team's "we" for hospitality; the curator's (Desi's) signed voice for the art (§5.4, §11.1).
- The Gallery Series is an untranslated proper noun in every language.
