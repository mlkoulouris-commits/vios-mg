# M03 · HIGH IMPACT · Weighing the trip

Digital · minutes · Phase 1, deciding · Self-led · Owner: marketing

**Dominant inputs:** brand (Worth the trip; the 2 km as filter, book § 1.5) · BE (the trip as a cost) · concept (address, directions, open fact 1) · persona.

## CURRENT STATE
They like it; the question is the distance. Next: booking (M04).

## DO
1. Website, GBP and booking page state the distance plainly: 2 km from the centre, Stochna Gara.
2. How to get there, one line each: taxi, tram or bus, parking (CONFIRM with the address).
3. Never apologise for the distance.

## SAY
EN "2 km from the centre, in Stochna Gara." · BG „На 2 км от центъра, в Сточна гара.“

## DON'T
- No "easy to reach!" if it isn't; no "hidden gem".
- No discount to make up for the trip.

## BEHAVIORAL ECONOMICS
BE 12 loss aversion: the trip is felt as a cost; naming it plainly makes it a choice (BE 9 framing: "everyone here came on purpose").

## RECOVERY
- Directions wrong or missing → fixed the same day. Open fact 1 (address) blocks this page.

**State arc:** Is it worth the trip? → Plain distance, plain directions → Everyone there came on purpose.
**Success metric:** booking page views → bookings (n shown).
**Target emotion:** decided. **Brand pillar:** Worth the trip.
