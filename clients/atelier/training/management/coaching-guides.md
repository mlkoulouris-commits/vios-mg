# Coaching guides — Atelier del Gelato

**Draft — book edition v1.0 unsigned (open-facts 39); observation move not held.**

For Menelaos and any shift lead, in weekly one-to-one coaching (15 minutes per team member, `cadence-calendar.md` § 1). Operational register. The topic follows the four-week cycle; a gap found in the floor audit overrides the cycle for that person.

## 0. The manager-as-coach standard (S4-07)

**The standard.** Whoever leads the counter (Menelaos; any shift lead he names, 62) is a coach first. The job is to develop the people behind the counter so that the ritual holds when the lead is not there.

**How it is evaluated.** At the monthly design review (ops brief § 8), Evo reviews the lead on two things weighed equally: (1) the floor: the sampled-hour results by counter (x of n), recoveries logged; (2) the team: team members signed off against the checklist (x of n), coaching sessions held (x of n planned), audit lines moved from "fix" to "keep". A lead who runs a good floor and coaches nobody has met half the standard. Menelaos evaluates any shift lead the same way. **Evo's agreement to this standard is open (66);** without it the standard is a statement, and the S4-07 failure mode (coaching decays to cover counts) is the expected result.

**What the coach does not do.** Coach in front of a guest. Coach a named team member at the pre-shift. Use audit numbers for blame (ops brief § 7: diagnostic, not punitive).

## 1. Coaching language (used in every topic)

- **Opener — observation, impact, question.** "I noticed the question came after the scoop twice in the golden hour. The impact is that she'd already started her order before she was asked. What was going on?"
- **Listen to the answer to the end.** The same rule as M11.
- **One thing.** One behaviour per session, named in floor words from the role card.
- **Closer — practise it once.** "Let's run that moment again, once." At the counter, before or after hours, or in a quiet band with the Second covering.
- **Follow-up.** Book the next sampled hour on that behaviour; note it on the coaching log (date, behaviour, next check).

## 2. Topic: Moment execution (week 1)

**What it covers:** the look (M06), the question (M09), the beat (M10), the answer (M11), the card (M18), the goodbye (M25), the eyes to the door (M26).

**Observe (from the floor audit):**

| Behaviour | Seen as |
| --- | --- |
| Eyes up within 3 s | look before any word; not from the till |
| The question whole | the exact words; before the scoop; not "Celebrating?" |
| The beat | no words between question and answer |
| No push after "nothing" | zero second questions |
| Reaction cap | no "Wow!", no clapping; smile not above 3 of 5 |
| The card | blind draw; edges; before the till; not in the bag |
| The goodbye | face to her, hands still; not to the till or the newcomer |
| Eyes to the door | the next question does not start before she has turned |

**Common drifts and the language:**

- *An improvised greeting formula fills the gap where no script exists* (the risk of a threshold locked as a behaviour, not a line). "What did you say when she walked in? … Try just the look and 'Hi'. Let the look do it."
- *The question said as a slogan.* "Say it to me as if you were asking a friend what time it is." Then once more at normal pace.
- *The beat filled.* "After you ask, count one, two in your head. Say nothing. Let's do ten in a row."
- *The card glanced at or chosen.* "Hand in the jar, eyes on me. Don't look down."

**Follow-up:** the next week's sampled hour checks the one behaviour named.

## 3. Topic: Recovery handling (week 2)

**What it covers:** the recovery character (one sorry, the fix, back to her; no compensation in the moment), the six HIGH recoveries, the scenarios, R-ESC.

**Observe:** recoveries logged since the last session; how the team member talks about them. Ask her to walk one: what was the trigger, what did she do, what did she say.

**Common drifts and the language:**

- *Over-apology.* "How many times did you say sorry? … One is the rule. Then the fix."
- *A freebie in the moment.* "I know it felt right. The rule is that I decide later, privately. Next time: the line, and tell me."
- *Drafting or explaining on an escalation topic.* "That one's mine. Stop the order, get me, say nothing else. You did the hard part by noticing."
- *Blaming the Lab, the mall or a colleague in front of the guest.* "What could she hear?"

**Practise:** one scenario from `recovery.md`, chosen by the coach, run once.

## 4. Topic: Choreography (week 3)

**What it covers:** one face for the ritual (choreography 1.1), the end with the next guest (1.2), where the team stands (1.5), the handoffs H1, H2, H8, R-ALONE.

**Observe:** with two on, who asked and who handed the card; whether the Second re-greeted or said "anything else?" at the till; both backs to the door; the handover note filled.

**Common drifts and the language:**

- *The Second takes the card or asks the question for the Counter's guest.* "Who asked her? Then the card is theirs. You hold the till."
- *Both at the back during a quiet spell.* "Who was facing the door at 15:10?"
- *R-ALONE applied backwards (the goodbye cut, the watch kept).* "Under a rush, what goes first?" The answer is the watch to the door.

**Practise:** the welcome-symmetry drill, both variants (two on, one alone).

## 5. Topic: Brand awareness (week 4)

**What it covers:** the two sentences (wall chart, 70), the four values in their operating form, the filter test, the never-words, the knowledge block (the menu, the four origins, what may and may not be said).

**Observe:** ask, don't test. "What would you say if a guest asks why we ask the question?" (The answer is not an explanation of the ritual to the guest; M09 says don't explain it. It is the team member's own understanding.) "Is 'treat yourself' talking to the order?"

**Common drifts and the language:**

- *A claim creeping in* ("it's healthy", "gluten-free"). "What does the fact sheet let us say? … Then that's all we say."
- *An origin for a flavour that has none on file.* "Which four have an origin we say?"
- *Superlatives.* Run the filter test on the line together.

**Practise:** the team member sorts five lines the coach says, pass or fail, with the reason.

## 6. When coaching is not working

A behaviour in the "fix" band for three audits running after coaching: the coach re-runs the session block from the Journey Training Plan on that moment, one to one, and re-signs that line on the checklist. Twice more without change: Menelaos and Evo discuss it as a fit question (value "Bring the attitude": hire for attitude, train skill), never at the counter and never with the team.
