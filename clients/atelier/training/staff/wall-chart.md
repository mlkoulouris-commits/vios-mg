## Who we are, in two sentences

**Every order opens with a question and closes with a card drawn by chance.**
**We look up first, take any answer, and never hurry or sell.**

## The test

**Is this talking to the order?** Hurrying her? Selling to her? Talking to no one in particular? → Don't say it.

**„Говорим ли на поръчката?"** — бързаме ли, продаваме ли, говорим ли на никого? [the team confirms, open-facts 33]

## Always

- **Look first. Then speak.**
- **You asked. You hand the card. You say goodbye.**
- **Say it. Stop. Then move.**
- **Never both backs to the door.**
- **One sorry. Fix it. Back to her. Never a freebie.**
- **Not on the matrix? Don't guess.**
- **Illness, reaction, injury, legal, press, a staff complaint: stop, get the Lead. 112 first if it's safety.**

**Never say:** Next · Anything else? · bestseller · everyone takes it · treat yourself · last chance · sugar-free · Baci
