**The counter will call you with a guest's exact question.** Allergens, gluten, nuts, what's in a flavour.

- **Answer from the written matrix. Exactly. Never "should be fine".** [the matrix is not on file yet: open-facts 13]
- **Not sure? Say "not sure".** The counter won't serve it. That's the right answer.
- **How fast, and how they reach you:** ______ (open-facts 69)

**"Made this morning" is said at the counter.** It has to be true. Anything not made today: tell the Lead before open.

**A flavour runs out in the day?** The counter says so once. What replaces it, and how fast: ______ (open-facts 56)

**Cards.** The jar at each counter needs a reserve. Low stock, reprint, who orders: ______ (open-facts 53)

**Deliveries to partners (M32):**

- In the morning, at the agreed hour.
- **The story cards on top.** Not inside, not under the invoice.
- Hand-over to a named person:

  > "Today's delivery. The cards are on top." / „Днешната доставка. Картичките са отгоре." [to sign; the story cards: open-facts 48]

- Late, short or warm? Tell the partner before you arrive, plainly, with the new time. Tell Menelaos.
- Note on the delivery log: were the cards on the tables last visit?

**Never:** cards left in the box or the van · a delivery without cards because "they have some" · an upsell at the door · a claim the fact sheet doesn't carry (gluten-free until open-facts 13).

**Illness, a reaction, a complaint about a delivery that touches food safety: no reply. Menelaos, now, with the facts.**
