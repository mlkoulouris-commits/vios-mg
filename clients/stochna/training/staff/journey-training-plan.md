## Five principles

1. **Foundations before moments.** Learn the three rules once; every moment uses them.
2. **Say it, then walk it.** Words first, then the words with the body, the distance and the step back, in the real room.
3. **The step back is the lesson.** Spend as long on what not to do as on what to do.
4. **Drill the counter-intuitive.** The instinct to help, check in and explain is trained out.
5. **Floor-ready is a checklist,** not a feeling (`sign-off.md`).

## Session 1 — Foundations (60 min, always first, alone)

**What Stochna is, in two sentences:** We brew the beer and cook the food here, made for each other. People come with friends, stay, and come back with different friends.

**Three rules:**
1. **Nothing is asked of the guest.** No review, rating, card, survey, ever.
2. **Offer once.** A no is final until they ask.
3. **Nobody stops working to greet.** Look up, nod, keep going.

**Five continuities:** offer once · step back after every line · same plain words online and on the floor · nobody stops working to greet · nothing asked. (`choreography.md` § 1)

**The filter test:** "Would someone who never explains themselves say or do this at their own table?"

**Words we use / never use:** brew, cook, pour, table, friends, here, proper · never: amazing, best, artisan, curated, "experience", "enjoy!", "welcome to our…".

## Session 2 — The six HIGH moments (90 min)

Each: what it is → the line → the body and the distance → the step back. Practise in the room, before service.

| Moment | The line (BG / EN) | The body | The step back |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| M07 The door | — (no line) | eyes within 10 s, a nod | keep working |
| M15 The order | „[Бирата] върви добре с [ястието].“ / "The [beer] goes well with the [plate]." · „Ако не сте сигурни, ще ви налея нещо.“ / "If you're not sure, I'll pour you something." | standing at the table end | after the order is repeated back |
| M17 Beer and bread ★ | „Бирата е варена тук. Хлябът е изпечен тук…“ (or the fallback, until open fact 6) | place, one line to the table | gone in a beat; no waiting for a reaction |
| M24 The next round ◉ | „Има една по-тъмна, която върви с това, което идва. Искате ли да я опитате?“ · after a no: „Разбира се.“ | side approach, arm's length | immediately; no second offer |
| M30 The bill ⊐ | „Разбира се.“ / "Of course." | bill at the centre | turn away within two seconds |
| M31 The farewell ⊐ | „До следващия път.“ / "See you next time." | same as M07: look up, nod | back to work |

**Rehearse M30 and M31 together** (the end pair), then M07 and M31 back to back (welcome symmetry).

## Session 4 — Standards, recovery, the whole arc (90 min)

**Standards:** water before they sit (M11) · the making in view, never restaged (M20) · read your tables from your station (M23) · the room's sound is one person's job per shift (M26) · bathroom clean every hour (M29).

**Recovery** (from the playbook): R-FULL · R-WRONG · R-UPSET · R-OVER · **R-ALLERGY and R-ESC: not yours, get the manager.** Never give anything free; the manager decides later.

**The arc walked once,** door to door, narrated by the trainer, the trainee as the guest.

Shadow shifts start only after this session: two, on different nights.

## Session 5 — Silences and drills (60 min, last, never skipped)

The six silences: coats (M10) · reading the menu (M13) · the wait for the pour (M16) · the first sip (M18) · the conversation (M22) · the walk out (M32).

**Three drills:**
1. **Step-back drill:** say the line, step back 2–3 m, no glance back. The glance is the tell.
2. **Silent-moment drill:** a seated stand-in, a clock, 2 minutes after M17 with no approach. It should feel uncomfortable before it feels normal.
3. **Welcome-symmetry drill:** M07 and M31 back to back, same posture.

## Knowledge you must hold

- This menu edition: every beer, every plate, the pairing anchor (CONFIRM, open fact 6). Refreshed with each menu change.
- What is brewed and baked here, in one plain sentence each (only confirmed facts).
- Who knows more: the brewer, the kitchen lead. Defer rule: "I'll ask the brewer."
- Allergens: never from memory; the kitchen lead or the manager, from the sheet (open fact 26).
- Prices: on the menu; never quote a "was" price.
- **The AI host:** if a guest asks about the host they saw online: „Да, [Име] е нашият AI домакин онлайн. Бирата, храната и залата са истински.“ / "Yes, [Name] is our AI host online. The beer, food and room are real." (guardrails 6)

## Ongoing — pre-shift (15–20 min, daily)

Tonight's anchor and paired beers · bookings and big tables · who covers the door · notes from the last shift · **one role-play** of a locked moment, rotating (M07 → M15 → M17 → M24 → M30/M31 → a recovery).
