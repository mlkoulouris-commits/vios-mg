## CURRENT STATE

She has paid, with the card in her hand (M18, M19). She looks round for somewhere to stand or sit, or hovers by the counter.

## DO

1. Only if she lingers or looks round: offer the chair once, lightly, with an open hand toward it.
2. If she is clearly leaving: nothing; M25.
3. A chair free and clean before she needs it (M05 standard).
