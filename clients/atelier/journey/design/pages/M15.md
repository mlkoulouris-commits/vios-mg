# M15 · CRITICAL IMPACT · The note from the jar ◉

In-room · Marking it (phase 4) · ~15 sec · Counter, hand to hand · **◉ peak**

Dominant inputs: **brand** and **non-verbal**; BE present (peak-end, endowment, affect); concept carries the objects (the jar, the 25-card set, the station, the box).

Two latitudes the handoff left open are decided here and need Menelaos's confirmation: **the team member draws**, not the guest; **the Note is handed before payment**, with the order. Reasons in the BE block and the footer.

## CURRENT STATE

She has answered the question or declined (M9), chosen with the team member (M10–M11), and watched the cup filled and the flavour written on it, or the tiramisu boxed (M14). Her state: "I can take my time" moving to "This is for me." Next: payment (M16), packing if to-go (M17), then the silence in which she reads the card (M18). This is the brand's last act before the memory forms.

## DO

1. The same team member who served her. The order is complete in her sight (cup filled, or box closed) before anything else happens.
2. The jar: glass, open-topped, on the station on her side of the sightline, the handwriting face of the cards visible. The team member reaches in without looking into the jar, takes one card, does not read it.
3. The card goes into her hand: carried the short distance deliberately, offered palm up with the card resting on the fingers, released when she takes it. Not laid on the counter, not slipped into the bag, not tucked under the lid.
4. As the card passes, the locked line. Gaze: to her hands as the card passes, then up to her eyes for the end of the line.
5. Release; half a step back; hands at rest. Eyes come off her, deliberately, to the next action (the till or the bag). She reads it unwatched (M18).
6. If she said "nothing" at M9: the same card, the same line, the same gaze. Nothing added.
7. Then payment (M16): the price, plainly. The card is already hers when money changes hands.
8. Sizes and formats: single tiramisu and gelato, the Note in her hand. Sharing box the same. Celebration box (12): the larger celebration note if it is printed (**to confirm**; v3 §7.4), otherwise a standard Note; in her hand, not in the box. Only B2B deliveries (M30) and boxes bought to give away carry the card inside the box, and then the team member says so: "The note's inside, for whoever opens it." (filter: pass).
9. The room: one jar per station, including Ring Mall; refilled before it drops below a third, so the draw always looks like a draw; a reserve of cards under each station; warm directed light on the counter; the counter quiet enough for a line said at low volume.

## SAY

**LOCKED** — every order, every atelier:

> EN: "Here's a little note for your moment."
> BG: „Ето една бележка за твоя момент."

Registers:

| Register | Line |
| --- | --- |
| Default | The locked line, once per card |
| Group, several orders | The line once per hand-over, to the person receiving |
| After "nothing" at M9 | The locked line, unchanged |
| Box to give away | The locked line, then: "The note's inside, for whoever opens it." |
| First-timer who asks "what is this?" | "A note for today. It goes with every order." |
| Guest who asks to pick | "They're drawn by chance. This one's yours." |
| B2B pickup at the Lab | No line; story cards in the case (M30) |

Filter test (*Is this processing the guest?*): addressed to her, no hurry, nothing sold. All lines **pass**. Register ти / warm "you".

## DON'T

- Don't choose the card to fit her answer. Chance is the design; a chosen card is a message from the brand, and the brand is not the hero.
- Don't look into the jar while drawing; don't read the card first; don't read it aloud; don't ask which one she got.
- Don't hand it with the receipt or the change; don't put it in the bag unspoken; don't lay it on the counter.
- Don't announce it as a game: no "lucky dip", "pick one", "let's see what you get".
- Don't watch her read it; don't comment on it ("oh, that's a good one").
- Don't skip it for "nothing to celebrate", a child's order, a hurried guest, a regular "who has lots already".
- Don't let the jar run low or stand behind the till out of sight.
- No flourish, no exclamation energy; the card is passed with a still face.

## NON-VERBAL

- **Geometry** (NV A.3, A.4): across the counter at level eyes; the hand extends to her side of the counter edge; after release, a half step back and a side-step toward the till, never a turn of the back.
- **Eyes** (NV C.2): her hands as the card passes, her eyes for the end of the line; then released to the next action. Not held while she reads.
- **Face**: still, small smile, intensity 2 (Bible §9, "Handing the Note: sincerity").
- **Hands and handling** (NV D.3): the draw unhurried and unseen by the drawer; the card carried deliberately; offered on open fingers, not pinched and thrust.
- **Haptics** (NV D.1): the card stock and the handwriting face are the brand in her hand; for one second the card is the only thing she is holding.
- **Tone** (NV B.1): calm, light, sincere; a notch quieter than the question; said as a note, not an announcement.
- **Pace** (NV B.2): about two seconds for the line; no rush into the price.
- **Silence** (NV B.4): after the line, nothing. M18 begins when her eyes drop to the card.
- **Timing** (NV E.3): after the order is complete, before money; never during scooping, never with the receipt.

## BEHAVIORAL ECONOMICS

- **Peak-end and duration neglect** (BE 7): the remembering self samples the peak; the design places the brand's last act immediately before the private moment in which the surprise lands. Whether she sits ten minutes or leaves at once, this is what she keeps.
- **Endowment effect** (BE 12): the card goes into her hand and is hers from that second; it leaves with her and sits on her desk (M24). The brand never takes it back, never asks to photograph it, never asks her to post it.
- **Affect heuristic** (BE 5): the surprise colours the first taste (M19) and the farewell. The library's failure mode governs the restraint: affect engineered with a flourish reads as manufactured, so the card is passed still, not presented.
- **Framing** (BE 9): "for your moment" frames the card as hers, about her day; the card itself carries no offer, no logo-forward, no call to action (check the print set).
- **Cognitive ease** (BE 1): chance removes the one decision the brand could make (which card) and gives her none to make; the moment costs nothing.
- **Why the team member draws**: a guest's hand makes it a game to perform and slows the counter; a blind draw by the team member keeps chance visible and keeps the Peer geometry (hand to hand). **Why before payment**: the ritual "ends with a Note" (v3 §2.3); if the till is the last exchange, the peak moves to the tasting and the card becomes packaging (Reasoning Note §7).
- **Decision here**: none for her.

## RECOVERY
