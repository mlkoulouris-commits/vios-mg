# Journey inventory — Stochna Kitchen Brewery (band 2, Move 2)

Date 2026-11-01 · Brand Book V2 (23 Feb 2026) · the full inventory behind `journey/journey-map.md`. Every moment in sequence with the customer's action, the brand's action and what is at stake. Weight and placement live in the map; the reasons in `journey/reasoning-note.md`. No weight or judgement here.

Flags: **conditional** = happens at some tables, not every evening; **standing** = runs across the phase rather than at one point in time. Both are mapped so Stage 3 designs them (pattern S2-04).

## Phase 1 — Deciding (days to hours before)

| Code | Moment | Customer does | Brand does | At stake if done badly |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| M01 | Hearing of it | Hears "you have to try this place" from a friend, or sees one post, while choosing where to take friends | Shows what is happening: a full table mid-evening, beer and food together, the making behind it; states, never sells | The place reads as a product or a scene; the Social Seeker scrolls past |
| M02 | Checking the profile | Thin-slices the Instagram grid and the Google profile: brewery, restaurant or scene? | Keeps the grid and the profile one system: people at tables, hands making, no product shots, no staff portraits (§ 6.5) | The two halves look like two businesses; "brewery with snacks" |
| M03 | Weighing the trip | Asks whether 2 km into an industrial zone is worth it for the group | Names the distance plainly and never apologises for it (§ 1.5); says who comes: people who came on purpose | The distance reads as a drawback to overcome instead of a filter |
| M04 | Booking the table | Books for the group at book.stochna.com (Google reserve the largest route), in BG or EN | Restates date, time, number of people plainly, formal register, no pressure device, no upsell; attribution link unchanged | Pressure language; a group that cannot tell whether there is room for them |
| M05 | The reminder | Checks the plan the day before | Repeats the plan, one warm line, how to change it; nothing sold | A reminder that reads like a system, or a no-show from an unclear plan |

## Phase 3 — The threshold (about 30 sec to 3 min)

| Code | Moment | Customer does | Brand does | At stake if done badly |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| M07 | Crossing the threshold | Steps in; scans for cues about what kind of place this is; checks the trip was worth it | The room does the work: smell of bread dough and hops, kitchen and brewery in view, people already having a good time; whoever is nearest nods (§ 4.2, block 1) | The snap judgement anchors on empty, over-designed or uncertain; everything after is read against it |
| M08 | The greeting | Waits to be acknowledged; may give a booking name | One practical, warm line without ceremony ("First time? Good. Sit anywhere…"), same for first-timer and regular; the booking name after the line, not inside it | Ceremony, a philosophy tour, or a rushed "do you have a reservation?" |
| M09 | Shown the table | Finds the table for the group, or picks one | Points to the table laid for sharing; walk-ins: "sit anywhere"; does not escort or narrate | A table that does not fit the group; the table as a transaction slot |
| M10 | Coats and settling | Hangs coats, chooses seats, looks around | Nothing: hooks and room are already there | Staff hovering during the settle; the room never gets its first minute |

## Phase 4 — The first round (10–25 min)

| Code | Moment | Customer does | Brand does | At stake if done badly |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| M11 | Water down | Sits; may want water | Water and glasses on the table without asking; no ceremony | A table that has to ask for water; a presentation of water |
| M12 | The menu arrives | Receives the menu; registers its length | Hands over one short, legible page; leaves the table to read it; does not explain why it is short | The menu feels incomplete, or the short list is explained as a philosophy |
| M13 | Reading the menu | Reads, talks, compares | Nothing | Staff returning before the table has decided |
| M14 | A constraint questioned (conditional) | Asks about Wi-Fi, a missing dish, why only six items | Holds the line calmly, acknowledges without apologising, offers an alternative where one exists, no philosophy (§ 4.5) | Apology, argument, or a lecture; the anti-persona is bent to |
| M15 | The order for the table | Decides what to drink and eat, for the table | One suggestion that links beer and plate ("this goes well with that"); "if you're not sure, I'll pour you something"; takes the order for the table, not per head; no upsell, no lecture (block 2) | The table orders defensively; staff cannot say why items are on the menu |
| M16 | The wait for the pour | Talks; watches the room | Nothing at the table; the pour happens at the bar in view | A preview of what is coming; a staff member filling the gap |
| M17 | First pour and the bread | Receives the first beer and the bread together | Lands both with one plain sentence: brewed here, baked here with this morning's spent grain; then leaves (block 3) | The two arrive apart; the sentence becomes a speech; the integration feels performed |
| M18 | First sip | Tastes, passes glasses, comments | Nothing for about two minutes | Staff present during the first taste |
| M19 | First plates land | Receives the plates designed for the beer | Places them for sharing; names them in a few words only if the table looks up; no presentation | A plated speech; plates served per person; pairing not visible |
| M20 | The making in view (standing) | Sees the brewery and kitchen working through the evening | Works where guests can see, equipment where it is used, nothing staged (§ 6.1) | Production hidden, or staged as theatre |
| M21 | A question about the making (conditional) | Asks about the beer, the bread, the process | Answers from experience in plain words, as far as the interest goes and no further (block 3, § 4.6) | Notes-of, mouthfeel, a lecture; or a staff member who cannot answer |

## Phase 5 — The evening (60–100 min)

| Code | Moment | Customer does | Brand does | At stake if done badly |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| M22 | Conversation takes over (standing) | Talks; time becomes elastic | Nothing: no check-ins, no "everything OK?" | Managed energy; the table interrupted |
| M23 | Reading the table from a distance (standing) | Does not notice | Watches glasses, plates and pace from a distance; approaches only when the table is ready | A table that has to wave; or hovering |
| M24 | The next round, unhurried | Decides, without deciding, to stay; accepts the next beer | Offers the next beer when the table is ready, tied to what is coming from the kitchen ("there's a darker one that goes well with what's next, want to try it?"); one offer, then steps back (block 4) | Urgency implied; the round pushed; or the table left dry and the evening ends early |
| M25 | Plates cleared, nothing pushed | Finishes plates; may ask whether the table is needed | Clears finished plates only, leaves full glasses; no dessert push, no "anything else?"; if asked: "Stay as long as you like. We're not counting." | Clearing pressure; a transactional turn |
| M26 | Room sound and light (standing) | Hears the table, not the room | Holds sound and light for conversation; nothing competes (value 3) | Music or screens fragment the table |
| M27 | A friend joins late (conditional) | Texts a friend "come"; a friend arrives | A chair, a glass, no fuss: "Bring friends. There's room." | A late guest treated as a problem |
| M28 | Something goes wrong (conditional) | Gets a wrong plate, a beer off, a long wait | "We got that wrong. Here's what we're doing instead." Fixed fast, no repeated apology, manager told (§ 4.7) | Over-apology pins the table to the failure; or no acknowledgement |
| M29 | Bathroom and the walk through the room | Walks through the room | Plain signage, the walk passes the making, nothing decorative on the way (§ 6.4) | Decoration that only exists for atmosphere; a guest who cannot find the way |

## Phase 6 — Leaving (5–10 min)

| Code | Moment | Customer does | Brand does | At stake if done badly |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| M30 | The bill, when asked | Asks for the bill | Brings it when asked, never before; settles it without rush; requests nothing: no review ask, no loyalty card, no rating (block 5) | The evening ends as a transaction; a review request breaks guardrails 2 and the book |
| M31 | "See you next time" | Gets up, gathers the group | Says the farewell as a prediction, warm and short; "bring friends, there's room" if it fits (§ 4.6) | A scripted "thank you for visiting"; or no farewell |
| M32 | The walk out | Walks to the door, may photograph the room | Nothing: no second goodbye, no card, no flyer | A last message that undoes the farewell |
