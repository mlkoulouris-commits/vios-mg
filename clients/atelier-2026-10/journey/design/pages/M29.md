# M29 · HIGH IMPACT · Choosing the partner

B2B · The partner table (phase 8) · weeks · owner: marketing (Menelaos, Evo informed) · customer: the venue owner (no persona on file, **to confirm**)

Dominant inputs: **brand** (Vision, commitment 5) and **concept** (the Lab's capacity and list); BE (commitment); non-verbal in the tasting only.

## CURRENT STATE

A café or restaurant owner asks, or is approached. "This is a partner, not a supplier."

## DO

1. Fit before volume: the partner will put story cards on the table and let the question travel (v3 §1.3 commitment 5, §8.1). A venue that wants the product without the cards is declined, warmly.
2. Tone to the owner: clear, warm, professional, Вие (v3 §5.4 B2B row; Bible §6).
3. The offer on one page (the For Venues page, v3 §7.5): Gelato & Tiramisu made fresh daily at the Lab, delivered with story cards; what the cards say; two menus a year; prices (B2B list **CONFIRM**).
4. A tasting at the Lab or at the venue, run as M11: two at a time, named plainly, no adjectives.
5. The agreement states: cards with every delivery; card text approved by Atelier and not altered; whether the partner's staff ask the question aloud (optional, per partner).
6. Evo informed of every new partner before signing (Bible front page).

## SAY

**LOCKED** B2B line: "Every delivery arrives with story cards for your tables." / „Всяка доставка идва с картички с история за Вашите маси." **Pass.**

Declining a venue: "We only supply venues that serve the story with it. If that changes, write to us." **Pass**: a fact, no push.

## DON'T

- Volume discounts as the pitch; "exclusive" without a decision; "best gelato in Sofia"; ти with an owner; the cards presented as optional.
- Chasing B2B volume over fit (v3 §8.1).

## BEHAVIORAL ECONOMICS

A commitment device: the cards clause commits the partner to the story before the first delivery. Framing (BE 9): partner, not supplier.

## RECOVERY

| If … | → |
| --- | --- |
| A partner stops using the cards | One conversation; then supply reviewed by Evo and Menelaos. R-PARTNER. |
| A partner rewrites a card | Stopped; the approved text restored; logged. |
