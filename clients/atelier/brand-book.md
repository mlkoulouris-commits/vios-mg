#### Primary messages in house format

**1 — Your moment.** For: Elena, 34, central Sofia, walking home along Rakovski with the day still on her shoulders; and the venue owner who walks in and is Elena for ten minutes. Pain: her small wins pass unmarked. Understanding we want to create: ordinary days hold things worth marking, and this place thinks so too. Emotional value: recognition. Competitors say "treat yourself"; we say "what are we celebrating today?". Where it lives: discovery posts (a real guest's moment, with permission), the story card, the question at the counter.

**2 — Not an order.** For: the same Elena, in the queue at any counter in the city. Pain: processed as one of many; "Next!"; the generic friendly; the joyless healthy option. Understanding we want to create: she is a person before she is an order, here and nowhere else in the category. Emotional value: relief. Competitors say "fast, friendly service"; we say nothing — we look up. Where it lives: the arrival (eyes up before words), the no-script rule, the Mindful line stated plainly without a lecture.

**3 — The pause.** For: Elena at the end of a working day; a group on a Saturday. Pain: rushed everywhere. Understanding we want to create: ten minutes here are a pause, not a wait; nobody hurries her through choosing, tasting or reading her Note. Emotional value: calm; feeling lighter on the way out. Competitors say "grab a scoop"; we say "take your time". Where it lives: the tasting of two flavours together, the silence while she reads the card, the chair by the window, the closing line said to her by name.

**4 — Ask, not sell.** For: Elena and the venue owner. Pain: being upsold, anchored, told what everyone takes. Understanding we want to create: here she is asked a question and the answer is accepted, including "nothing"; nothing is pushed, no bestseller, no add-on. Emotional value: trust. Competitors say "our bestseller is…"; we say "same as last time, or shall we look?". Where it lives: the ordering moment, the two-flavour taste, every caption's single question, the B2B conversation (a partner is chosen, not sold to).

**5 — The question and the Note.** For: every guest at every counter, including Ring Mall (how it survives a queue: open-facts 9). Pain: the moment leaves no trace. Understanding we want to create: the question opens the moment and the Note closes it; she can carry it out of the door. Emotional value: being seen, and a small thing to keep. Competitors say "enjoy!"; we say "there's a card in the bag; it's yours". Where it lives: the ritual itself (signature = the question, provisional peak = the Note), the jar on the counter, the card in every B2B delivery, the after-visit reply when a guest posts her card.

Long form, EN then BG (rewritten, not translated):

1. **The existing world.** Elena is thirty-four and runs a small design team in the centre of Sofia. She walks home along Rakovski most evenings with the day still on her shoulders. Her life is good. It is also unmarked: the sign-off that went through, the client who finally said yes, the Tuesday she got through, none of it gets noticed by anyone, including her.
2. **The obstacle.** On the way home every counter treats her as the next order. The service is fast and friendly in the way that is friendly to no one. Dessert is a transaction. The "healthy" option is the joyless one. She has stopped expecting to be seen; she expects to be served.
3. **The call to action.** One evening there is something small in the day worth marking and nobody to tell. If she walks past again, the moment is gone by the time she gets home, and it will not come back.
4. **Meeting the guide.** She steps into the Atelier on Rakovski. Somebody looks up before saying anything. Eight flavours made this morning, a jar of cards on the counter, nothing shouting. The person asks, as if it were the most ordinary question, what we are celebrating today.
5. **The challenge.** Saying it out loud feels small, almost silly. The real risk is not the price or the choice; it is being met with the generic friendly again, and feeling foolish for having answered.
6. **The transformation.** The answer is taken as it is. Two flavours are tasted together; a card is drawn by chance and handed over for her moment. Ten minutes, nobody hurrying. She walks out lighter, with the card in her pocket.
7. **The new world.** Her days have a place where they get noticed. She tells a friend: "They asked what I was celebrating. And I actually told them."

BG:

1. **Съществуващият свят.** Елена е на трийсет и четири и води малък дизайнерски екип в центъра на София. Повечето вечери се прибира по „Раковски" с деня още на раменете си. Животът ѝ е добър. И незабелязан: одобреният проект, клиентът, който най-после каза „да", вторникът, който издържа — никой не ги отбелязва, включително тя.
2. **Пречката.** По пътя към къщи всеки щанд я посреща като следващата поръчка. Обслужването е бързо и любезно по начина, който е любезен към никого. Десертът е транзакция. „Здравословното" е тъжното. Отдавна не очаква някой да я види; очаква да я обслужат.
3. **Зовът.** Една вечер в деня има нещо малко, което си заслужава, и няма на кого да го каже. Ако подмине пак, до вкъщи моментът ще е отминал и няма да се върне.
4. **Срещата с водача.** Влиза в Ателието на „Раковски". Някой вдига поглед, преди да каже каквото и да е. Осем вкуса, направени тази сутрин, буркан с картички на щанда, нищо не крещи. Човекът пита, сякаш е най-обикновеният въпрос, какво празнуваме днес.
5. **Изпитанието.** Да го каже на глас ѝ се струва дребно, почти глупаво. Истинският риск не е цената или изборът; а пак да срещне общата любезност и да се почувства смешна, че е отговорила.
6. **Промяната.** Отговорът е приет такъв, какъвто е. Опитва два вкуса заедно; картичка, изтеглена на късмет, ѝ е подадена за нейния момент. Десет минути, никой не бърза. Излиза по-лека, с картичката в джоба.
7. **Новият свят.** Дните ѝ имат място, където ги забелязват. Казва на приятелка: „Попитаха ме какво празнувам. И аз наистина им казах."

**The belief, deployed.** "Happiness is an attitude." / „Щастието е нагласа." Used when the moment calls for narrative rather than category: the lock-up, the monthly letter, the story card's back, the welcome to new staff. Never at the front of the differentiator and never as a reply to a person.

## Filter test (Part 5; derived 2026-11-01 by `vios-filter-test` from `diagnosis.md` §5, §8, §9 and `persona-bible.md` v1.0; batches in `archive/filter-test-batches.md`)

**Is this talking to the order?** — does it hurry her, sell to her, or speak to no one in particular?

Pass when the answer is no. BG: **„Говорим ли на поръчката?"** — бързаме ли, продаваме ли, говорим ли на никого? (BG form to be said aloud by the team before it goes on the wall chart, open-facts 33.)

**Trait and why.** The category's default is the transaction: a counter talks to the order in front of it, serves it well and fast, and forgets it. Atelier refuses that; its one differentiating trait is that it talks to the person. The diagnosis says it three ways: the persona's fear is "being processed as one of many", the founder's own hard case is "Next!" and the rushed counter, and the Role is Peer, beside the person. Three markers so two people answer the same way: **hurry** (speed words, "next", "anything else?", a choice pushed, a goodbye cut short, an apology for the queue); **sell** (bestseller, add-on, "treat yourself", a price pushed, a last chance, "everyone takes it"); **no one in particular** (hype, self-praise, exclamation energy, a form letter, a slogan where a sentence to her belongs). A plain fact given to whoever is reading is not "no one in particular"; hype is.

**Passes.**
- "You had the hazelnut last time. Same, or shall we look?" — a memory offered to this person; nothing pushed.
- "The pistachio is from Bronte. That's the whole story." — one fact to someone choosing; no adjective doing sales work.

**Fails.**
- "Go for the pistachio, everyone takes it." — sells, and addresses "everyone", not her.
- "We're thrilled to welcome you to our little corner of Italy!!" — performed warmth for a crowd; the exclamation energy the Innocent guardrail forbids.

**Scope.** Any line a guest, a partner or a team member will read or hear: counter scripts, captions, replies, Notes, story cards, emails, Google posts, signage text, internal notes. The name and the taglines are judged by `archive/naming.md`, not here; the recommended editorial line "What are we celebrating today?" is the one asset that is also a line to a guest, and it passes. Character fit only; quality is judged separately.

**Where it sits.** This section; `persona-bible.md` front page and the ten-line checklist; `archive/filter-test-batches.md` (regression set); Stage 4 wall chart and floor playbook (BG form); `vios-consistency-qa` "Filter test" check; `vios-persona-voice` pre-write.

**Review.** Yearly, next 2027-11-01, or when the brand book moves edition. Revisions logged in the batches file.
