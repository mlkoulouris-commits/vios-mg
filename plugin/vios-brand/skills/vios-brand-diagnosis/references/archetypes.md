# The twelve archetypes — reference tool

Source: "Brand Archetypes Reference Tool" PDF in Drive › BRANDING RESOURCES, transcribed 2026-11-01. Pick primary and secondary from this table (70/30, max two). The persona builder proposes pairs from here, never from memory.

Four families: **Leave a mark** (Outlaw, Magician, Hero) · **Pursue connection** (Lover, Jester, Everyman) · **Provide structure** (Creator, Ruler, Caregiver) · **Explore spirituality** (Innocent, Sage, Explorer).

## How to use in the diagnosis
1. Audience archetype first (Worksheet 5 personality insights): which drive and fear describe the guest in this situation.
2. Then what the guest wants in the context of their situation (Worksheet 10 situational desire).
3. Then the brand archetype that serves that want, from the table. State the primary at 70 and the secondary at 30. Say in one line why the pair, and what the secondary must never become (the guardrail).
4. Then the Brand Role: Guide, Inspiration, Peer, Defender or Leader, with the role statement.

Worked example (Atelier del Gelato, Brand Book v3): guest Creator 70 / Lover 30 → wants quality and to be appreciated → brand Lover 70 / Innocent 30, guardrail "Innocent stays light, never cute" → Role: Peer.
