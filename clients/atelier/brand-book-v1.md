## 1. Foundation — why we exist

*Source: `diagnosis.md` §4 (steps 1–4), tested against the worksheet rules; the founder's words not yet heard (open-facts 7). The manifesto is the practice's draft in the brand's voice, written from the purpose and values, and is replaced by Evo's own words after the sitting if they differ.*

### Manifesto (brand voice, draft)

We think happiness is an attitude. Not a mood that arrives when the day is good, but a way of looking at an ordinary Tuesday. Most days have one small thing in them worth marking, and most of the time nobody marks it, including the person it happened to. So we ask. Every order, every counter, every day: what are we celebrating today? Nothing is a fine answer. You get the same scoop and the same card either way. The gelato was made this morning in our own Lab, the Italian way, with its true story and no shortcuts. The card is drawn by chance; it is yours. Ten minutes, nobody hurrying. That is the whole idea.

### Purpose, vision, mission

Not yet shown to run at the Ring Mall counter (contradiction 1, open-facts 9). |

### Second audience: B2B venue owners

Owners and managers of Sofia cafés and restaurants buying from the Lab; 53 % of Lab sales Jun 2025–Aug 2026. Need: a product they can serve under their own roof without embarrassment, reliable fresh delivery, and a reason to choose this Lab over a cheaper tub, which the brand answers with the story card on their tables. Names, count and tenure of current partners: **to confirm (open-facts 19)**; the one-paragraph venue-owner persona is written when that closes. A venue owner who walks into Rakovski is Elena for those ten minutes: one welcome on the floor.

### Competitors (step 6)

Five from the October 2026 audit (per-competitor scores in Drive; the method wants ten, re-run before this edition is final, open-facts 17).

| Competitor | What they own |
| --- | --- |
| Doppio | "joy every day", the one emotional word in the category; cream-and-green identity |
| Gelateria Naturale | provenance as proof: own farm, origin on the label |
| Afreddo, Gelato & Latte | volume gelato chains; named staff create loyalty where it exists; guests police authenticity in reviews |
| Franco's Nuvole | tiramisu as a specialist category; novelty formats |

**Ideas worth adopting:** provenance stated as fact; named staff; tiramisu as a serious category; packaging with personality. **Opportunities (the gaps):** service and warmth is the category's weakest point in reviews; no competitor owns a ritual; nobody combines premium gelato with a serious tiramisu collection; nobody makes honesty (no hype, true origins, no added sugar said plainly) a visible promise; nobody supplies venues with a story attached. The purpose already stands in all five gaps, which is the sign the positioning is credible rather than wished.

### Differentiator (step 7) and positioning (step 9)

**Experience keywords:** noticed · celebrated · unhurried · fresh today · true story · together.

**The one thing:** every order opens with "What are we celebrating today?" and closes with an Atelier Note drawn by chance. It turns a 60-second transaction into a moment the guest carries out of the door, costs no theatre, only people, and so travels to a mall counter and a partner's table. **Onliness:** the only gelato and tiramisu atelier where every order becomes a small celebration of your day.

**The brand belief, held separately from the differentiator:** "Happiness is an attitude." The belief is why; the question and the Note are what. The belief is never the front of the differentiator and never a reply to a person (Part 8, "the belief deployed").

**The differentiator performed:** the question at the counter, a beat of silence, "nothing" accepted, two flavours tasted together, the card drawn by chance from the jar and handed with eye contact, a card in every B2B delivery. Who draws the card and when it is handed, as practised today: open-facts 28.

**Positioning statement:** We help people in Sofia who believe their everyday moments matter but feel processed and rushed wherever they go, to experience a short pause in which their moment is noticed and celebrated, unlike gelato shops that sell product and tiramisu bars that sell novelty; our solution is fresh Italian gelato and tiramisu handed over with a question and a note.

Full statement and the proposals it rejects: Part 6.

## 3. Soul — who we are

*Source: `diagnosis.md` §8 (step 8, instruments 1–4), `persona-bible.md` v1.0 §1–2, 4. Carried as the diagnosis found it; the founder re-reads it at the sitting.*

### The audience link and the Brand Role

Elena's personality desire is company for a minute: "You see me, and you celebrate with me." Not advice, not inspiration, not protection. The Role that answers it is **Peer**. Connection statement: "Your day matters to us as much as it does to you, so let's celebrate it together." The ritual only works side by side ("what are *we* celebrating"); Guide or Inspiration would put the brand above the counter; Caregiver would make the guest a patient.

### Brand archetypal mix

| | Archetype | Characteristics the brand takes | Characteristics it refuses |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Core (70) | **Lover** | closeness, affection, red-carpet treatment for an ordinary day; its fear of invisibility mirrors Elena's fear of being processed | sensuality, heaviness, devotion as display (the Dec 2025 "poetic, sensual, devotional" register is not this brand) |
| Influencer (30) | **Innocent** | lightness, honesty, simplicity, optimism without deceit | cuteness, naivety, party energy. **Guardrail: Innocent stays light, never cute.** |

Rejected pairs, with reasons: Caregiver (guest as patient; the brand is beside her, not above); Jester (party energy breaks the pause); Creator as primary (makes the brand the hero of its own craft; the guest is the hero). Drive: that one ordinary day gets noticed. Desire: to be the place people think of when something small deserves marking. Fear: being the transaction, the fake, the faceless.

### The eight expression dimensions as one imagined person

A warm peer in her early thirties. Physical presence: in the room, at the counter, never the hero of the frame. Dress and style: linen in cream, sage and caramel; nothing styled-perfect. Accessories: a pencil behind the ear, a worn notebook of guests' names; no devices between her and the guest. Language and vocabulary: plain; "we" and "today"; one question before a statement. Tone of voice: warm, 4 of 5, never gushing. Volume: low, 2 of 5; no exclamation energy. Attitude: lightly playful, 2 of 5, earnest without solemnity. Beliefs: spoken rarely; "Happiness is an attitude" said once in a while, never as a reply. She never says "Next!" or "our bestseller". Slider positions and the one-notch-either-way readings: `persona-bible.md` §5.

### The brand's point of view toward the world

On the city: Rakovski is the street people walk home along with the day on their shoulders; the Atelier is the ten minutes that set it down. On Ring Mall: the same question in a queue, which is harder and not optional (open-facts 9). On gelato: made this morning, true origins, nothing hyped, eight flavours are enough. On tiramisu: a collection made quietly behind the scenes, never theatre; what carries the winter. On the Mindful line: no added sugar, said plainly, no lecture. On partners: only venues that will ask the question at their own tables; chosen, not sold to. **On money, growth and mistakes: to confirm** (open-facts 35); the book will not invent a founder's view of his own balance sheet.

### Persona in Practice (instrument 6): where it lives

The nine layers are carried in `persona-bible.md` v1.0, which is Part 5.6 of this book by reference; Annex A compresses them into the AI Persona Brief. Format (real team + written voice, or a disclosed AI host) is the founder's decision (open-facts 26).

*Source: `diagnosis.md` §7, `fact-sheet.md` (signature asset and ritual rows, 2026-10-30), `brand-book.md` › Journey (provisional).*

The differentiator is not a line; it is an order of operations at two counters and in every delivery box.

| Moment | What happens | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Arrival | eyes up before words; a greeting by name if known | designed in Stage 3 |

| Delivery (B2B) | fresh from the Lab in the morning, story cards on top | fact sheet; partner names open-facts 19 |

Long form, EN then BG (rewritten, not translated):

1. **The existing world.** Elena is thirty-four and runs a small design team in the centre of Sofia. She walks home along Rakovski most evenings with the day still on her shoulders. Her life is good. It is also unmarked: the sign-off that went through, the client who finally said yes, the Tuesday she got through, none of it gets noticed by anyone, including her.
2. **The obstacle.** On the way home every counter treats her as the next order. The service is fast and friendly in the way that is friendly to no one. Dessert is a transaction. The "healthy" option is the joyless one. She has stopped expecting to be seen; she expects to be served.
3. **The call to action.** One evening there is something small in the day worth marking and nobody to tell. If she walks past again, the moment is gone by the time she gets home, and it will not come back.
4. **Meeting the guide.** She steps into the Atelier on Rakovski. Somebody looks up before saying anything. Eight flavours made this morning, a jar of cards on the counter, nothing shouting. The person asks, as if it were the most ordinary question, what we are celebrating today.
5. **The challenge.** Saying it out loud feels small, almost silly. The real risk is not the price or the choice; it is being met with the generic friendly again, and feeling foolish for having answered.
6. **The transformation.** The answer is taken as it is. Two flavours are tasted together; a card is drawn by chance and handed over for her moment. Ten minutes, nobody hurrying. She walks out lighter, with the card in her pocket.
7. **The new world.** Her days have a place where they get noticed. She tells a friend: "They asked what I was celebrating. And I actually told them."

BG:

1. **Съществуващият свят.** Елена е на трийсет и четири и води малък дизайнерски екип в центъра на София. Повечето вечери се прибира по „Раковски" с деня още на раменете си. Животът ѝ е добър. И незабелязан: одобреният проект, клиентът, който най-после каза „да", вторникът, който издържа — никой не ги отбелязва, включително тя.
2. **Пречката.** По пътя към къщи всеки щанд я посреща като следващата поръчка. Обслужването е бързо и любезно по начина, който е любезен към никого. Десертът е транзакция. „Здравословното" е тъжното. Отдавна не очаква някой да я види; очаква да я обслужат.
3. **Зовът.** Една вечер в деня има нещо малко, което си заслужава, и няма на кого да го каже. Ако подмине пак, до вкъщи моментът ще е отминал и няма да се върне.
4. **Срещата с водача.** Влиза в Ателието на „Раковски". Някой вдига поглед, преди да каже каквото и да е. Осем вкуса, направени тази сутрин, буркан с картички на щанда, нищо не крещи. Човекът пита, сякаш е най-обикновеният въпрос, какво празнуваме днес.
5. **Изпитанието.** Да го каже на глас ѝ се струва дребно, почти глупаво. Истинският риск не е цената или изборът; а пак да срещне общата любезност и да се почувства смешна, че е отговорила.
6. **Промяната.** Отговорът е приет такъв, какъвто е. Опитва два вкуса заедно; картичка, изтеглена на късмет, ѝ е подадена за нейния момент. Десет минути, никой не бърза. Излиза по-лека, с картичката в джоба.
7. **Новият свят.** Дните ѝ имат място, където ги забелязват. Казва на приятелка: „Попитаха ме какво празнувам. И аз наистина им казах."

## Annex B — Designer brief (draft, issued when open-facts 3, 34 and 36 close)

*Source: § 2.1.6 and the six-section common structure; Part 5.5 reproduced; `persona-bible.md` §9–10; room facts pending open-facts 27. Four to eight pages when issued, with this book as appendix. The indifference section is the practice's draft; Evo confirms it before issue (open-facts 36).*

**1. The brand in one paragraph.** Atelier del Gelato is a gelato and tiramisu atelier in Sofia (Rakovski 149, a Ring Mall counter, an own production Lab supplying both and B2B partners) whose one differentiating act is that every order opens with "What are we celebrating today?" and closes with a handwritten-style Note drawn by chance. The guest is the hero; the brand is a warm peer beside her, Lover 70 / Innocent 30, light and never cute. It believes happiness is an attitude. It refuses the transaction: no hurry, no selling, no speaking to no one in particular.

**2. What the identity must achieve.** Read as a quiet craft place with a belief, next to Aesop and COS without a flinch; carry the lock-up tagline and the two descriptor lines in one lock-up; make the Atelier Note and the story card the recognisable signature assets, in a handwriting face taken from real team handwriting; hold a warm, light lead colour that is not Doppio's cream-and-green; work on a mall counter, a delivery box lid and a partner's table card as well as the Rakovski shop-front; give the two counters and the B2B line one family without a sub-brand; carry Bulgarian and English with equal care.

**3. What the brand refuses.** Posed perfection, product-only heroes, stock smiles, pastel kitsch, process theatre; exclamation energy anywhere in the system; any "Baci"; the retired "alla minute" marks and tiramisu-layer lines; a geometric, systems register of type; a palette that reads as a chain; cuteness (Innocent stays light); any treatment that makes the craft the hero and the guest the audience; any element that makes the Note look like a voucher or a ticket.

**4. What the brand is indifferent to (practice's draft, Evo confirms).** Whether the wordmark is serif or sans, provided it is humanist; whether botanical line drawings, swirls or textures survive at all; the grid and layout system; whether the apricot lead lands warm-orange or warm-peach, provided it is light; the choice of handwriting source within the team; whether the descriptors sit under or beside the wordmark; icon style for digital.

**5. Applications and the decisions asked of the designer.** Signage at Rakovski and the mall counter; the menu boards (two menus a year); the Note cards and the story cards (the signature assets; sizes and stock); the box of 12 and single/sharing tiramisu packaging; delivery packaging for B2B with the story card on top; digital: Instagram, Facebook, Google Business, email letters; staff: aprons or clothing in the register (cream, sage, caramel; no chain uniform), name badges if used; print: the celebration board if one exists (open-facts 27). Constraints from the room: not on file until open-facts 27 closes (counter height, display from the street, table positions). The designer is asked to decide the wordmark, the palette in Pantone/CMYK/RGB, the type roles, the image rules, the graphic elements and the style guide.

**6. Brand Identity Notes.** Part 5.5 reproduced in full in the issued brief.

**7. Review and revision protocol.** Versioned and dated brief (B-v1 on issue); first response reviewed by Menelaos with Evo within one week; tensions the brief did not anticipate are written back as B-v1.1 and the version in force is the one both parties name; the delivered system is checked against the coherence test (Part 12, rows 8–9) before it replaces Part 5.5 and moves this book to v2.0; the Canva brand kit (open-facts 38) is built from the delivered system, not before.
