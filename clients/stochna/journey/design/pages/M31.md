# M31 · CRITICAL IMPACT · "See you next time" ⊐ end-2

In-room · ~10 sec · Phase 6, leaving · Staff-led · **End, designed as one with M30** (see `M30.md`; `choreography.md` › The end pair)

**Dominant inputs:** brand (block 5; § 4.6) · non-verbal (the bookend to M07) · BE (the last moment) · concept (who is near the door, open fact 7).

## CURRENT STATE
Coats on, standing, moving to the door. The last words they will hear here. Before: paid without fuss (M30). After: the walk out, left alone (M32).

## DO
1. Whoever is nearest the door when the table stands looks up and catches their eyes — the same person-at-work posture as at M07, not a staff member sent over.
2. One line, while still doing what they were doing; no walk to the door, no holding it open unless hands are full.
3. If the guest says something about the evening, answer it in kind, one line.
4. Then back to work. The walk out (M32) is theirs.

## SAY (to lock)
- EN: "See you next time." · BG: „До следващия път.“
- When a guest says they'll bring friends: EN "Bring them. There's room." · BG „Водете ги. Има място.“

## DON'T
- No "thank you for choosing us", no "hope you enjoyed".
- No review ask, no card, no "follow us".
- No second goodbye from another staff member.
- No line from someone who wasn't part of their evening, if someone who was is in view.

## NON-VERBAL
**Bookend of M07 (S3-06):** the same eyes-before-words, the same nod, the same not-stopping-work. Smile reaches the eyes (NV C.2); warm, low, unhurried voice (NV B.1, B.2). Line said as a prediction, not a farewell: a slight rise, not a fall.

## BEHAVIORAL ECONOMICS
BE 7 peak-end: end-2 is the last frame of the remembered evening. BE 9 framing: "next time" frames the visit as one of several, not a one-off. No device; the prediction is the only push.

## RECOVERY
- Guest leaves unseen → no chasing to the street; the after-visit (M33–M35) does the work. Log it at pre-shift as a door-cover gap (R-M31).
- Guest leaves upset → R-UPSET; the manager follows up the same night if contact exists (booking), never on the street.

**State arc:** One-time or my place? → Seen out → This is my place.
**Success metric:** guests reply with a plan ("we'll be back", "next week") at the door; count at observation.
**Target emotion:** belonging.
**Brand pillar:** Worth the trip · story block 7.

**Brand-coherence test:** Guide at the open door ✓. **Dependencies:** who covers the door 18:00–24:00 (open fact 7).
