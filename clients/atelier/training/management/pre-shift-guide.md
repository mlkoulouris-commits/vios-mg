# Pre-shift guide — Atelier del Gelato

**Draft — book edition v1.0 unsigned (open-facts 39); observation move not held.**

Facilitator guide for the pre-shift ritual (S4-06). Operational register; what the team says is in quotation marks. For whoever leads the pre-shift: Menelaos, a shift lead, or the senior person on. Durations from `cadence-calendar.md` § 1. Whether a briefing exists today, and shift start times: open-facts 60, 63.

## 1. Why it is a ritual, not a meeting

The same shape every shift, opened and closed by the same person, the brand point first. What comes first sets the frame for the shift; if the announcements go first, within weeks the brand point is dropped. Hold the order even on a night when the announcements feel urgent.

## 2. The full pre-shift (15 minutes, where two or more start together)

| Minutes | Block | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–6 | **Brand point** | Today's slot from the rotation (§ 3). One format only: standing moment, standing scenario or standing customer. Ends with one role-play, once, at the counter. |
| 6–11 | **Operations** | Today's case: what was made this morning, anything missing or finished. Jar count and reserve. The handover note: every open "I'll check", anyone owed a message from Menelaos. Who is Counter and who is Second in each band; who takes the line at the golden hour. |
| 11–15 | **Readiness check** | Each person says the question aloud once, in BG and EN (ops brief § 6). Eyes up, room check done. The lead closes with one line: today's point in one sentence. |

## 3. The 14-day rotation (`cadence-calendar.md` § 3)

| Mon | M06 Someone looks up | R-M09 the question skipped |
| Tue | M09 + M10 the question and the beat | the regular |
| Wed | M11 the answer, and "nothing" | R-QUEUE |
| Thu | M18 the Note | R-NOTES and R-M18 |
| Fri | M25 + M26 the end, with the next guest | the celebration-box buyer (M20) |

**Acceleration:** a moment in the "fix" band of the week's floor audit (`floor-audit.md`) takes the next two pre-shifts' standing slot, whatever the rotation says. A moment in the "keep" band three weeks running drops one turn.

## 4. The three standing formats (6 minutes each)

### Standing moment

1. The lead names the moment and says its floor line from the role card. ("She looks up. You ask it. Whole. Every order.")
2. One team member says the locked line aloud, EN then BG.
3. Role-play once at the counter: the lead plays the guest. The others watch for one thing: the eyes, the beat, the half step back.
4. One sentence from a watcher: what she saw. No discussion.

### Standing scenario

1. The lead names the recovery code and its trigger: "The jar is empty and she's at the till."
2. One team member says what she does and says (playbook § 8), without the card.
3. Role-play once. Check: one sorry, the fix, back to her; no freebie; a named person where it is an escalation.

### Standing customer

The guests on file (`persona-bible.md` § 6 tone by mood; Elena from the brand book):

| Customer | How you recognise her | What she needs from us | The line |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Elena, walking home (17–19) | the walk-home hour, the day still on her shoulders, a little drained | to be seen, and ten minutes nobody hurries | the question; "No rush on it." at the chair |
| The unsure first-timer | reads the whole case, looks up uncertain | a taste, no explanation of the ritual | "Want to taste two together?" |
| The regular | you know her name and her last flavour | to be remembered, not pushed | "[Hazelnut] again, or shall we look?" |
| The box buyer | asks about the box or "something for…" | the same question, more time, no upsell | "Who's it for? We'll choose the twelve together." |
| The excited group | loud, several answers at once | the energy met without matching the volume | "Then something that tastes like that." |
| The child with a parent | a child at the case | both addressed, light, no cute voice | "And what are you two celebrating?" (open-facts 52) |
| The upset guest | raised voice, a complaint | calm, short, no defence, a named person | "I'm sorry. Menelaos will write to you today." |

## 5. The solo opening (5 minutes, one person alone)

Before 11:00 (Rakovski) or before the mall opens (Ring Mall, 11):

1. Read both panels of the role card aloud. Say the question aloud once, BG and EN.
2. Room check (playbook § Before we open).
3. Read the handover note.
4. Read the lead's message for today's point, if one was sent (how it is sent: open-facts 63).

## 6. The pre-shift after a bad shift

Something went wrong yesterday (a complaint, an escalation, a run of misses on the audit):

- **The brand point still comes first.** Choose the scenario that matches, as a standing scenario. Run it as a rehearsal, not a post-mortem.
- **Facts only, no names in front of the team.** "Yesterday a guest was given the wrong flavour twice at the golden hour." Not who.
- **One fix, said as a floor line.** "Wrong one? Take it back. Make it again."
- A named team member's performance is a weekly coaching topic, one to one, never a pre-shift item (`coaching-guides.md`).
- An escalation topic (guardrails 11) is not discussed at the pre-shift beyond "Menelaos has it."

## 7. What the pre-shift never becomes

Announcements first · a list of rules read out · blame · a sales target ("push the boxes today") · the question skipped from the readiness check because "everyone knows it".
