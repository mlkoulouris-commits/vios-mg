# M14 · HIGH IMPACT · A constraint questioned (if)

In-room · ~20 s · Phase 4, the first round · Guest-raised · Conditional

**Dominant inputs:** brand (Proper is better than more; Outlaw "firm, never argues") · concept (what is and isn't offered, CONFIRM) · BE · non-verbal.

## CURRENT STATE
Someone asks for something Stochna doesn't do: a beer not brewed here, an off-menu dish, Wi-Fi. Next: the order (M15).

## DO
1. Acknowledge, don't apologise; one plain reason only if asked why.
2. Offer the nearest real alternative, if one exists.
3. Move on to the order.

## SAY (to lock)
- EN "We don't do that one. The [item] is close." · BG „Това го нямаме. [Нещото] е близко.“
- Asked why: EN "We'd rather do a few things properly." · BG „Предпочитаме да правим малко неща както трябва.“
- Wi-Fi (only once confirmed there is none): EN "No Wi-Fi, honestly. We never set it up." · BG „Нямаме Wi-Fi, честно. Никога не сме го пускали.“

## DON'T
- No apology loop; no "I'll ask the kitchen" when the answer is no.
- No philosophy.

## NON-VERBAL
Even tone, no apologetic smile (NV B.1); stay standing, same distance (NV A.3).

## BEHAVIORAL ECONOMICS
BE 9 framing: a short list framed as care, not lack.

## RECOVERY
- Guest annoyed → R-UPSET.

**State arc:** Why not? → Calm, short → They're not going to adjust. Fine.
**Success metric:** the table orders without a second complaint.
**Target emotion:** respected. **Brand pillar:** Proper is better than more.
