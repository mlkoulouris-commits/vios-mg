## 2. Purpose, vision, mission, values (from the book — CONFIRM with founder)

- **Purpose:** create the social experience that beer and food deserve when they are made together in one place. Commitment → brew and cook as one system → every service. Has action, so it has substance.
- **Vision:** elevate the social identity of beer — a brewery as the centre of a city's social life when brewing and cooking work as one. Cultural, 5–10 years, believable.
- **Mission:** brew and cook in one place as one system, offer proper choices with care, host people as equals, create the conditions for good company. Present tense, action-based.
- **Values (each carries a behaviour):** 1 We brew and cook as one thing · 2 We offer proper choices, not more choices · 3 We create conditions for genuine social experience · 4 We welcome people as equals. Each has a decision test (book § 1.4) — strong.

## 3. Audience

**Primary: the Social Seekers** (book § 2.1). 28–42, Sofia urban core, creative and knowledge workers, medium–high disposable income, social life is identity. Pain: "Most places are either good for beer OR good for food OR good for hanging out. I want all three." Before → after: "Will this be worth the trip?" → "This is my place — I'm bringing everyone here." Archetype: Everyman primary, Outlaw latent.

Built from the book, not from reviews or interviews. The 50 latest Google reviews are the fastest check (open fact 9).

**Anti-persona:** the Convenience Maximizer (speed, Wi-Fi, choice as value). Stance: polite, clear, does not bend.

## 4. Competitor map

The book names four (Kanaal, White Stork Republic, Vitamin B, Sofia Electric Brewing) and maps them structurally. The W6 scoring (10–20 competitors, six criteria, 1–10) is **not done** — open fact 22. The ideas and opportunities below are from the book's narrative only.

**IDEAS (worth adopting):** Kanaal — strong atmosphere and a known name since 2011; Vitamin B — knowledgeable staff who love beer; Sofia Electric — tours that show the making; White Stork — a clear, loud brand identity people can repeat.

**OPPORTUNITIES (their gaps):** Kanaal — service criticised as unfriendly; DJ nights break conversation. White Stork — doesn't brew on site; entertainment competes with the product. Vitamin B — overcrowded, no food, choice overload. Sofia Electric — social experience underdeveloped; brewery and taproom separated. No one in Sofia integrates brewing, cooking and table-first hosting.

## 5. Differentiator and positioning

**Differentiator (structural):** beer brewed and food cooked in one place as one system, with the whole evening designed around the table that creates.

**Positioning:** WE HELP Sofia friends planning an evening together WHO find that places are good for beer, or food, or hanging out, but rarely all three, TO EXPERIENCE an evening where the beer and food were made for each other and the table is the point, UNLIKE breweries that optimise for selection, entertainment or production, OUR SOLUTION brewing and cooking as one system, a short proper menu, and a room and service rhythm built for the table.

## 6. Archetype and role

- Audience: Everyman (belonging, ease) with latent Outlaw (admires calm refusal).
- What they want here: a place worth the trip that feels like someone's place, where no one has to perform.
- **Brand archetype:** Outlaw 70% (the principled, quiet refuser) + Everyman 30%. Creator as the operational engine, expressed in products only.
- **Brand Role: Guide** (book § 5.4 block 5).

## 7. Contradictions — what must change upstream

1. **Review cards vs the book.** Razberry review QR cards are synced (accounts.md), but the book's departure block lists "loyalty or reviews requested" as a failure mode. Decide: where and how reviews are asked (guardrails 2: ask every guest, never reward, never gate) without breaking the departure moment. Open fact 23.
2. **Silence on Google vs "warmth through presence".** 50 unanswered reviews read as absence, not quiet refusal. First move already in README.
3. **"Not a content backdrop" vs paid video.** The agency's ads were three videos with no measurable lift and untagged links. Any new ads must follow book § 6.5 (documentary, no product shots) and carry `?src=meta_ad`.
4. **Claims not yet verifiable.** "Spent grain in the bread", "six items on the menu", "no Wi-Fi", "Bulgarian grain and hops" appear in the book's voice examples; none is on the fact sheet. No copy uses them until confirmed (guardrails 9; open fact 6).
