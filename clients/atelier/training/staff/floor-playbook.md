# Floor playbook — Atelier del Gelato

**Draft — book edition v1.0 unsigned (open-facts 39); observation move not held.**

For everyone behind the counter, at Rakovski and Ring Mall. Kept under the counter at each counter (exact place: open-facts 65). Print one moment per page.

How to read it: **bold** is what you do. Quoted lines are what the guest hears, word for word, EN then BG. Don't change them.

Marks: ★ the question · ◉ the card · ⊐ the goodbye · ○ say nothing. **[to sign]** = a line Menelaos still has to sign. **[gap n]** = we don't know yet; don't make it up, ask the Lead.

The words on this page are in English for now. The Bulgarian version of the instructions is written by the team (open-facts 64).

## The test for any line you say

**Is this talking to the order?** Are you hurrying her, selling to her, or talking to no one in particular? If yes, don't say it.

BG: „Говорим ли на поръчката?" — бързаме ли, продаваме ли, говорим ли на никого? [gap 33: the team says it aloud and confirms]

**Never say:** "Next" · "Anything else?" · "That's our bestseller" · "Everyone takes it" · "Treat yourself" · "You deserve it" · "Last chance" · "The best gelato in Sofia" · "sugar-free" · "Baci".

## Before we open

**Room check. At open, then every hour (Rakovski). At open and mid-afternoon (Ring Mall).**

- Case full. Labels straight. No gaps. The eight classics in their places.
- Jar on the counter, guest side. Cards visible. Reserve under the counter. [gap 43, 53, 54]
- Music low. Two people can talk across the counter. [gap 44]
- Path clear. A chair free (Rakovski). [gap 27]
- Window lit. Nothing stacked. Nothing shouting. Light on till close.
- Spoons at hand. Bin out of sight. [gap 55]
- Allergen matrix at the counter. [gap 13: no matrix yet, so no "yes" to any allergen question]
- Read the handover note: jar count, open "I'll check", anyone owed a message.

**Eyes-down work** (prep, counts, phone, the screen): before 11:00, after close, or by the Second at the back. **Never both backs to the door.**

## 2. The threshold

**M05 · The room (Rakovski).** The room speaks first. Say nothing. Keep it to the room check.

**M06 · Someone looks up. HIGH.**

- **Three seconds: eyes up to her face.** Before any word.
- Smile. Then hello.
- **Free:** "Hi." / „Здравей." / „Добър ден." Nothing more. (Your words, not a script.)
- **Busy with another guest:** eyes and a small nod. Words when it's her turn.
- **You know her name, for sure:**

  > "[Name]. Good to see you." / „[Име]. Радвам се да те видя." **[to sign]**

- Hands off the till and the screen. Scooping? Keep scooping, lift your eyes.
- Then half a step back. Let her read the case.

**Don't:** speak before you look · greet from the till · "Welcome to Atelier del Gelato!" · "What can I get you?" · call across the room · guess a name.

**M07 · The line (Ring Mall, or any line).**

- Every new face in the line: a look, a nod. Between orders, never over a guest's answer.
- Her turn:

  > "Thanks for waiting. What are we celebrating today?" / „Благодаря, че изчака. Какво празнуваме днес?"

- Second free? The Second takes the looks. Nobody takes orders from the back of the line.

**Don't:** "sorry for the wait, we're slammed" · "have your choice ready" · "who's next?" · shorten the question.

[gap 9: how the ritual runs at Ring Mall is Evo's answer; until then, the same ritual, in a queue.]

**M08 · She reads the case. ○**

**Say nothing.** Half a step back. Body toward her, eyes not fixed on her. Wipe a scoop, or be still. **When she looks up: the question.**

**Don't:** name a flavour · "our bestseller" · "need help?" · lean over the case.

## 3. The question

**M09 · The question. ★ HIGH.**

- She looks up from the case, or steps to the counter. Face her. Eyes on hers.
- **Ask it. Whole. Every order. Both counters. Regulars too. Closing time too.**

  > "What are we celebrating today?" / „Какво празнуваме днес?"

- Only a short fact goes before it. The question never changes:

| When | EN | BG |
| --- | --- | --- |
| After a wait | "Thanks for waiting. What are we celebrating today?" | „Благодаря, че изчака. Какво празнуваме днес?" |
| Closing time | "We've got time. What are we celebrating today?" | „Има време. Какво празнуваме днес?" |
| She ordered first | "[Pistachio]. And what are we celebrating today?" **[to sign]** | „[Шамфъстък]. А какво празнуваме днес?" **[to sign]** |
| Child with a parent | "And what are you two celebrating?" [gap 52] | „А вие двамата какво празнувате?" [gap 52] |

- A pair or a group: ask once, to all of them. Take the first answer.
- Ordinary pace. No stress on "celebrating". For her, not for the room.

**Don't:** "Celebrating?" · "…or just ice cream?" · explain the ritual · say it to the till · ask a second question before she answers.

**M10 · The beat. ○**

**Then nothing.** Eyes on her. Face warm, resting. Hands still. One or two seconds, longer if she's thinking.

**Don't:** "…or nothing, that's fine" · "haha" · reach for the scoop · look at the next guest.

**M11 · Her answer. HIGH.**

- **Listen to the end.** Eyes on her. Hands still.
- **She answered:** one sentence back, with her own word. Under ten words. No exclamation. ("Your test. Then something that tastes like that.") No sentence comes? The line:

  > "Then something that tastes like that." / „Тогава нещо с вкус на това."

- **"Nothing", a shrug, "I just want ice cream":** at once, lightly:

  > "Then just something good." / „Тогава просто нещо хубаво." [gap 52: Menelaos confirms this form]

  Same scoop. Same card.

- **A heavy answer** (a hard day, a loss): quietly. No celebration words after it.

  > "I'm sorry. Let's find you something good." / „Съжалявам. Да ти намерим нещо хубаво." **[to sign]**

- She tells you more? Listen. No second question. No advice. No story of your own.
- A colleague nearby says nothing.

**Don't:** push after "nothing" · "Wow!" · "Congratulations!!" · laugh at it · call someone over · "Happiness is an attitude" as a reply · repeat her answer to anyone.

**Your face:** a good answer gets a real smile, **not bigger than 3 of 5**. A heavy one gets a quiet, attentive face.

**M12 · Choosing together.**

- Nothing unasked. Wait for her eyes or her question.
- **A regular, name and last flavour known:**

  > "[Hazelnut] again, or shall we look?" / „Пак [лешник], или да погледнем?"

- **Hesitating, or "what's good?":** offer two to taste (M13). Not a dish. A method.
- **She asks about one flavour:** one true fact. Only these four:

| | EN | BG |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pistachio | "The pistachio is from Bronte." | „Шамфъстъкът е от Бронте." |
| Vanilla | "The vanilla is from Madagascar." **[to sign]** | „Ванилията е от Мадагаскар." **[to sign]** |
| Hazelnut | "The hazelnut is from Piedmont." **[to sign]** | „Лешникът е от Пиемонт." **[to sign]** |
| Lemon | "The lemon is from Sicily." **[to sign]** | „Лимонът е от Сицилия." **[to sign]** |

  No origin for any other flavour. New menu? New lines first (menu switch: gap 25).

- Her pick is the pick. No second suggestion.

**Don't:** "bestseller" · "everyone takes it" · "most people choose" · "amazing" · "the best".

**M13 · Two tasted together.**

- She hesitates, or she's new: offer, unasked.

  > "Want to taste two together?" / [gap 52: „Искаш ли да опиташ два заедно?" or „Да опитаме два заедно?"]

- Two spoons at once, handle toward her.
- Half a step back. Quiet while she tastes.
- A third taste? Give it. No comment.
- Spoons straight into the bin.

**Don't:** "only one taste, there's a queue" · pitch between spoons · "which did you like?" before she says.

[gap 55: spoon type and practice at each counter]

**M14 · Mindful and Aperitivo.**

- **Aperitivo:** say it once.

  > "This one has alcohol in it." / „В този има алкохол." **[to sign]**

- **Might she be under 18? A child, through a parent?** No Aperitivo. Offer a classic, plainly. [gap 14: the legal age line]
- **Mindful:** say what sweetens it, as the menu names it. Nothing about health.

  > "It's sweetened with stevia." / „Подсладен е със стевия."
  > "It's sweetened with honey." / „Подсладен е с мед."
  > "It's sweetened with agave." / „Подсладен е с агаве."

  Don't say "no added sugar" yet. [gap 51: which ones may carry it]

**Don't:** "sugar-free" · "healthy" · "guilt-free" · "diet" · a joke about the alcohol.

**M15 · The price, once.**

- Read it from the price list. Say it once. [gap 12: prices not on file]

  > "It's [price]. Made this morning in our Lab." / „[Цена]. Направено тази сутрин в лабораторията ни."

- She reacts? One true sentence. No comparison. Back to choosing.

**Don't:** "but it's worth it" · "special price today" · "was X, now Y" · a price from memory.

**M16 · "Is it gluten-free? Nuts?"**

- **Answer only from the written allergen matrix.** No matrix yet [gap 13], so nothing is confirmed.
- Not on the matrix:

  > "I'll check with the Lab and tell you exactly." / „Ще проверя с лабораторията и ще ти кажа точно."

- Can't check now? Don't serve it:

  > "I'm not sure, so I won't guess with this one." / „Не съм сигурен, затова няма да гадая с този." (a woman says „сигурна") **[to sign; gap 52]**

- She has an allergy: clean scoop. No tasting from shared tubs without the check.
- **A reaction, or she ate and then told you: R-ESC. Now.**

**Don't:** "should be fine" · "I think so" · "most people with allergies eat it" · any "yes, it's gluten-free" until the Lab's line is filed.

## 4. Marked

**M17 · Scooped. No theatre.**

- Scoop in her sight. Plainly. The portion as sold.
- Tiramisu: fetch it from behind, already portioned. Hand it with its name ("L'Originale").
- In her hand inside a minute. At her chest height. Base toward her.
- Quiet while you scoop. She asks? Answer: "Made this morning in our Lab." / „Направено тази сутрин в лабораторията ни."

**Don't:** toss, sculpt, show off for the line · "alla minute" · "our master gelatiere" · extras she didn't ask for · eyes on the line while you hand it over.

**M18 · The card. ◉ HIGH. The moment she takes home.**

- **Order in her hand. Before the till. Turn to the jar.**
- **Draw blind.** Hand in the jar, eyes on her, not the jar. Don't read it. Don't swap it.
- Hold it by the edges, face toward her, at her chest height.

  > "A card for your moment. It's drawn by chance." / „Картичка за твоя момент. Теглим ги на късмет."

- Let go when she takes it. Half a step back. A beat. **Then the till.**
- Her hands are full: lay it face up in front of her, squared to her. Say the line.
- Takeaway: into her hand, never into the bag. She puts it in the bag if she wants.
- "Nothing" guest: same card, same line. A child: the line and the card to the child.

**Don't:** choose it · read it first · bag it · hand it with the receipt or change · "there are 25, collect them" · skip it for a queue · a flourish · touch her hand.

[gap 28: who draws today, and when it's handed. gap 43: jar and cards at Ring Mall.]

**M19 · Payment.**

- **The card first. Always. Then the amount, once.**
- Eyes back to her as soon as the screen lets you.
- Receipt and change: into her hand or on the counter, never with the card.
- Two on? The till person says the amount only. No re-greeting, no question again.

**Don't:** "anything else?" · an add-on · push a tip [gap 54] · "next" while she pays.

**M20 · The box of twelve.**

- The question first (M09). Then:

  > "Who's it for? We'll choose the twelve together." / „За кого е? Ще изберем дванайсетте заедно."

- Five names, two rows: the classics (L'Originale, Pistachio, Hazelnut), then the fruit (Blueberry, Mango). Her call each time.
- Close the box in her sight. One card from the jar into her hand. [gap 46: a card in the box too?]
- Ordered ahead? Say the day it's ready, once. [gap 46: lead time; don't promise a day the Lab hasn't confirmed]
- A line builds? The Second takes it. You don't hurry her.

**Don't:** "the box is better value" · "everyone loves the pistachio" · push the price.

## 5. The pause

**M21 · The chair (Rakovski).** Only if she lingers or looks round. Once. Open hand toward it.

  > "Take a chair if you like. No rush on it." / „Седни, ако искаш. Няма бързане." **[to sign]**

Clearly leaving? Nothing. The goodbye.

**M22 · She reads the card. ○** Say nothing. Eyes off her card. She shows it to you? A small smile. **Don't:** "which one did you get?" · "that's a good one" · another card · a photo.

**M23 · Eating, talking (Rakovski). ○** Say nothing. Body toward the counter, not the tables. Clear only after she's gone. **Don't:** "all good?" · "everything OK?" · clear her cup · stand facing the tables · turn the music up.

**M24 · Eating at the mall counter.** Leave her be. Your voice is for the next guest, not over her. **Don't:** call the next order over her · "can you step aside?" (unless she blocks a safety route; then once, plainly) · comment on the card.

## 6. The door

**M25 · The goodbye. ⊐ HIGH.**

- **She turns to go: stop. Scoop down. Hands still.**
- Shoulders and face to her. Not the till. Not the line.

  > "Until the next one, [name]." / „До следващия, [име]."
  > "Until the next one." / „До следващия."

- Her name only if you're sure. Once.
- Hold her eyes until she turns. She leaves without looking? Say it anyway, same volume, to her back.
- Closing time: the same line. Never "we're closing" as the last words.
- A child: eyes to the child first, then the parent.
- After a hard day: the same line, softer.
- She paused at Rakovski and a colleague is nearer the door? The nearer one says it. Never both.

**Don't:** say it to the till or over your shoulder · say it to the next guest's face · "have a nice day" · "see you soon" · "come back" · ask for a review.

**M26 · Eyes to the door. ⊐ HIGH.**

- **Eyes with her until she's out** (the door at Rakovski, the flow at Ring Mall).
- Body still. No step to the till. The till waits two seconds.
- **The newcomer is already there?** A look and a nod only **after she has turned her back**.
- **Two on:** you hold her to the door. The other one gives the newcomer the hello. Agree it with a look.
- **Alone, and a rush:** keep the words and your eyes until she turns. Then go to the newcomer. What you drop is the watch to the door. Never the goodbye.
- She looks back? A small nod or smile. No wave.

**Don't:** turn to the next guest while she still faces you · "next" · "who's next?" · start the next question over her goodbye.

### R-ESC — stop the order

**Illness · allergic reaction · injury · a fall · choking · legal threat · press · a complaint about a team member · discrimination · harassment.**

1. **Stop the order.**
2. **Safety first.** A reaction, a fall, choking: **call 112.** First aid per the incident procedure. [gap 59: the procedure, the kit, who is trained]
3. **Get the Lead.** Menelaos, now. Not reachable? [gap 58: the backup]

   > "I'll get [Lead] now." / „Веднага викам [име]." **[to sign]**

4. **Say nothing else.** No explanation, no opinion, nothing online. Evo handles legal and press.

## 9. Handing over and closing

**Two of you on:**
- The one who asked keeps the question and the card. The Second does the till, the looks to the line, the room.
- Till: pass the amount with a look or a word, not over her.
- Allergen question to the Lab: her exact words. She hears: "I'll check with the Lab and tell you exactly."

**At close, the handover note** (where it lives: gap 65):
- Jar count and reserve.
- Gaps in the case; anything finished.
- Every open "I'll check".
- Anyone promised a message from Menelaos.
- Cards left on the counter or tables (count them).
