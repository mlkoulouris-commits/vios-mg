# Harvest — Stage 2, Atelier del Gelato (2026-11-01)

Draft; book edition v1.0 unsigned (open-facts 39); observation move not held. Candidates only (one use); promoted to confirmed on a second.

## Arc patterns (candidates)

- **The counter arc with the question as its own phase.** On a counter format the category's single "ordering" phase splits into asking/receiving (the guest exposed) and making/marking (the guest receiving). Stage 3 needs the split because geometry, script and recovery differ. Matches the archived candidate S2-C1 (the counter arc); second use would confirm it.
- **The walk-home phase.** For walk-in retail with no booking, discovery online and the decision on the street are one decision made twice, held in one phase; the pre-arrival phase is owned by marketing and the street, not by a reservation.
- **One map, two counters.** A second counter of the same brand is mapped as venue-tagged moments inside the same arc (here four RM moments and two tested there), not as a second map, until the founder says the ritual differs; the map names the fact that would split it.

## Moment patterns (candidates)

- **The silence a question requires** (M10): a fourth kind of silence beyond the manuscript's three, the beat between a question and its answer; without it the signature line becomes a slogan.
- **The end on a counter as two moments** (M25 + M26, designed as one), because the leaver and the newcomer share a face in the same seconds. Matches the archived candidate S2-C4; second use.
- **The signature before the peak** (M09 → M18): the ritual opens with the signature and closes with the peak. Matches the archived candidate S2-C2; second use.
- **A third-party phase mapped MEDIUM-only** (phase 8): a phase whose customer has no persona and whose moments a partner performs carries no HIGH and passes to Stage 3 as briefs.
