# M03 · HIGH IMPACT · The window on Rakovski

Environmental · Before: the blur (phase 1) · ~2 min · Approach · owner: floor (the designer for the signage)

Dominant inputs: **brand** and **non-verbal** (environment); concept (the frontage); BE (affect).

## CURRENT STATE

Walking home along Rakovski with the day still on her shoulders (v3 §4.3). She passes the window. "I could stop here."

## DO

1. Signage: the name with the descriptor "Gelato · Tiramisu · Caffè"; handwritten or hand-painted elements welcome (v3 §7.2). No "Baci", no alla-minute marks anywhere on the frontage (diagnosis contradiction 1; audit the current window, door and A-board).
2. Light: warm and directed, visible from the pavement from dusk; the display lit as the centre, seen from outside where the room allows (sightline **to confirm**).
3. People visible inside, at the counter; a team member seen from the street stands as at M5 (relaxed-upright, no phone).
4. The door: clean glass, hours posted plainly, open in warm months. No sandwich board with offers, no "try our…" call-outs.
5. The frontage carries the tagline only inside the lock-up, never as a slogan on its own.

## SAY

Nothing spoken. Signage text: name, descriptor, hours, the tagline in the lock-up. Facts; **pass**.

## DON'T

- "Best gelato in Sofia" or any superlative on the glass; price promotions in the window.
- A frontage that looks closed at 19:30; stale posters; a dark display.
- Retired marks: tiramisu-layer lines, the crafting-time clock, "alla minute" (v3 §6.5, §7.4).

## NON-VERBAL

Light (NV F.1) and surface (NV F.4: marble, oak, linen in view) read from the pavement; posture of staff visible through glass (NV C.1); an open door is an invitation without a call (proxemics: the brand does not step out to pull).

## BEHAVIORAL ECONOMICS

Thin-slicing (BE 17) begins on the pavement; affect heuristic (BE 5); cognitive ease (BE 1): the descriptor says what the place is, so nothing has to be worked out.

## RECOVERY

| If … | → |
| --- | --- |
| A frontage element still carries "Baci" or an alla-minute mark | Retired, dated, owner action; logged in the brand-book summary. |
| The display light is out | Fixed before dusk the same day; pre-shift checklist. |

**State arc:** Passing → Noticing → Deciding to stop

**Success metric:** Signage audit clean of retired marks; walk-ins who enter without reading a menu outside (sampled).

**Target emotion:** drawn, gently

**Brand pillar:** welcome; story block 3 (the call to action)
