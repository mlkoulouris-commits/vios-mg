# Choreography & continuities — Atelier del Gelato

Stage 3, § 2.3.2.5. Read once; then assumed on every page. Written 2026-11-01 against the 31 pages in `pages/`. Operational register.

## 1. Continuities

Rules that run through the whole journey. Each is a title and a rule; the pages do not repeat them.

**The voice is the same from the caption to the farewell.** Giulia's written voice online (warm · attentive · light · calm · honest; short sentences; "we" and "today"; no emoji, no exclamation marks) and the team's spoken voice at the counter are one voice in two registers. A guest who read the caption at M1 hears the same person at M7 and M22. Filter test on every line: *Is this processing the guest?*

**Eyes before words, every time.** At the door (M5), before the question (M7), before each spoon (M11), before the card (M15), before the farewell (M22). No line is said to a guest the team member is not looking at. The one designed look-away is M18: after the card is handed, the eyes leave so she can read.

**The signature leads, every guest, every daypart.** "What are we celebrating today?" is asked at every order: first-timer, regular, child's order through the adult, to-go, celebration box, queue, Ring Mall. It is the one line that is never dropped. The tasting shortens under pressure; the question and the Note never do.

**Across the counter, at level eyes, one step back.** The brand's geometry is head-on across the display, the counter width as the distance, a half step back from the counter edge after every locked gesture (the question, the spoons, the card). The team member never leans over the counter to shorten the distance, never bends to it. Hands are empty and visible whenever a line is said.

**Step back after every locked gesture.** Ask, then still. Spoons, then still. Card, then still and eyes away. Farewell, then the held attention. The stillness after each act is what makes the act land; the pages call it the beat, the wait, the silence, the held attention. It is one discipline.

**Where the team stands when not with a guest.** At the station, facing the room, hands at rest or at a quiet task, face resting warm, phone out of sight. Never with the back to the door, never in a huddle at the till. Between guests the room is being read through the glass (M3) and from the door (M4): the resting face is on duty.

**The moment cluster around the peak: M14–M19 protects M15.** The scoop is quiet (M14) so the card is the event. The card is handed with the order, before payment (M15), so the till is not the last exchange. The price is plain (M16) and the bag is quiet (M17) so nothing competes with the card. Then two silences (M18 reading, M19 the first taste) so the surprise lands without a witness. Every moment in the cluster is designed to make M15 the one she keeps; none of them is allowed its own flourish.

**The end cluster: M22–M23 against the next guest's M5.** The farewell line and the held attention are one design performed on the same face that the next guest is reading. The rule: shoulders stay with the leaving guest until she is past the counter; eyes go up to the newcomer within ten seconds. Two on the counter at peak so that the two moments do not have to be performed by one body; with one on, the degradation is named (eyes only to the door), never silent.

**The silences are design.** M8 the beat, M18 reading, M19 the first taste, and the two steps from the door to the counter. Staff trained without the why will fill them; the training (Stage 4) teaches the reason before the rule. A filled silence is logged as a miss, like a dropped question.

**Nothing she can see is announced.** The display, the jars, the tiramisu collection, the hours on the door, the water on the counter: visible, therefore not said (S3-03). What is said is what she cannot see: the origin, "made this morning", the alcohol in a flavour, that the Note is for her.

**Facts only, from the fact sheet.** Origins, "made this morning", "no added sugar", gluten-free, prices, hours: each line traceable to the fact sheet and its date. A fact not on the sheet is not said; a question beyond it goes to Menelaos.

**Intensity capped at 3.** The expression palette (Bible §9) tops at 3 for welcome, shared joy and the sign-off; 2 for showing a flavour and handing the Note. Nothing in the room is louder than that. Innocent stays light, never cute; Lover is warm, never effusive.

**The Ring Mall counter performs the same choreography until Evo decides otherwise.** Every page marks the mall as "to confirm"; nothing is shortened there by default.

## 3. Handoffs

Who passes the guest to whom, what is said and shown, what the guest does not see.

**M5 → M7, two on the counter.** The team member who met her eyes asks the question. No handoff: the look and the question belong to one person. If the one who looked up cannot serve her (mid-order, hands full), the second team member takes her from the look onward, and the first's look was still hers; nothing is said between them. The guest does not see a transfer; she sees two people who both saw her.

**M15 → M16, the Note and the till.** The same hands. If the station and the till are apart, the card is handed at the station, then the team member moves to the till with her; the card does not wait at the till.

**M22 → M23 → M5 (next), two on the counter.** The one who served her gives the farewell and holds the door-attention; the other takes the newcomer's eyes-up. The orientation transfer is silent: one body stays angled to the door, the other squares to the counter. The guest leaving sees a face still with her; the guest arriving sees a face already on them.

**Counter → Lab (M29–M30).** The partner is Menelaos's; the delivery is the Lab's. The handoff is the dispatch sheet: flavours, date, card count. The partner's named receiver signs it. The partner's guest (M31) never sees Atelier's people; the card is the handoff.

**Floor → Menelaos (any escalation).** The team member says the one line ("I'm sorry. Menelaos will write to you today."), takes the guest's name and a way to reach her if she offers it, and tells Menelaos the same day with the facts. The guest sees one calm person and hears one promise that is kept. She does not see a huddle, a manager summoned to the counter, or a second apology.

**Floor → marketing (M25, M26).** A guest moment seen on the floor is not posted by the floor. The team member tells Menelaos; marketing asks the guest for permission in writing; only then M1. The guest does not see her moment posted before she said yes.
