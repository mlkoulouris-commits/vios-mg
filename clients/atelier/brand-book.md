## Social rules from v3 §7.5 (binding for the content calendar)

| Pillar | Share | Content |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Guest moments | 35% | real notes and celebrations, with permission |
| Attitude | 25% | "Happiness is an attitude": short lines, small rituals |
| Creations | 25% | gelato and tiramisu with true stories; seasonal launches |
| Behind the people | 15% | the team, their handwriting, their own small wins |

- Channels: one account; Instagram primary, Facebook for information and events, Pinterest optional.
- Hashtags: #MyAtelierMoment (primary), #HappinessIsAnAttitude. Retired: #TiramisuAllaMinute, #AllaMinuteMoment.
- Campaigns: Happiness is an Attitude (belief) · What Are We Celebrating Today? (ritual) · Celebrate at Your Table (B2B). Never stage a moment.
- Content lengths: flavour story 50–75 words · social caption 30–50 · email 150–200 · story card 25–40 · Atelier Note 10–15.
- Email: three-letter welcome series, monthly letter, quiet birthday note, first-visit anniversary.

Voice: see `persona-bible.md` (built from v3 §3 and §5.4).
