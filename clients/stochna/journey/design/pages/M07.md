# M07 · CRITICAL IMPACT · Crossing the threshold

In-room · ~30 sec · Phase 3, the threshold · Room-led, then staff · Threshold sequence M07 → M08 → M09 (S3-08 + S3-01 + S3-02)

**Dominant inputs:** brand (block 1; Role: Guide who doesn't stop working) · non-verbal (thin-slice, the room as channel) · concept (who is at the door, open fact 7; open kitchen and brewery in view) · BE (first impression).

## CURRENT STATE
They made the 2 km trip on purpose and are checking whether it was worth it. First seconds inside, before anyone speaks. Before: the street and the door (M06). After: the greeting line (M08).

## DO
1. **Threshold lock (behaviour, not a line):** within 10 seconds of the door opening, the person nearest the door looks up and meets the guest's eyes, nods, and keeps working. The book's character doesn't stop to greet (§ 4.2), so the lock is the eyes, not a welcome.
2. The line comes at M08, from the same person, within the next 20 seconds.
3. The room does the work: kitchen and brewing equipment in view from the door, people at tables, the smell of bread and hops at the door (CONFIRM baking times, open fact 6).
4. Sound at the door low enough to hear one sentence without raising voices (target to set at observation; music policy open fact 11).
5. Light warm, no spotlight on the door, no host stand blocking the view of the room (CONFIRM the floor plan).

## SAY
Nothing at M07. The locked greeting is M08.

## DON'T
- No "Welcome to Stochna!" ceremony; no host rushing over.
- No reservation question before eye contact.
- No phone or tablet in the hand of the person looking up.
- No empty-room effect: on quiet nights seat the first tables in view of the door.

## NON-VERBAL
Eyes before words (NV C.2), a nod (NV C.3, gesture economy), body stays turned to the work (NV C.4). Room: F.1 warm lighting register, F.2 sound register, F.3 scent at the threshold. Distance: no approach yet (NV A.1).

## BEHAVIORAL ECONOMICS
BE 17 thin-slicing: the evening is judged in seconds, before any word. BE 5 affect heuristic: the feeling at the door colours every later judgement. BE 1 cognitive ease: nothing to decode; the room explains itself.

## RECOVERY
- Guest hovers more than 15 seconds unseen → anyone who sees it gives the M08 line, wherever they stand (R-M07).
- Room full, walk-in → R-FULL.
- Guest arrives upset (parking, the walk) → R-UPSET; listen once, then the M08 line.
