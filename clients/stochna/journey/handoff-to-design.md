## Critical dependencies Stage 3 must flag

1. **Observation before locking.** Two shadowed services and a 50-review read (`open-facts.md` row 9). Every HIGH is brand-led until then.
2. **Daily spent-grain bread and the timing of the first beer with it.** The signature M17 depends on both (row 6). Fallback: the signature moves to M19.
3. **Door staffing.** Whether anyone is at the door 18:00–24:00 or the nearest staff member greets (row 7). The M07/M08 design differs.
4. **Payment route.** Table or counter (row 8). End-1 differs.
5. **Returning-guest visibility in Memento.** Whether the floor can see a returning booking (row 5). M36 cannot carry a design without it.
6. **Sound and music policy.** None in the book (row 11). M26 and the peak depend on the room letting the table hear itself.
7. **Staff dress.** Not in the book (row 13). The dominant-dimension column at M07/M08 lists attitudes and tone, not dress, for that reason.
8. **The weekend day arc and the events line** are not in this map (rows 2 and 14). Stage 3 designs the evening only.

## Where the design has latitude

- **Wording of every line except the book's own phrases.** The book gives candidate lines (§ 4.5, § 4.6); Stage 3 locks them or writes plainer ones in the floor register, tested aloud, within the words-used and words-avoided lists.
- **How the next round is offered**: spoken, or a glass shown, or the darker beer named on a card at the plate; the constraint is once, when ready, tied to the kitchen.
- **Table geometry and how a table is "laid for sharing"**: the book says shared plates and rounds; it does not say how many to a board or where the bread sits.
- **The sentence at the signature**: the book gives "The spent grain in this bread came from this morning's brew"; Stage 3 may lock a shorter one.
- **What the staff member nearest the door does with their hands** at the threshold: the book says they keep working; Stage 3 decides what work is visible from the door.
- **Recovery compensation**: none in the moment unless the guest is visibly upset; the manager decides at the bill, if at all (three-registers example 3). The book is silent on compensation; Stage 3 designs it within "We got that wrong. Here's what we're doing instead."
- **Nothing in the map constrains the room beyond the book's Part 6**, so no architect or interior brief revision is issued at this stage. If observation shows the door and window do not let the room be seen from the street (M06, M07), an interior brief revision follows.
