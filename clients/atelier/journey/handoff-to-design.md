## D. Where the design has latitude

- The exact wording of the greeting at M06 for an unknown guest (no locked line; a behaviour is locked).
- The geometry of the two-flavour taste (spoons, cups) and the moment it is offered.
- The chair, the window, the table layout (once 27 closes).
- How names are learned and kept (a notebook is the persona's accessory; the real method is open, 45).
- The partner brief's format and the quarterly check.
- The Ring Mall counter's queue choreography, within the rule that the question is never shortened.
- Recovery lines, within the Bible's vocabulary; every new floor line is logged as new copy in `archive/filter-test-batches.md` for the approver.

## E. What the design must not do

Script the welcome; choose the card; shorten the question for the queue; add a kiosk; invent a price, an origin, an allergen or an age line; fill a silence; let the goodbye go to the next guest; design phase 8 as if the partner were the brand.
