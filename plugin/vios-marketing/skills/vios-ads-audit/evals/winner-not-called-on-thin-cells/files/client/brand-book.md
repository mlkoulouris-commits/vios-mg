# Brand book (live summary) — Taverna Kato v1

## Messages and story
### Core messages
| Short name | Tier | Understanding to create |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The table is the centre | primary | Sunday here is one long table; you come for the people as much as the lamb. |
| Made here, slowly | primary | The lamb goes in at eleven; nothing is rushed, nothing is reheated. |
| Lozenets kitchen | secondary | This is the neighbourhood's kitchen, not a destination restaurant. |

### Story framework
1 existing world: a Sunday that stays ordinary · 2 obstacle: nobody cooks for twelve any more · 3 call to action: a chair at the long table · 4 meeting the guide: Kato, calm, cooking · 5 the challenge: wanting the family Sunday back · 6 transformation: the table at two, full · 7 new world: Sunday has a place again.

## Journey (touchpoints)
discovery · profile check · booking · reminder · arrival · ordering · making · the evening (Sunday: "the table", peak) · departure · after-visit · review
