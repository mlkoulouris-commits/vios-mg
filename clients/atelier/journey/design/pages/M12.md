## CURRENT STATE

She has answered, or said "nothing" (M11). She is between flavours, or asks "what's good?". She does not want to be sold to. A regular wants to be remembered.

## DO

1. **Counter**: wait for her to look up from the case or ask. Nothing unasked.
2. **Regular** (name and last flavour known): the regular line, once.
3. **Hesitating, or "what's good?":** the decision anchor is a method, not a dish: offer two to taste together (M13).
4. **She asks about one flavour:** one true fact, from the four origin lines; nothing more.
5. Accept her pick at once; no second suggestion.
