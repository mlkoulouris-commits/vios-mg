## Omitted, with reasons (add back when the team grows)

| Document | Why left out now |
| --- | --- |
| Workbooks per role | small team; the training plan carries the role notes; add when there are 3+ servers on rotation |
| Role cards | the playbook is short enough to sit at the bar; cards once the scripts are locked (laminating unlocked lines wastes a print run) |
| Wall chart | needs the founder's sign-off on what goes on a staff wall; draft after lock |
| Reader | no senior staff tier yet |
| Facilitator guides | the trainer works from the training plan; one guide for the pre-shift ritual after the first month |
| Operating handbook | the operations brief plus the design folder serve the manager for now; assemble at the 6-month review |
| Coaching guides, assessment instruments | the standards table in the operations brief § 7 is the first instrument; full guides once a manager is named as coach |
| Cadence calendar | the cadence is in the training plan § 6; a year calendar after the first-thirty-days review |
