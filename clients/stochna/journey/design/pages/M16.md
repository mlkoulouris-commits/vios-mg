# M16 · SILENT · The wait for the pour ○

In-room · 3–6 min (CONFIRM) · Phase 4 · Between two HIGH moments (M15 → M17), pattern S2-03

## DO
Nothing at the table. M15 repeated the order; the bar and the pass time beer and bread to land together (`choreography.md` › handoffs).

## DON'T (would break the silence)
- No "your drinks are coming"; no bread sent ahead to fill the wait.
- No beer delivered alone to break the wait.

**Metric:** beer and bread land within one minute of each other (observation).
