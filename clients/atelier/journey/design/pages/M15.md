# M15 · STANDARD · The price, once

**Draft — book edition v1.0 unsigned (open-facts 39); observation move not held.**

In-room · Phase 3 The question · ~5 sec · Both counters · markers none · weight provisional · dominant dimension: language (plain) · inputs: concept (prices **CONFIRM**, open-facts 12), BE (anchoring), brand (guardrails 3)

## CURRENT STATE

She asks the price, or reacts to it ("why so expensive?"). She wants a fact, not a defence.

## DO

1. State the price as a fact, once, from the current price list.
2. If she reacts: one true sentence about what it is, no comparison.
3. Then back to choosing, at her pace.

## SAY

Bible hard moments, **not lockable until prices are on file** (open-facts 12):

| | EN | BG |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Price | "It's [price]. Made this morning in our Lab." | „[Цена]. Направено тази сутрин в лабораторията ни." |

## DON'T

- No "but it's worth it", "special price today", "was X, now Y" (guardrails 3; the only reference price is the lowest of the previous 30 days).
- No price said twice, or said to push a size.
- No price invented or remembered: read the list.

Metric: price stated once, no defence, in a sampled hour (x of n price questions). Recovery: "why so expensive?" said with irritation → **R-PRICE**.
