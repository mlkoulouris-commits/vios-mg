# Harvest — Stage 3, Stochna Kitchen Brewery

Date 2026-11-02 · collated from the `Harvest:` flags on the 36 page footers, `recovery.md` and the filter-test batches · § 2.3 harvest. Draft design, unobserved and unsigned: every entry below is a candidate and counts as one use only once the design is signed and has run.

## New patterns (candidates)

### S3-C1 — Read the table by three signals before the peak offer
- **When to use:** when the peak is an offer (the next round) and the brand forbids check-ins.
- **What it produces:** a fixed rule for when to approach: glasses a third full or less · a pause in the conversation · someone looking round. All three = approach; one alone = not yet. Paired with a server station that sees its tables.
- **What it asks of the brand:** tables per server low enough to watch (operations brief A1).
- **Failure mode:** the rule drifts into a timer ("every 20 minutes"), which is a check-in under another name.
- Source: M23, M24.

### S3-C2 — The threshold lock as a behaviour where the book forbids ceremony
- **When to use:** when the brand character "doesn't stop to greet" and a welcome line at the door would break the book.
- **What it produces:** S3-01 met by a lock on behaviour, eyes and a nod within 10 seconds from whoever is nearest, with the first locked line one moment later (M08). The skill names this case; this is the first brewery use.
- **Failure mode:** no one owns the door, so the 10 seconds become 30 (R-M07).
- Source: M07, M08.

### S3-C3 — End-2 by the person at work, not a sent host
- **When to use:** with S3-06 (bookended end) in a venue with no host role.
- **What it produces:** the farewell comes from whoever is nearest when the table stands, in the same posture as the threshold; no one is sent to the door.
- **Failure mode:** the guest leaves unseen; the after-visit (M33–M35) has to carry the end alone.
- Source: M31, M32.

### S3-C4 — Grid ratio rule for a brand with an AI host
- **When to use:** when an AI ambassador posts on a profile whose target state is "someone's place, not a concept".
- **What it produces:** at most one tile in three is the ambassador; people and the making lead. The pilot decides whether the AI stays on the grid at all.
- **Failure mode:** the ambassador becomes the brand's face and the profile reads as a concept.
- Source: M02; `persona-bible.md` § 8, § 15.

## Recovery scenarios (candidates for the catalogue)

- **R-OVER, framed for a brewery:** stop offering, water to the table, the manager decides; staff never script the conversation. Alcohol venues need it as a mandatory scenario.
- **R-FULL with a plan:** a full room is said plainly with the next real free time ("Thursday has room"), never with scarcity language (guardrails 1).
- **R-M17 signature fallback:** when the two objects can't land together, the next plates carry the signature (M19); the map's fallback written as a recovery.

## Template refinements

1. **"Claim blocked" as a filter-test verdict.** A line can pass the test and still be unlockable because the fact behind it isn't on the fact sheet (spent-grain bread, no Wi-Fi). Batches 3–4 use "pass, claim blocked"; propose it as a standard column.
2. **Fallback line on a page whose locked line rests on an unconfirmed claim** (M17). The page can train on the fallback while the claim is chased.
3. **"Trainable except the facts listed"** used in the close test with a fact → pages map; worked well, keep the map format.
4. **Operations brief § 2 on assumptions** when no POS data exists: headed "to replace with data", with the rule that short staffing breaks the peak cluster first. Worth a line in the chapter.
