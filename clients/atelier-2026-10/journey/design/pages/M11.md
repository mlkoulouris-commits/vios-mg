# M11 · CRITICAL IMPACT · Tasting two together

In-room · Choosing together (phase 3) · 30–90 sec · Counter, across the display · the decision moment

Dominant inputs: **brand**, **concept** and **behavioural economics** (a decision moment); non-verbal carries the time and the spoons.

## CURRENT STATE

She has answered the question or declined (M9) and heard one or two flavours tied to her answer, each with one true line (M10). She is at the display, choosing; the queue may be behind her. "They're choosing with me" moving to "I can take my time." The only decision she makes in the visit is this one. Next: gelato, tiramisu or both (M12); the Aperitivo line if she picks it (M13); the scoop (M14).

## DO

1. After the suggestion (M10), not before it and not instead of it: the offer of a taste, the locked line.
2. Two at a time at most, one ceramic or wooden tasting spoon each (v3 §7.6, §7.7 Touch), taken from the holder in view, offered handle first across the display, one in each hand or one after the other. Each spoon named as it passes: the flavour name, no adjective. If asked, the origin from the fact sheet and nothing more.
3. Then half a step back, hands at rest, eyes to the display so she tastes unwatched. The second locked line if she is weighing it. Then nothing until she speaks or looks up.
4. The first used spoon goes to the discard dish; the gesture shows where, no words.
5. She chooses: the flavour name said back to her, plainly, eyes to her; then the scoop (M14). Nothing is scooped before she has said the name.
6. More tastes: yes, the same way, one or two at a time, no ceiling stated to her. After three, if she is still unsure, one anchor, once (SAY).
7. Lines with rules: Mindful is "no added sugar", never "sugar-free"; Aperitivo is named as alcoholic in one plain line (M13); gluten-free is verified for gelato and tiramisu (fact sheet, Lab confirmation to be filed); any other allergen goes to the allergen policy (CONFIRM) or to Menelaos, never to a guess (guardrails 9, 11).
8. A regular: "Pistachio again, or something new today?" was M10; here, a taste of the new one if she wavers, nothing if she does not.
9. Queue: the tasting is not shortened by the team member; she sets the pace. Two on the counter: the second station opens. One on: the next guest gets eyes up (M5) and the tasting continues. Ring Mall form to confirm.
10. Palate water (v3 §7.7 Taste) on the counter, within reach, not offered aloud.
11. A child in the group: a taste for the child too, with the adult's nod; the question and the choice are the adult's.
12. The room: the display lit as the centre, flavours grouped as the menu groups them (Classics, Aperitivo, Mindful, Seasonal; v3 §7.1); spoons in a ceramic holder and a discard dish at every station; the counter top clean between guests.

## SAY

**LOCKED**:

> EN: "Want to taste two together?"
> BG: „Искаш ли да опиташ две заедно?"

> EN: "Take your time."
> BG: „Не бързай."

Registers:

| Register | Line |
| --- | --- |
| Default | The two locked lines, in that order, once each |
| Naming a spoon | The flavour name only: "Bronte pistachio." „Шамфъстък от Бронте." |
| Indecision after three tastes, she shared a moment | "If you're not sure, the [flavour]. It's the one for a day like that." |
| Indecision after three tastes, she said nothing | "If you're not sure, the [flavour]. Made this morning." (only when true that day) |
| Regular wavering on something new | "Taste the new one?" |
| She hurries herself ("just a pistachio") | "Pistachio it is." No taste pushed. |
| She declines the anchor too | "No hurry." Then silence. |

Filter test (*Is this processing the guest?*): to her, no hurry, nothing sold; the anchor is one named flavour, stated once, tied to her day or to today's batch, never to popularity. All **pass**. Register ти / warm "you".

## DON'T

- Don't look at the queue, the clock or the till while she tastes.
- Don't offer three at once; don't offer all eight.
- Don't "That's our bestseller", "Most people take…", "Our most popular", "I'd go for…" without her asking.
- Don't put an adjective on the spoon ("amazing", "to die for"); the name, and the origin if asked.
- Don't "Are you ready?", "Have you decided?", "What'll it be?".
- Don't hand a spoon while talking to someone else; don't scoop before she has said the name.
- Don't fill her silence with suggestions; one anchor, once, only on indecision.
- Don't call Mindful "sugar-free", "healthy" or "guilt-free"; don't call anything "natural" or "organic" (guardrails 9).
- Don't push the tiramisu ("and a tiramisu with that?"); M12 is one offer.
- Don't "last of today's batch" or any count that is not counted (guardrails 1).

## NON-VERBAL

- **Time** (NV E.1, E.2): the wait is hers. The half-step back after the spoons is what makes it a wait rather than a delay; the team member's stillness is the design.
- **Eyes** (NV C.2): on her as each spoon is handed; released to the display so she tastes unwatched; back when she looks up. Never to the queue.
- **Hands** (NV D.3): spoons carried deliberately, offered handle first; the discard dish shown by use, not by words; no scoop in the hand during the taste.
- **Haptics** (NV D.1): ceramic or wooden spoon, cool gelato in a warm room; never plastic (v3 §7.4 materials).
- **Posture** (NV C.1): relaxed-upright; no drumming, no shifting weight toward the next guest.
- **Orientation** (NV C.4): body stays to her; a colleague's question waits.
- **Tone and volume** (NV B.1, B.3): the flavour name said plainly, falling; for her.
- **Silence** (NV B.4): after "Take your time", nothing until she speaks or looks up.

## BEHAVIORAL ECONOMICS

- **The decision moment**: the one choice she makes in the visit; System 2 is engaged; the design lowers its cost without taking it from her (the library's failure mode for defaults: a guest who feels handled rather than hosted).
- **Choice architecture** (BE 8): sixteen gelato flavours and five tiramisu is too many to choose from cold. M10 narrows to one or two tied to her answer; the taste turns names into a sensory comparison; the full display stays visible so she never feels directed.
- **The decision anchor** (S3-04): one named flavour on indecision, tied to her answer or to today's batch, defended by a fact ("made this morning"), never by popularity. The anchor is named at pre-shift from the day's batch so it does not drift.
- **Endowment effect** (BE 12): a taste on the spoon is brief possession; the flavour she held is worth more to her than its name on the list. A declined taste is taken back without comment.
- **Decision fatigue** (BE 14): she arrives after a day of decisions; two spoons, not eight; the anchor is a default she can accept or decline.
- **Defaults** (BE 6): no default size, no default add-on. The only default in the visit is the Note, and it is not a product.
- **Friction** (BE 10): time added where the category removes it; "Take your time" is the friction, said once.
- **Not used**: loss aversion ("last scoop of the batch"), social proof as a sales line ("everyone takes…"). Guardrails 1.

## RECOVERY

| If … | → |
| --- | --- |
| Long queue, one on the counter, she is slow | The tasting continues at her pace; the next guest gets eyes up (M5). Named degradation at peak: the second station is the fix, not a faster guest. R-QUEUE. |
| She hurries herself | "Pistachio it is." No taste pushed; the Note still goes (M15). |
| She dislikes a taste | No line. The spoon to the discard dish; the other flavour offered. |
| She asks about gluten | "The gelato and the tiramisu are gluten-free; the Lab checks cross-contact." (fact sheet 2026-10-30; written Lab confirmation to be filed). |
| She asks about any other allergen | The allergen policy (CONFIRM) or "I'll ask Menelaos." and he answers. Never a guess. R-ALLERGEN. |
| She asks about sugar for a health reason | The fact only: "No added sugar in the Mindful line." No advice. If it becomes a medical question, Menelaos (guardrails 11). R-ESC. |
| She picks an Aperitivo flavour and may be under age | The age line (CONFIRM) before the spoon. R-AGE. |
| The suggested flavour is sold out | Said plainly, once; the next flavour tied to her answer; no apology stack. |
| She cannot decide and declines the anchor | "No hurry." Then silence. She may leave without ordering; the Note goes with every order (v3 §5.4), so without one there is no Note, and the farewell (M22) still comes, by name if known. |
