# M09 · HIGH IMPACT · The answer, or none

In-room · Choosing together (phase 3) · ~10 sec · Counter · owner: floor

Dominant inputs: **brand** and **non-verbal**; BE (framing, friction removed from declining); concept light.

## CURRENT STATE

The beat (M8) has passed. She says it, hesitates, names something small, or shrugs. "I can say it, or not. Either is fine."

## DO

1. A win: the face lights, the smile reaches the eyes, intensity 3, never party energy; one line back that uses her own word, then the choosing.
2. A small thing ("Friday", "just because"): received as a complete answer, the same warmth, the same line.
3. Nothing, a shrug: warm, no second try; the "nothing" line; the Note still comes (v3 §7.6).
4. Something painful (a loss, a diagnosis, a job gone): intensity 2, the "sorry" line; no advice, no follow-up questions; if it touches a guardrails 11 topic and she needs anything beyond the order, Menelaos.
5. A group answering at once: each received, one at a time, eyes to each.
6. Nothing written down in front of her; the names habit is a Stage 4 method and no guest record is kept without consent (guardrails 12).
7. The answer is held for the farewell: "Small wins count." is available at M22 only when there was one.

## SAY

**LOCKED**:

| Case | Line |
| --- | --- |
| A moment shared | "Then let's choose something that tastes like it." / „Тогава да изберем нещо, което има вкус на това." |
| Nothing | "Then let's just choose something good." / „Тогава просто да изберем нещо хубаво." |
| Painful | "I'm sorry. Let's choose something good for today." / „Съжалявам. Да изберем нещо хубаво за днес." |

Filter test: to her, no hurry, nothing sold. All **pass**.

## DON'T

- "Come on, there must be something"; "Really? Nothing?"; repeating the question; a visible drop in warmth; a joke about a bad day.
- "Oh that's AMAZING"; "Congratulations!!!" energy; "wow".
- Writing it down; repeating it to a colleague aloud; posting it (only with a signed release, M25).
- "Most people say…".

## NON-VERBAL

Face per the expression palette (Bible §9: shared joy, intensity capped at 3); eyes on her; tone one notch warmer for a win, one notch lower for a loss (NV B.1); a half-beat of silence before the response so the answer is heard (NV B.4); hands still.

## BEHAVIORAL ECONOMICS

Framing (BE 9): her answer is handed back as a flavour to find, which turns a confession into a choice. Affect (BE 5): her first emotional exchange with a person here. Friction removed from declining (BE 10): "nothing" costs her nothing and loses her nothing.

## RECOVERY

| If … | → |
| --- | --- |
| The answer touches illness, injury, a staff member, a legal matter | No counsel, no draft; the "sorry" line, the order, the Note; Menelaos if she needs anything more. R-ESC. |
| A child answers for the group | Received with the same face; then the adult. |
| The answer is in a language the team member does not follow | The smile, the "nothing" line, the choosing. |

**State arc:** Deciding whether to say it → Said, or not → Choosing

**Success metric:** On sampled orders, no second ask is heard; "nothing" answers receive the same tasting and the Note; the farewell uses the answer where there was one.

**Target emotion:** received

**Brand pillar:** Notice the moment; core message blocks 2–3
