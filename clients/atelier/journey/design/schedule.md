## Format decision

**Full house layout, not the small-format variant.** The counter exchange (M06–M19) is under five minutes and often has the next guest present, which is the small-format condition; but Rakovski has a room, chairs and a 5–15 minute pause (M21–M23), the map carries 33 moments with a separate after-visit and partner phase, and Stage 4 trains from these pages. The counter's one small-format problem, the end and the next threshold on one face in the same seconds, is designed as one composition on M26 and in `choreography.md` § 1.2. Ring Mall moments are written as "the same ritual, in a queue" (handoff B1); if Evo answers open-facts 9 otherwise, the mall becomes a second set.
