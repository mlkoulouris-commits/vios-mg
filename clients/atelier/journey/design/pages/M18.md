# M18 · CRITICAL IMPACT · The Note drawn and handed ◉

**Draft — book edition v1.0 unsigned (open-facts 39); observation move not held.**

Object + in-room · Phase 4 Marked · ~10–15 sec · Both counters (Ring Mall jar and cards: open-facts 43) · **◉ peak** · weight provisional, brand-led

Dominant inputs: **brand** (differentiator, message 5, story block 6) · **BE** (peak-end, defaults, endowment) · **non-verbal** (handling of objects, eye behaviour). Concept consulted: the jar, the 25 printed cards, the order card-before-till.

## CURRENT STATE

She has the cup, cone or tiramisu in her hand (M17), made this morning; she has said what today is, or said "nothing" (M11). The till is next (M19), then the pause (M21–M24). This is the one thing she carries out of the door.

## DO

1. **Counter** (the team member who asked the question): the moment her order is in her hand, before the till, turn to the jar. Never after payment.
2. Draw one card **blind**: hand into the jar, eyes on her, not on the jar. Do not read it, turn it, or swap it.
3. Hold the card by its edges, face toward her, and offer it across the counter at her chest height.
4. Say the line. Let go when she takes it. Half a step back, one beat, then move to the till.
5. Her hands full: lay the card face up on the counter in front of her, squared to her, on the clean side, and say the line.
6. Takeaway or bag: the card goes into her hand, not into the bag. If she wants it in the bag, she puts it there.
7. Box of twelve (M20): one card into her hand as above; the card inside the box is separate (M20).
8. Room: nothing changes. No bell, no music cue, no announcement. The jar on the counter is the only staging.

## SAY

**LOCKED** (Bible §7; not new):

| | EN | BG |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Default | "A card for your moment. It's drawn by chance." | „Картичка за твоя момент. Теглим ги на късмет." |

Registers: **"nothing" guest** — the same words; the card is still for her moment. **Child with a parent** — the same line, to the child; the card to the child. **Guest draws** — only if open-facts 28 answers that the guest draws today: no line is locked until then (**CONFIRM**).

Filter test: hurry no · sell no · no one in particular no → **pass**. After the line, nothing. No comment on which card it is (M22).

## DON'T

- Don't choose the card, read it first, or swap it for a "better" one.
- Don't drop it in the bag, under the cup, or hand it with the receipt or the change.
- Don't explain it ("there are 25, collect them all"). Collecting makes it a game and a loyalty scheme.
- Don't skip it because there is a queue.
- Don't perform it: no flourish, no "ta-da", no raised voice.
- Don't touch her hand.

## NON-VERBAL

Hands: one hand, edges only, face toward her (NV D.3). The card crosses the counter at her chest height, not slid flat across it (NV A.1). Eyes: on her hands as she takes it, then her eyes for the line (NV C.2; Bible §9 "at the guest's hands, then eyes"). Face: still, small smile, intensity 2 of 5. Voice: lower than the question, unhurried, falling at the end (NV B.1, B.2, B.3). Timing: line, beat, release (NV E.3). No touch (NV D.2).

## BEHAVIORAL ECONOMICS

- **BE 7 peak-end** (evidence tier): the remembering self keeps this and the end; the card is unexpected (she asked for gelato), physical (it leaves with her) and hers.
- **BE 6 defaults**, used in her favour: chance, not choice, removes the suspicion that the card is a sales pick.
- **BE 12 endowment**: a card handed to her by hand becomes hers; a card in the bag skips the handing that makes it hers.
- **BE 1 cognitive ease**: nothing to decide here; zero load at the peak.
- **Peak placement test (S3-05):** if the visit ended here, would it feel complete? **Yes**: she was asked, she holds what she chose and something for her day. What follows (till, pause, door) must not take it back. Passed on paper; provisional until the observation move (open-facts 40).

## RECOVERY

- If the jar is empty → **R-NOTES**.
- If the card drops or creases → draw another, blind; the dropped one is not handed.
- If she says "no thanks" → nod, put it back, no second offer (**R-M18**).
- If it was forgotten and she is still at the till → hand it now, with the line, before she pays; if she is out of the door → let her go, no chase, log it (**R-M18**).
- If the card reads wrong for her day (she said it was a hard day) → no comment, no swap in front of her; the text set is checked in advance (open-facts 53; **R-M18**).
