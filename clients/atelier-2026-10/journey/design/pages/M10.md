# M10 · HIGH IMPACT · A flavour for the moment

In-room · Choosing together (phase 3) · ~15 sec · Counter, across the display · owner: floor

Dominant inputs: **brand**, **concept** (the menu, today's batch) and **BE** (choice architecture); non-verbal (gesture, gaze).

## CURRENT STATE

Answered or not (M9); the display in front of her. "They're choosing with me."

## DO

1. One or two flavours named, tied to her answer where there is one, to today's batch where there is not; each with one true line: the origin as the fact sheet has it, "made this morning" only when true that day.
2. Named plainly, with one open-hand gesture per flavour toward the tray (NV C.3): "Bronte pistachio, made this morning. Or the hazelnut from Piedmont."
3. Then the tasting offer (M11).
4. A regular: the locked regular's line.
5. Lines with rules: Mindful is "no added sugar"; Aperitivo is named as alcoholic (M13).
6. Celebration box (12): the locked box line; more time, same welcome, no upsell language (Bible §6).
7. Tiramisu: gelato first, then tiramisu, or together if she prefers (M12). One offer.

## SAY

**LOCKED**:

| Case | Line |
| --- | --- |
| Regular | "Pistachio again, or something new today?" / „Пак шамфъстък или нещо ново днес?" |
| Celebration box | "Who are we celebrating? We'll choose the twelve together." / „Кого празнуваме? Ще изберем дванайсетте заедно." |
| Default pattern | "[Flavour], [one true line]. Or [flavour]." |

Filter test: all **pass**.

## DON'T

- Reciting the display; more than two named at once; choosing for her.
- "Bestseller", "our famous", "most popular", "healthy", "natural", "organic"; adjectives on a flavour; an origin not on the fact sheet (guardrails 9).
- "Made this morning" when it was not.

## NON-VERBAL

One gesture per name, open hand, not a point (NV C.3); eyes to the flavour, then to her (Bible §9 showing a flavour: quiet pride, intensity 2); tone plain, falling (NV B.1).

## BEHAVIORAL ECONOMICS

Choice architecture (BE 8): two from sixteen, tied to her. Anchoring (BE 3): the first flavour named is the anchor, so it is the one that fits her answer best. Framing (BE 9): "tastes like it". No defaults on size or add-ons (BE 6).

## RECOVERY

| If … | → |
| --- | --- |
| The named flavour is sold out | Said plainly once; the next one tied to her answer. |
| She asks "what's good?" | Two flavours tied to today's batch; never "everything". |
| She asks a price | The price, plainly (list **CONFIRM**). |

**State arc:** Open → Narrowed to two → Ready to taste

**Success metric:** On sampled orders, at most two flavours named before the tasting, one true line each, no forbidden words; the pre-shift sheet names today's made-this-morning flavours.

**Target emotion:** guided, as by a peer

**Brand pillar:** differentiator step two (choose together); core message block 8
