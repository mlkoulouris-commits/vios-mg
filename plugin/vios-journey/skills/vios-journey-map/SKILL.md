# Vios Journey Map

Stage 2. The map translates the brand into the moments where it has to act, what it has to do there, and which moments carry disproportionate weight in memory. It is a one- or two-page strategic instrument, not an emotion diagram and not a touchpoint inventory. Hard lines: `vios-core:vios-guardrails` 10 (the founder signs the map), 11.

## Read first

- `../../references/stage-2-journey-map.md`, the chapter verbatim: six moves, the five-band template, patterns S2-01 to S2-05, the harvest. This skill applies it; it does not restate it.
- `[client]/brand-book.md` and `brand-book-vN.md`: the six intake elements of § 2.2.2.1, read in this order and held throughout: audience persona (all layers), Brand Role and Connection statement, brand archetypal mix, the eight Personality Expression dimensions, differentiator and positioning, core messages and story framework. No brand book, or a book without a Role and Connection statement: stop, `vios-brand` first. A map built on a brand the practice has not internalised maps to nothing.
- `[client]/fact-sheet.md`: the operation as it is (hours, service model, reservation route, the room).
- `../../references/section-3-3-three-registers.md`: the map is written in the operational register and points forward to floor scripts; it never contains them.

## Move 0 — Intake summary

One page: the six elements in the practice's words, dated, with the brand book edition. Opens `[client]/journey/` as the Stage 2 file. If any element is thin, say so here; the map inherits the gap.

## Move 1 — Establish the arc

Four to eight phases, named for this brand's customer, not the category (pattern S2-01). The arc starts before arrival (decision, booking) and ends after departure (post-visit) when the brand owns those phases. Mark each phase's share of the experience. Test: do the phases produce different moments, or only different names?

## Move 2 — Identify the moments

Walk each phase and ask three questions: what does the customer do, what does the brand do, what is at stake if either does it badly. Every answer that is not "nothing" is a moment. Capture operational moments and brand moments, all of them, in sequence, with the customer's action and the brand's action, coded M1, M2 … Twenty to fifty is normal. Routine moments (coat, water, bathroom) are mapped, not skipped (pattern S2-04).

## Move 3 — Assign weight

HIGH, MEDIUM or LOW for every moment, from four sources, named per moment:

1. **Peak-end and duration neglect** (`vios-core:vios-be-lens`, Two Selves): name the peak and the end; both HIGH by default.
2. **Moments of decision**: order, drink, dessert, stay, return; usually HIGH.
3. **The brand's own logic**: where the differentiator becomes visible, where the Role is enacted (a Defender's threshold, a bar-led brand's every bar moment).
4. **The threshold thin-slice**: the first seconds inside are HIGH in every hospitality journey, whatever their length.

Restraint rule (pattern S2-02): three to seven HIGH out of the inventory. More than that and there is no hierarchy; renegotiate.

## Move 4 — Place the brand at each moment

Four fields per moment, in the operational register:

| Field | Draws on | Form |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Brand intention | Connection statement, core messages, the moment's place in the story framework | one sentence: what the guest should feel, understand or experience |
| Role-in-moment | Brand Role | one phrase: how a Guide / Defender / Peer / Leader / Inspiration shows up here |
| Dominant expression dimension | the eight dimensions | one or two: appearance, dress, accessories, language, tone, volume, attitudes, beliefs |
| Customer target state | persona before-state → after-state | one phrase locating this moment on that arc |

HIGH moments get fuller text; LOW moments one line. Every placement derives from the brand book; a placement the book does not support is rewritten or the book is questioned.

## Move 5 — Name the silent moments

Where the brand deliberately does nothing: between two HIGH moments (pattern S2-03), during private activity (eating, talking, reading the menu), in transitions (the walk, the wait between courses). Silent is not LOW: LOW still acts a little, silent acts not at all. Mark each with its reason; Stage 3 receives them as instructions.

## Move 6 — Close the map

Five questions, each yes or no with the fix: arc specific to this customer · all moments named · real hierarchy in the weights · every placement from the brand book · silences read as choices, not gaps. Then write the **Reasoning Note** (pattern S2-05): by phase, why the peak is where it is, why each HIGH is HIGH, why each silence is silent, where the arc departs from the category. Four to eight pages, read once by the founder, then archived.

## Output

1. `[client]/journey/journey-map.md`: the five bands as tables. Band 1 arc (phase, share); band 2 moments (code, phase, name in 3–4 words, customer action, brand action); band 3 weight (tier, peak ★, end ◆); band 4 the four placement fields; band 5 silent moments with reason. One or two pages when rendered. The Claude Doc version when the docs connector is present, landscape, readable at a glance by a founder or a designer.
2. `[client]/journey/reasoning-note.md`.
3. `[client]/journey/handoff-to-design.md`: where Stage 3 has latitude and where it is constrained (§ 2.2.6).
4. **Bridge to marketing**: update `[client]/brand-book.md` › Journey so the canonical touchpoints (`vios-core:vios-guardrails` › Journey) point at their moment codes, the peak is marked, and the guest state per touchpoint is copied from band 4. `vios-content-calendar`, `vios-persona-voice` and `vios-persona-builder` read that table; the full map stays in `journey/`.
5. Brief revisions, if the map constrains the room: architect or interior-designer brief revision, versioned and dated (§ 2.2.6).
6. Harvest note (§ 2.2.7): arc patterns, moment patterns, template refinements, peak-end placements, in `[client]/archive/harvest-stage-2.md`.

End with: phase count, moment count, HIGH count, the peak and the end named, the silences, and the next step (`vios-journey-design`). The founder signs the Reasoning Note before design starts.

## Example (brewery, band 4 for one HIGH moment; placeholders)

**M14 First pour explained** · phase: making · HIGH · intention: what you are drinking was made here, together with what you will eat, on purpose · role-in-moment: Guide alongside, one sentence then step back · dominant dimensions: language and vocabulary, attitudes · target state: curious but casual, moving from "is this real?" toward "someone thought about this". Silent moment S3 follows: the first sip, no staff presence for two minutes.
