# M31 · HIGH IMPACT · The story card at the partner's table

Object, at one remove · The partner table (phase 8) · the partner's service · owner: the partner (a brief, not a script) · customer: the partner's guest, unknown to the brand

Dominant inputs: **brand** (the question travels) and **BE** (framing); concept (the partner's service); non-verbal through the object only.

## CURRENT STATE

A guest the brand never meets is served a tiramisu or a gelato at a café in Sofia; a card is with it. "Someone far from here asked about my day."

## DO (a brief to the partner)

1. The card arrives with the dessert, on the plate or beside it, text up. Not under the plate, not in a stand among flyers.
2. The card text (v3 §4.8 pattern, 25–40 words): "What are we celebrating today? This tiramisu was made fresh this morning at Atelier del Gelato in Sofia. Take a moment for yours." Only when true that day; the Lab's date on the delivery note is the check.
3. Optional, agreed per partner (M29): the server asks the question aloud, in "we". If not, the card asks.
4. The partner does not edit the card, add an offer, or cover it.
5. A guest who asks about the atelier is given the address and hours (card back, print **to check**).

## SAY

The card. Optionally the spoken question, M07's locked line. **Pass.**

## DON'T

- A card with "visit us" pressure, a QR to a promotion, the partner's own slogan on it.
- The partner's staff pitching ("it's from Atelier, you should go").
- A card under the plate.

## BEHAVIORAL ECONOMICS

Framing (BE 9): a dessert reframed as a moment of the guest's day. The brand is a guide at one remove; any social proof for the atelier is incidental, never engineered.

## RECOVERY

The brand cannot recover at this table. It observes: a quarterly unannounced visit by Menelaos to each partner (R-PARTNER), and acts at M29 and M30.

**State arc:** Served → Reads the question → Pauses, or not

**Success metric:** Quarterly partner visits: the card on the table as agreed, yes or no per partner; mentions of a partner card in enquiries or posts (raw count).

**Target emotion:** paused

**Brand pillar:** differentiator; Vision; story block 4 (meeting the guide, by card)
