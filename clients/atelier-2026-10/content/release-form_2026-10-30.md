# Съгласие за използване на образ / Likeness release — Atelier del Gelato

Guardrails 7: written, time-limited, says what happens when the person leaves. One form, two versions (guest, team member). Print on the atelier's paper, sign on the spot, photograph the signed sheet and file it in Drive › clients › atelier › consents as `YYYY-MM-DD_<name>_<guest|staff>.jpg`. No minors without a guardian's signature. Not legal advice: have Evo's accountant or lawyer glance at it once before first use.
