# M20 · STANDARD · Space to sit

In-room · The ten minutes (phase 5) · 5–15 min · The tables · owner: floor · dominant dimensions: sound, attitudes

## CURRENT STATE

She sits with it. "Nobody's hurrying me."

## DO

1. The room holds: music low enough to talk over, 20–22 °C, warm light; water for the palate within reach on the table or the counter (v3 §7.7).
2. Nobody clears while she sits; nobody wipes her table; nobody asks "everything all right?".
3. A second guest at the next table is served at the counter, not across her.
4. If she catches an eye: a nod, nothing more. If she asks for something: it comes, with one line at most.
5. She is never hinted to leave; the cup is cleared only after she has gone (M23).

## DON'T

- Clearing, wiping, hovering, checking; "Can I get you anything else?"; turning the music up; stacking chairs in sight near closing.

**Success metric:** On sampled sit-downs, no staff approach to the table unprompted; cups cleared only after the guest has left. Filter: not applicable.
