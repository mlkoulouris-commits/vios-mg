# M08 · SILENT · The beat

In-room · The threshold (phase 2) · 2–3 sec, longer if she needs it · Counter · ○

## CURRENT STATE

The question has just been asked (M7). She is deciding whether to say it. The silence is hers.

## DO

Nothing. M7 did the work: the eyes, the line, the "we". The team member stays where M7 left them: squared to her, one step back from the counter edge, hands empty and still, eyes on her and soft, face open. Nothing is picked up, nothing is looked at, nothing is said. The beat ends when she speaks, shrugs, or looks down at the display; then M9.

Where the team member is while not acting: at the station, in front of her, not drifting to the till or the next guest.

## DON'T

What would break the silence:

- Repeating the question, or rephrasing it ("What's the occasion?").
- Supplying an answer ("Friday, right?", "a birthday, maybe?").
- Filling it with a flavour ("the pistachio is lovely today").
- Looking at the queue, the display or the till while she thinks.
- A laugh, a "no pressure", a "take your time" (that line belongs to M11, not here).
- Reaching for the scoop.

**Why silent (Reasoning Note §9):** the question is a gift only if the answer is not demanded. Filling the beat turns it into a greeting formula. The persona's own rule is "asks before she tells"; the beat is where she does not tell.

**Success metric:** On sampled orders, the team member does not speak again until the guest has spoken or looked away.
