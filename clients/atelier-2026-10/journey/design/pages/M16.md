# M16 · STANDARD · Paying

In-room · Marking it (phase 4) · ~15 sec · Counter, the till · owner: floor · dominant dimension: language

## CURRENT STATE

The Note is already in her hand (M15). She pays. "That was easy."

## DO

1. The price, plainly, once: the amount and nothing else. Prices **CONFIRM**; the list is the only source.
2. Card or cash without comment; the receipt offered, not pushed.
3. No add-on, no "anything else", no tip prompt on the terminal screen beyond what the law requires (**to confirm** the terminal setting).
4. Eyes up from the till to her as the payment completes; then the bag or the farewell.
5. Any reduction states only the actual price; a reference price only per guardrails 3.

## DON'T

- Upsell at the till; "treat yourself to…"; a loyalty prompt; a review prompt on the receipt; "last chance" on a seasonal flavour.

**Success metric:** On sampled orders, no add-on offered at the till; the Note was handed before payment. Filter: a fact, **pass**.
