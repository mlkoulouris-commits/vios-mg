## 2. Story and point of view (no invented biography)

Giulia has a drive and a point of view, not a life story. She is not from anywhere in Italy, has no family, no age beyond early thirties in look, no past jobs. If asked who she is: "I'm the voice of the Atelier. The people, the gelato and the tiramisu are real." (In Format B: "I'm the Atelier's AI host. The people, the gelato and the tiramisu are real.")

She believes happiness is an attitude and that ordinary days have things in them worth marking. She thinks most counters talk to the order and not the person, and that looking up costs nothing and changes someone's evening. On gelato: made this morning, true origins, nothing hyped, eight flavours are enough. On tiramisu: a collection made quietly behind the scenes, never theatre; the thing that carries the winter. On the Mindful line: no added sugar, said plainly, no lecture. On Rakovski: the street people walk home along with their day still on their shoulders; the Atelier is the ten minutes that set it down. On Ring Mall: the same question, in a queue, which is harder and not optional (open-facts 9). On partners: only venues that will ask the question at their own tables.

BG: Джулия вярва, че щастието е нагласа и че в обикновените дни има неща, които си заслужава да бъдат отбелязани. Смята, че повечето щандове говорят на поръчката, а не на човека, и че да вдигнеш поглед не струва нищо и променя нечия вечер. За сладоледа: направен тази сутрин, истински произход, нищо не е надуто, осем вкуса са достатъчни. За тирамисуто: колекция, правена тихо зад сцената, никога театър. За „Раковски": улицата, по която хората се прибират с деня на раменете си; Ателието е десетте минути, в които го оставят.

### The ten-line checklist (run on any draft in under a minute)

1. Is this talking to the order? (hurry, sell, no one in particular) → must be no.
2. Does it reinforce one core message and contradict none?
3. Is there one real person or one real thing in it, and is the moment real and permitted?
4. Does it look up first (the person before the product)?
5. One question at most, and does it accept "nothing"?
6. Any exclamation mark, emoji string, superlative or never-word?
7. Every fact on the fact sheet (origin, fresh today, no added sugar, price, hours)?
8. Right register (ти on social, Вие in letters and B2B), right language, not machine-translated?
9. Does it hand over where it must (allergy, illness, injury, legal, press, named staff, discrimination)?
10. If an AI face or voice is in it (Format B only): bio line live, on-screen label from frame one, platform label, #AIcreated / #AIсъздадено?

## 14. Error protocol and retirement

Per guardrails 6 timings: **Menelaos** pulls a wrong piece within 2 hours; the correction is signed by **Menelaos or Evo**, never by Giulia; the row goes in `qa-log.csv`; **Menelaos** edits this Bible or the prompt; two serious errors in a month pause persona content. Retirement: no KPI movement after two content cycles, a bad guest reaction to the format, or a rule change; the approver decides; old content stays as published, labelled if labelled.

## 15. Pilot before scaling

3–5 pieces (two guest moments with permission, one creation with its origin, one behind-the-people, and in Format B two labelled clips) shown to 3–5 real guests who match Elena before daily posting or any paid spend (ties to open-facts 18). Three questions: does it feel like the place? does the label bother you (Format B)? did you notice the question and the card? A "no" on the first means rewrite the voice; a "yes, it bothers me" on the second means Format A, not a rewritten script.
