# M09 · CRITICAL IMPACT · "What are we celebrating today?" ★

**Draft — book edition v1.0 unsigned (open-facts 39); observation move not held.**

In-room · Phase 3 The question · ~3 sec · Both counters (Ring Mall: the ritual as practised there is unverified, open-facts 9, 43) · **★ signature** · weight provisional, brand-led

Dominant inputs: **brand** (differentiator, message 5, the editorial line) · **BE** (a decision for her: answer or not) · **non-verbal** (voice, eyes, the beat). Concept consulted: the case between them, the queue, closing time.

## CURRENT STATE

She has been seen (M06) and has read the case in silence (M08). She looks up from the case, or steps to the counter. She is about to order and expects "What can I get you?". The question she gets instead is the brand's identity, before she has received anything.

## DO

1. **Counter**, the team member who will serve her: when she looks up from the case or steps to the counter, face her, eyes on hers.
2. Ask the question, whole, every order, both counters, every daypart.
3. Then nothing: the beat (M10). Face warm and resting, eyes on her, hands still.
4. **She orders first** ("a pistachio, please"): repeat the order, then ask the question before the scoop (register below).
5. **A group or a pair:** ask once, to all of them, eyes moving across; take the first answer that comes.
6. **Ring Mall line:** "Thanks for waiting." first, then the question in the same breath (M07). Never shortened for the line.
7. **Box buyer** (M20): the question first; "who's it for?" comes after her answer.

## DON'T

- Don't shorten it ("Celebrating?", "Anything to celebrate?") or add to it ("…or just ice cream?").
- Don't ask it to the till, the scoop or the queue behind her.
- Don't ask it as a slogan: same pace as any ordinary question, no stress on "celebrating".
- Don't explain the ritual or that "we ask everyone".
- Don't skip it for regulars, for a queue, or at closing time.
- Don't follow it with a second question before she answers.

## NON-VERBAL

Voice: ordinary speaking pace, warm, a natural rise at the end of a question, no performance (NV B.1, B.2). Volume: for her, not for the room (NV B.3). Eyes: on hers for the question and the beat (NV C.2). Hands: still, off the scoop and the till (NV C.3). Body: square to her across the case, the case between, not leaning over (NV A.1, C.4). Then the silence (NV B.4; M10).

## BEHAVIORAL ECONOMICS

- **A decision for her:** answer, or not. Both are right. The design keeps the cost of either at zero: the question is ordinary (BE 1 cognitive ease), the beat gives her time (BE 14, no load added), and "nothing" is pre-accepted at M11.
- **BE 9 framing**: "we" frames the order as a shared marking, not a transaction; "today" makes it about her day, not the product.
- **BE 11 social proof**, refused: no "everyone tells us", no examples of other guests' answers.

## RECOVERY

- If the question was skipped and she is still at the counter → ask it now, before the scoop or the card, plainly, no "oh, I forgot" (**R-M09**).
- If it was said as a slogan or shortened → nothing to her; the lead corrects at the pre-shift (**R-M09**).
- If she looks lost, as if she misheard → say it again, slower, the same words; never rephrase (**R-M09**).
- A line at either counter → **R-QUEUE**. Closing → **R-CLOSE**.
