## CURRENT STATE

She has left. She may write a review, or not. Guardrails 2: every guest is asked the same way, never rewarded, never gated on satisfaction.

## DO

1. **The ask is printed, not spoken**, so it is the same for every guest and does not take the end (M25): one line on a small card at each till, seen at payment (M19). Approval and placement: **CONFIRM** (open-facts 57).
2. Not on the Atelier Note (the Note stays only hers), not at the door, not in the goodbye.
3. **Replies:** marketing drafts, **Menelaos signs**, by name, every review, within a week; escalation topics no draft (**R-ESC**).
4. A negative review: plain, one acknowledgement, the offer to talk privately; no defence (**R-UPSET**).
5. Count reviews that mention the question, a card or being noticed (raw count monthly; Bible §3).

## SAY

Printed line at the till, **NEW**, **not lockable until** the listing name is fixed (open-facts 23) and the approver signs (57):

| | EN | BG |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Till card | "We read every review. Google: Atelier del Gelato." | „Четем всеки отзив. Google: Atelier del Gelato." |

Review replies: written voice, signed "Menelaos"; never Giulia. Filter test on the till line: hurry no · sell no · no one in particular no (a plain fact to whoever reads) → **pass**; logged in the Stage 3 block.

## DON'T

- No reward, discount or draw for a review; no "5 stars?" (guardrails 2).
- No asking only the happy guests; no QR that routes unhappy guests elsewhere (gating).
- No spoken ask at the counter or the door.
- No reply in persona; no reply on an escalation topic without Menelaos (guardrails 11).
- No "Baci" on anything printed (open-facts 23, 24).

## BEHAVIORAL ECONOMICS

House doctrine over BE 11 social proof: reviews are earned and asked plainly, never engineered. A printed line read at the till is a cue, not pressure (one CTA, said once).

## RECOVERY

A review raising an escalation topic → **R-ESC**. A wrong reply published → Menelaos corrects it, signed, within 2 hours of noticing.
