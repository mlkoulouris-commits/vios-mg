## 1. Where the skill was unclear

1. **"Audience persona (all layers)" and the chapter's "all four layers".** The book has demographics, psychographics, hospitality attitudes, an archetypal profile and a three-layer emotional transformation. Which four are meant is not defined anywhere I could read. I counted the book's persona as complete and said so in the intake. One line in Move 0 naming the four layers would remove the guess.
2. **"No brand book, or a book without a Role and Connection statement: stop."** The book has an explicit Role (Guide, § 5.4 block 5) but no Connection statement in Stage 1 form. The rule reads as both-or-stop. I proceeded on the Role and derived a Connection from § 1.1 and § 2.2, flagged in the intake, the Reasoning Note close and `open-facts.md` row 3. The skill should say whether a derivable Connection statement is enough or whether this is a hard stop back to `vios-brand`.
3. **Move 2b when the venue trades but nobody has observed it.** The skill's only "no observation" case is "remote engagement, no venue yet". Stochna is open (about 1.2k Google reviews), so observation is possible and simply has not happened. I treated it as the no-observation case (weights provisional, every HIGH brand-led) and made the observation a blocking row for the stage, which the skill does not say to do. It should say whether the map can be written before observation at all when the venue is open, or whether Move 2b is a prerequisite that stops the skill.
4. **The re-weighting date** is "thirty to sixty days after opening or after the design goes live". The venue opened long ago and the design has no date. I set "design live + 45 days", held by the account manager. The skill could say which anchor wins for an operating venue.
5. **House form from a worked deliverable I was not allowed to read.** The form is described in prose ("one table per phase", columns, markers, silent rows "carry code, name, ○ in weight and marker, and nothing else"). Points I had to decide: whether the marker column of a silent row holds ○ (I put ○ in weight and marker, as the sentence says); whether "LOW rows keep the intention to twelve words" applies to the intention only (yes); what "share of the experience" means for phases outside the visit (I wrote "before the visit" / "after the visit" and gave shares of a 90–120 minute visit for the rest). A one-screen skeleton of the house form in the skill would make the blind run match the worked deliverable without reading it.
6. **Codes.** The chapter says M1, M2; the skill says two-digit from the start. Followed the skill.
7. **Conditional and standing moments.** Five moments happen at some tables only (a constraint questioned, a question about the making, a late friend, something goes wrong, the return) and four run across a phase (making in view, conversation, reading the table, room sound). Neither the chapter nor the skill says how to carry them; band 2 positions moments at a point in time. I flagged them in the moment name and the inventory. Recorded as a template refinement candidate in the harvest.
8. **The bridge step** says "the guest state per touchpoint is copied from the placement table". The existing `brand-book.md` column was "Guest state and tension" (the tension from the micro-message blocks). I replaced it with the target states and dropped the tension (it still lives in the micro-message block table below). Not sure that is what the step intends; the alternative is two columns.
9. **Owner column for the trip phase.** "Pre-arrival and post-visit are marketing, in-venue phases are floor." The trip (M06, the street and the door) is neither; the sign and the window are the venue's, so I wrote floor. The return (M36) is after the visit but acted on the floor; I wrote marketing with "(M36 floor)".
10. **The skill's worked example is a brewery** ("M14 First pour explained · ★ · pillar: made together"), two lines from the moment I needed to choose a signature for a brewery. I derived M17 from the book (message 1 "where this message lives", block 3's spent-grain sentence, the tagline) and the choice would stand without the example, but the example primed it and the Reasoning Note cannot prove otherwise. For a blind test of a brewery client the example is a contaminant; a non-brewery example, or a note that the example is a placeholder from no client, would be cleaner.
11. **Section 3.1 for peak-end.** The skill points to `vios-core:vios-be-lens` for peak-end and the two selves. The task did not list the lens and the guardrails give a stand-in (the five-line version plus the evidence tiers). I used the chapter's own peak-end paragraph and the guardrails' tiers, did not load the lens. The skill could say whether loading the lens is part of Move 3 or optional.
12. **"Brief revisions, if the map constrains the room"** and **`journey/README.md`** for client-facing editions: neither produced. The handoff states that no brief revision is issued and why; the README is for editions the account manager asks for. Fine as written; noting that a blind reader could think both are required outputs.

## 3. Decisions taken without guidance

1. Proceeded past the Role-and-Connection stop (above, item 2).
2. Wrote the map before any observation and made observation a condition of signing rather than of writing.
3. Mapped the evening arc only; declared the weekend day arc and the events line as maps to come.
4. Seven phases, with the trip as a one-moment phase. The chapter allows four to eight; the test (different moments, not different names) passes, but a single-moment phase may be against the house grain.
5. The end as two moments (bill, farewell) designed as one. The skill allows it ("may be two moments"); the choice is mine.
6. Six HIGH. Five would have left the bill MEDIUM; I weighed the book's "transactional ending" failure mode as reason enough for end-1 to be HIGH.
7. Silence immediately after the threshold (M10) and after the end (M32): the chapter's three silence locations are between peaks, private activity, transitions. Both fit "transition", but the pattern name (S2-03, between peaks) is stretched for M10; I called it a variant.
8. Five conditional and four standing moments included in the count of 36. A reader expecting 36 sequential points will find 27 plus 9 that are not points.
9. Target states for conditional moments were written as the guest's sentence after the brand acted ("They fixed it and the evening carried on"), not as a before→after pair; the pair form was kept for the HIGH moments only.
10. In the bridge table, "Designed moment to show" was rewritten to match the map (silences named, lines from the book) rather than left as the Stage 1 transcription.
11. No `journey/README.md`, no Claude Doc edition, no BG redraft: all are "when the account manager asks".
12. The hand-over note for the sibling sessions was written to the shared notes folder, outside the repo.
