# Recovery — Atelier del Gelato

Stage 3, § 2.3.2.6. By failure scenario, not by moment; the pages reference these codes. Written 2026-11-01. Operational register; the lines in floor register, filter-tested (*Is this processing the guest?*).

**The brand's recovery character (pattern S3-07).** A Peer, Lover 70 / Innocent 30, recovers the way a friend does: it says what happened in one plain sentence, fixes it, and does not keep apologising. No over-apology (which pins her to the failure and reads as anxious, which the Innocent is not); no under-acknowledgement (which reads as the transaction the brand refuses). The ritual itself is the brand's apology: a guest who has been let down still gets the question, the time and the Note. Compensation is never offered at the counter by the team member; if any is due, Menelaos decides it afterwards and offers it privately. Escalation topics (guardrails 11) end with Menelaos taking over, never with a script.

Every HIGH moment has a recovery here: M5 (R-ROOM, R-QUEUE, R-UPSET), M7 (R-M9, R-ESC), M11 (R-QUEUE, R-ALLERGEN, R-AGE, R-WRONG-ORDER), M15 (R-M15), M22 and M23 (R-M22/23, R-UPSET).

## R-M9 — No answer, or a hard one

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Trigger | She shrugs, says "nothing", or answers with something painful. |
| Do | Warmth unchanged; no second try; move to choosing; the Note still goes. For a painful answer, intensity 2, no advice, no follow-up questions. |
| Say | "Then let's just choose something good." / „Тогава просто да изберем нещо хубаво." — or, for a hard answer: "I'm sorry. Let's choose something good for today." / „Съжалявам. Да изберем нещо хубаво за днес." |
| Inform | Nobody, unless the answer touches a guardrails 11 topic and she needs anything beyond the order: then R-ESC. |
| Guest is left | Having been asked and not pressed; with the same tasting and the same card as anyone. |

## R-M15 — The Note goes wrong

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Trigger | The jar is empty; a card is damaged; the card was forgotten and she is paying or leaving; she leaves the card behind. |
| Do | Empty jar: a card from the reserve under the station, same line, never "we've run out". Damaged: set aside, draw again, no comment. Forgotten: the card before she turns to go, with the line, never with the receipt; if she is already past the counter, let it go (a card chased to the door is a performance). Left behind: beside the cup or in the bag without a word; on a table, collected after the door closes. |
| Say | The locked line, "Here's a little note for your moment." Nothing added. |
| Inform | The opener, for the refill rule; a forgotten Note is logged at pre-shift as a miss. |
| Guest is left | With the card in her hand before money, as designed; or, if the moment has passed, without a chase. |

## R-M22/23 — The end goes wrong

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Trigger | The farewell was missed; a back was turned before she was out; the name was wrong; she is leaving while the team member is mid-serve. |
| Do | Mid-serve: the half-turn and the line as she passes; already at the door: the eyes and the nod, no shouted line. Missed and she is out: nothing; logged. Wrong name: not corrected mid-line; dropped next time. Back turned: the eyes to the door once, as late as it is. |
| Say | "Until the next celebration." by name where sure; otherwise the line alone. Never a line called after a guest who has left. |
| Inform | Pre-shift log of misses; a pattern of turned backs at peak is a staffing question for the operations brief, not a coaching one. |
| Guest is left | With a face still on her, even late; never with a shout. |

## R-UPSET — Guest visibly unhappy, at any moment

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Trigger | A complaint spoken; a face that has closed; a guest who says "forget it". |
| Do | Calm, short, no defence, no explanation of why it happened. One sentence that says the brand heard it; one that says who will act. Take her name and a way to reach her if she offers it. No compensation offered at the counter. No farewell line ("Until the next celebration" to an unhappy guest reads as deaf); the eyes and a nod. |
| Say | "I'm sorry. Menelaos will write to you today." / „Съжалявам. Менелаос ще ти пише днес." (Вие if she used Вие.) |
| Inform | Menelaos, the same day, with the facts: what, when, who served, what she said. Menelaos writes to her that day and decides any gesture. |
| Guest is left | Heard, with one promise, which is kept the same day. |

## R-ESC — Escalation topics at the counter (guardrails 11)

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Trigger | Illness after a visit, an allergic reaction, an injury in the room, a legal threat, press, a complaint naming a team member, discrimination or harassment. |
| Do | No draft of any kind, no explanation, no defence, no diagnosis. The one line, her details if she offers them, Menelaos immediately (a call, not a note at closing) for illness, allergy, injury or press; the same day for the rest. If someone is unwell in the room: help first, emergency number if needed, Menelaos second. |
| Say | "I'm sorry. Menelaos will write to you today." Nothing else about the topic. |
| Inform | Menelaos takes over in his own name. Giulia never replies on these; the team member never continues the conversation. |
| Guest is left | With one calm person and a named human who will contact her; never with a counter argument. |

## R-QUEUE — The queue outgrows the counter

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Trigger | Three or more waiting with one person on the counter; a wait past five minutes; a guest who leaves the queue. |
| Do | The look within ten seconds to each newcomer holds. The second station opens if anyone is in the back or the Lab. The tasting shortens to one spoon on offer rather than two; the question, the beat and the Note never shorten. The held attention at the door (M23) becomes eyes only. Nothing is announced to the queue. A guest who leaves is not called after. |
| Say | At her turn, past five minutes: "Thanks for waiting." then the question. Never "sorry for the wait", never "next". |
| Inform | The operations brief (section 2): a band that cannot be staffed is discussed, never silently degraded. Peak hours from Barsy decide the two-on rule. |
| Guest is left | Seen while waiting; asked when served; given the Note. |

## R-ALLERGEN — An allergen question, or an allergy disclosed late

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Trigger | A question about gluten, nuts, dairy, egg, alcohol or sugar; or a guest who says after tasting or buying that she has an allergy. |
| Do | Gluten: the verified fact (gelato and tiramisu gluten-free; Lab controls cross-contact; fact sheet 2026-10-30, written Lab confirmation to be filed). Any other allergen: the allergen policy (**CONFIRM**) if it covers it; otherwise "I'll ask Menelaos." and he answers; never a guess, never "I think". Disclosed late: stop, no sale or no further taste of the item in question, the facts, Menelaos if she is at all unwell (R-ESC). The Mindful line is "no added sugar", never "sugar-free" or "safe for diabetics". |
| Say | "The gelato and the tiramisu are gluten-free; the Lab checks cross-contact." · "I'll ask Menelaos." · for anything medical: "I'm sorry. Menelaos will write to you today." |
| Inform | Menelaos for anything outside the policy; immediately for any reaction. |
| Guest is left | With a fact or with a named person, never with a reassurance the brand cannot stand behind. |

## R-AGE — The Aperitivo line and a guest who may be under age

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Trigger | A guest who looks under 18 asks for limoncello sorbet, prosecco and peach or tiramisu gelato; or a child's order includes one. |
| Do | No taste, no sale of that item until the legal age and ID practice are confirmed (**CONFIRM**, schedule decision 11). Offer the nearest non-alcoholic flavour plainly. For a child's order through an adult, the adult is told the flavour is alcoholic and chooses. |
| Say | "That one has limoncello in it." / „В този има лимончело." · if asked: "That line is 18 and over." |
| Inform | Nobody for a routine case; Menelaos if a guest disputes it. |
| Guest is left | With a fact and another flavour, no lecture. |

## R-WRONG-ORDER — Wrong flavour scooped, wrong item boxed

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Trigger | She says it is not what she chose; the team member notices before she does. |
| Do | Take it back at once, no discussion; scoop the right one; the wrong cup goes behind the counter without comment. If she has already tasted it and wants to keep it, she keeps it, no charge for the swap. The Note is not repeated. |
| Say | "That's not yours. One moment." / „Това не е твоето. Един момент." Then the right flavour named as it is set down. |
| Inform | Nobody for a one-off; a pattern at pre-shift. |
| Guest is left | With the right cup within a minute and no apology stack; the visit continues as designed. |

## R-BILL — A complaint at the price

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Trigger | She questions the price, a size, or a charge. |
| Do | The list is the only source: show it, plainly. If the till is wrong, fix it on the spot and say so. If the list is right, say so once; no haggling, no "just this once". Any goodwill gesture is Menelaos's decision afterwards, never the team member's at the till (guardrails 3, 10). |
| Say | "That's the price on the list." / „Това е цената от листата." · if the till was wrong: "The till was wrong. It's [price]." |
| Inform | Menelaos if she remains unhappy (R-UPSET). |
| Guest is left | With a fact; with a fix if there was an error; with one promise if still unhappy. |

## R-ROOM — The room is wrong at open or mid-day

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Trigger | A cleaning smell, the music loud or wrong, the display dark, a jar hidden or near empty, boxes in the sightline, a phone on the counter, the room cold or hot. |
| Do | Fixed before the next guest where possible; the look (M5) still comes first for anyone already in. The opener signs the pre-shift checklist; a wrong room at open is a checklist failure, not a mood. |
| Say | Nothing to a guest about it. No "sorry about the smell". |
| Inform | The opener; recurring items to the operations brief (section 5). |
| Guest is left | In a room that corrects itself quietly. |

## R-PARTNER — Cards dropped or altered at a partner's table

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Trigger | A quarterly unannounced visit by Menelaos finds no cards on the tables, cards under plates or in a flyer stand, an edited card, or a card with an offer added. |
| Do | One conversation with the owner, in Вие, the agreement in hand; a date to fix it. A second finding: supply reviewed by Evo and Menelaos (v3 §8.1: never chase volume over fit). |
| Say | To the owner: "The cards are part of the delivery; that's what we agreed. Can they be on the tables by [date]?" |
| Inform | Evo, before any decision to stop supply. |
| Guest is left | The partner's guest is never addressed by the brand; the recovery is upstream. |

## Scenarios the category has that this concept does not

Over-served guest: not applicable at a counter selling alcoholic gelato in single portions; if a guest appears intoxicated, no Aperitivo sale, nothing else changes, Menelaos informed if there is any incident. Wait between courses: not applicable. Table over-booked: no bookings.
