# Vios Brand

Stage 1 of the Vios Practice: the fourteen interrogations, the brand persona and the brand book. Consulting engagements with a start, an end and the client owner's sign-off. Requires `vios-core`.

| Order | Skill | Stage 1 steps | Writes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **vios-brand-diagnosis** | 1–9: purpose, vision, mission, values, audience, competitors, differentiator, archetype and Brand Role, positioning | `diagnosis.md` |
| 2 | **vios-messages-and-story** | 10–11: nine core message blocks, seven-block story, micro-message blocks per touchpoint | `brand-book.md` › Messages and story |
| 3 | **vios-persona-builder** | step 8 instrument 6: Persona in Practice, the Persona Bible, AI Persona Brief | `persona-bible.md`, `character-sheet/` |
| 4 | **vios-name-and-tagline** | 12–13: deep interrogation, keywords, name candidates, tagline or no tagline | `archive/naming.md`, book Part 5 |
| 5 | **vios-brand-book** | § 2.1.3, 14, 2.1.6–7: assemble the book, Brand Wheel, designer selection, designer brief, harvest | `brand-book-vN.md`, Annex A/B |

`references/` holds the manuscript chapter verbatim (`stage-1-brand-strategy.md`) and the house book structure (`brand-book-structure.md`); the diagnosis skill carries the worksheets, the twelve archetypes and the competitor-audit template in its own `references/`. Skills read these before working; nothing is answered from memory.

Client folder convention: `vios-core:vios-guardrails` › client file. Next stage: `vios-journey` (journey map and design). Daily use: `vios-marketing`.
