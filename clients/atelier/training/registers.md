# Registers — Atelier del Gelato (Stage 4, Move 2)

**Draft — book edition v1.0 unsigned (open-facts 39); observation move not held.**

Date 2026-11-01. Section 3.3 applied to every item in the translation checklist (`architecture.md` § 2). For each: the **concept** line (from the brand book, the Reasoning Note or a page's "why"), the **operational** line (the design page, summarised here only to show the source; the page is the authority), and the **floor** line, redrafted for the counter, not compressed from the page. Guest-facing scripts are not redrafted here. They are the locked lines of the design pages, quoted word for word in `staff/floor-playbook.md`. A floor line below is what the team member tells herself; a script is what the guest hears.

## 6. Verification (Move 2, read together)

Each triple read on 2026-11-01: the floor line, performed, delivers the concept line in every row above. Two flags:

1. **M06 has no locked welcome for an unknown guest by design** (the book forbids a script). The floor line locks the behaviour ("three seconds, eyes up"); the risk of that choice (staff improvising a greeting formula to fill the gap) is trained against in the welcome-symmetry drill and the coaching guide on moment execution.
2. **M11's "her word back"** is the one free line in the ritual. The floor line cannot be shorter without losing the rule that the free sentence uses her own word; the locked fallback is on the card.
