**Operating principle: position assigns the role in the moment.** Whoever met the guest's eyes (M5) asks the question (M7), tastes with her (M11), hands the Note (M15) and sees her off (M22–M23). There is no greeter and no server; there is the person at the station she came to. Two team members on the counter are two stations, not a front and a back.

## 4. Service-flow assumptions

Each boxed with its reason and the moment it protects. Numbers are the design's; the Barsy data replaces them.

> **One guest at the counter per station at a time.** A tasting takes 30–90 seconds and the question needs a beat; a team member serving two guests at once cannot hold either. Protects M7, M11.

> **An order end to end: 3–5 minutes** (eyes up, question, beat, answer, two spoons, scoop, Note, pay, farewell). That is roughly 12–20 orders per station per hour at full ritual. **CONFIRM against Barsy tickets per hour**; where peak demand exceeds two stations' capacity, the choice is a third station or a named degradation, never a shortened question.

> **Queue ceiling: five minutes.** Past it, the second station opens or "Thanks for waiting." is said at her turn. Protects M6, M5.

> **The beat: 2–3 seconds, longer if she needs it.** It is not dead time; it is M8. Protects M7.

> **The blind draw: under five seconds, eyes not in the jar.** Protects M15.

> **The two-second rule at the door.** Shoulders stay with the leaving guest until she is past the counter; eyes go up to the newcomer within ten seconds. Needs two bodies at peak. Protects M23 and the next guest's M5.

> **Nothing cleared before the door closes.** A cup collected while she is still in the room is a turned back. Protects M20, M23.

> **The Note count equals the order count.** Cards out of the jar at close against Barsy tickets per station is the daily audit of the peak. Protects M15.

## 6. Training assumptions

**Locked lines every team member learns** (BG and EN; the filter test on the wall):

| Moment | Line |
| --- | --- |
| M5 (known guest) | "Good to see you again." / „Радваме се да те видим пак." |
| M7 | "What are we celebrating today?" / „Какво празнуваме днес?" |
| M9 | "Then let's choose something that tastes like it." · "Then let's just choose something good." · "I'm sorry. Let's choose something good for today." |
| M10 | "Pistachio again, or something new today?" · "Who are we celebrating? We'll choose the twelve together." |
| M11 | "Want to taste two together?" · "Take your time." |
| M13 | "That one has limoncello in it." |
| M15 | "Here's a little note for your moment." / „Ето една бележка за твоя момент." |
| M22 | "Until the next celebration." · "Small wins count." (only after a shared moment) |
| Recovery | "I'm sorry. Menelaos will write to you today." · "That's not yours. One moment." · "Thanks for waiting." |

**Knowledge requirements:** the fact sheet (origins, the two menus, Mindful and Aperitivo rules, the gluten line, prices, hours); the allergen policy; what was made this morning (daily); the four values as behaviours; the filter test; the escalation topics (guardrails 11) and that they end with Menelaos.

**Drills:** eyes up in 10 seconds while mid-scoop; the beat (not filling it); the blind draw with the gaze sequence; the two-second rule with two trainees at one counter; the recovery lines said aloud; the "nothing to celebrate" branch; reading the filter test on a line.

**Pre-shift briefing (daily, five minutes):** the room checklist; today's made-this-morning flavours and the anchor; sold-outs; jar levels; yesterday's misses (dropped question, filled beat, turned back); yesterday's best moment told in two sentences (v3 §4.8 staff micro-story), which is how names and usuals are remembered.

**Closing ritual:** the day's best moment told; the jar count against tickets; the room reset for the opener.

Full programme: `vios-training-programme` (Stage 4), with the three registers (concept for Menelaos and Evo, operational for this brief, floor for the playbook and the wall chart).

## 7. Standards and observable metrics

Sampled, not exhaustive; diagnostic, not punitive. Ten observations per shift by whoever is not serving, or by Menelaos when present.

| Standard | Observable behaviour | Target frequency |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Every guest acknowledged within 10 seconds | Eyes meet, head up, before any word | 10 of 10 arrivals |
| The question at every order, before the first product word | Hands empty when it is asked | 10 of 10 orders |
| The beat held | The team member does not speak again until the guest speaks or looks away | 10 of 10 |
| "Nothing" receives the same ritual | Tasting offered and Note given after a "nothing" | every case |
| At most two spoons at a time; no "bestseller" | Count and listen | 10 of 10 tastings |
| Eyes off the queue while a guest tastes | Watch the eyes | 10 of 10 |
| The Note drawn blind, in her hand before payment, with the line | Watch the draw and the order of events | 10 of 10 |
| Eyes away while she reads | Watch the eyes after the hand-over | 10 of 10 |
| One farewell line, by name where known, at the leaving trigger | Listen; note the trigger | 10 of 10 departures |
| Shoulders with the leaver until past the counter | Watch the body | 10 of 10 |
| Nothing cleared before the door closes | Watch the cup | every departure |
| Note count equals ticket count | Jar at open minus jar at close against Barsy | daily, zero gap |
| Room checklist signed | The clipboard | daily |
| No forbidden words | "Next", "Anything else", "bestseller", "treat yourself", exclamation energy | zero |

## 8. Management review cadence

- **Daily:** the pre-shift briefing and the closing ritual (section 6).
- **Weekly:** Menelaos with the team, fifteen minutes: the misses log, the names, the jar audit, staffing against the bands in section 2, anything a guest said that should change a page.
- **Monthly:** design review with Evo and Menelaos off the floor, one hour: the metrics in section 7 with n, the open CONFIRM items, pages that need revising. Small revisions over the first 6–12 months are expected and are versioned on the page footer.
- **Quarterly:** the partner visits (R-PARTNER); the filter test's regression batch re-run; the Ring Mall form reviewed once Evo has decided it.
- **Yearly:** the brand book review (v3 §8); if the book moves edition, the map is re-read first (`journey-map.md` realignment rule) and the pages follow.
