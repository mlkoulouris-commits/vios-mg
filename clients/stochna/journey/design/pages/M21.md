# M21 · HIGH IMPACT · A question about the making (if)

In-room · 30 s–3 min · Phase 4–5 · Guest-raised · Conditional

**Dominant inputs:** brand (Made together; Everyman "share knowledge when asked, never impose it") · concept (what staff know; is the brewer on shift?) · non-verbal · BE.

## CURRENT STATE
Someone asks how a beer is made, or what's in the bread. Next: back to the evening.

## DO
1. Answer from experience, in plain words, as far as the interest goes.
2. Stop when the guest stops asking.
3. If the brewer or cook is free and the guest wants more, offer once to bring them for a minute.

## SAY
EN "[Plain answer]. Want me to ask the brewer?" · BG „[Простичък отговор]. Да попитам ли пивоваря?“

## DON'T
- No lecture; no "notes of", "mouthfeel", "terroir" (book § 4.6).
- No claim that isn't on the fact sheet (guardrails 9); "I'll check" is fine.

## NON-VERBAL
Match the guest's energy, not more (NV B.1, B.3).

## BEHAVIORAL ECONOMICS
BE 1 cognitive ease: plain words keep it social.

## RECOVERY
- Allergen in the question → R-ALLERGY.

**State arc:** How is this made? → A straight answer → I asked and got a straight answer.
**Success metric:** the guest passes the answer on to the table (observation).
**Target emotion:** interested. **Brand pillar:** Made together.
