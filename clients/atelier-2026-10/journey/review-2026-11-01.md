# Atelier journey map — reviewer's answers (2026-11-01)

Answers to the four questions the blind-test session left, written by the practice for Menelaos to confirm and Evo to sign with the Reasoning Note. Each is a recommendation with the reason; the map changes only after the confirmation.

## 1. Threshold HIGH on the person (M05) or the room (M04)?

**Keep it on the person.** The brand book's differentiator says the ritual "needs no theatre, only people", and the persona's fear is being processed, not being under-lit. In a counter format the room is read in two seconds and then a face takes over; the thin-slice that decides the visit is "someone looked up". M04 stays MEDIUM and carries the room's standard (scent, sound, display, jar visible). The HIGH count stays at six. Revisit after the observation move (two shadowed services): if guests hesitate at the door before anyone looks up, the room was doing less than the book assumes and M04 moves up.

## 2. Signature = the question (M07) alone, or the whole ritual with the peak inside?

**Keep them separate.** The question is where the differentiator becomes visible to a guest who has not yet received anything; the Note is where the memory is made. They are two moments in time with two different actors' states, and Stage 3 needs two pages because the geometry, the script and the recovery differ. The map's sentence "the same promise seen twice" is the right description; the signature marker stays on M07, the peak on M15. For the brand book, the three-step ritual (ask, choose together, mark it) is one ritual; for the floor, it is three moments.

## 3. Phase 8, the partner table: keep or cut?

**Keep it thin, as mapped, and do not design it.** The Vision names the partner table, so leaving it off the map would say the map does not believe the Vision. But without a venue-owner persona the three moments cannot carry HIGH weight or a design page with a locked line. The three "pages" written for M29–M31 are briefs to the Lab and to partners, which is the right form. Open fact: the B2B persona, owner Evo and Menelaos, via `vios-persona-builder` as a second audience section in the brand book (Format A §2.5 style). Until then phase 8 is read, not performed.

## 4. The filter test in Bulgarian

"Is this processing the guest?" does not translate as a wall-chart line; „обработваме ли госта?" reads as a factory. Proposed BG form, to confirm with the team: **„Говорим ли на човека, или на поръчката?"** ("Are we talking to the person, or to the order?"), markers: бързаме · продаваме · говорим покрай нея. The EN question stays as the practice's form; the wall chart carries the BG line. Decision owner: Menelaos, after saying it aloud with Giulia's lines in the room.
