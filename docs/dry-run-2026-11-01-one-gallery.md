## Harvest entry for the manuscript (Stage 1–4, from this dry run)

Candidate patterns, each used once (threshold for recording), none yet confirmed by a second engagement:

- **S2-06 The signature marker.** The differentiator made visible is marked separately from the peak; they are usually different moments (ONE Gallery: M13 signature, M18 peak).
- **S2-07 The end as two moments designed as one.** Sign-up card and farewell carry the peak-end rule together; the first is an invitation offered once, the second a specific person.
- **S3-09 The DO / SAY / DON'T page.** The manuscript's eleven fields shipped as a page staff and trainers read: current state, do, say (locked, in registers), don't, non-verbal, behavioural economics, recovery, state arc, metric, target emotion, pillar.
- **S3-10 The peak cluster.** The moments around the peak are designed as one cluster, named in the choreography; a helpful act anywhere in the cluster breaks the peak.
- **S4-08 The onboarding plan by weight.** For a running venue: five sessions, foundations first and alone, HIGH before MEDIUM before LOW, silences last and never skipped, then shadow shifts and a signed checklist.
- **S4-09 The three drills.** Retreat without the glance back; silent moment under a clock; welcome symmetry.
- **S3-11 The eight-section operations brief.** Staffing by daypart, boxed service-flow assumptions, equipment inside, standards as observable metrics, a daily/weekly/monthly review cadence.
- **S1-06 Two brand-book formats.** Interrogation order as the engagement deliverable with the coherence table and open-items ledger; Why → Govern as the derived team edition.
- **S1-07 The revision pass.** Operating foundations written back after Stages 3–4 and after a marketing review, with one coherence row per addition.

Framework revision suggested: § 2.1.3 can now be specified from Format A. Instrument 6 (Persona in Practice) was not exercised here; it stays provisional.

**Framework revisions adopted 2026-11-01** (owner's decision, commit 7391d3e; to carry into the manuscript's next version):

- Stage 2, Move 2b: observe before weighing. Two shadowed services and a review read go into `journey/observation.md` before weights are assigned; observation is the fifth weighting source; a HIGH with no observation behind it is marked "brand-led"; every map carries a re-weighting date 30–60 days after go-live.
- `vios-ads-audit` winner rule: 50 results per cell, not ~15, with the reason stated; `vios-ad-factory` says up front whether the budget can reach it, otherwise round 1 is labelled a bet.
- Guardrails "Evidence and house doctrine": every rule is tiered as evidence, contested or house doctrine, and the non-verbal lens frames face-reading as a contested heuristic.

## Blind generation test (2026-11-01, Atelier del Gelato, fresh session)

A fresh session ran `vios-journey-map`, `vios-filter-test` and one `vios-journey-design` page on Atelier from Brand Book v3 and the client folder, with every ONE Gallery file withheld. Output in `clients/atelier/journey/` and `archive/`; its own notes in `clients/atelier/journey/blind-test-notes.md`.

Result: 31 moments, 8 phases, 6 HIGH, 12 MEDIUM, 10 LOW, 3 silent; signature ★ M07 the question, peak ◉ M15 the note from the jar, end ⊐ M22 + M23. The map, the Reasoning Note, the handoff and the M07 page follow the house form without having seen it; target states are quoted guest sentences, the B2B line is an eighth phase mapped thin, every missing input is marked to confirm. The filter test came out as "Is this processing the guest?" with markers hurry · sell · talk past. Judgement: the skills generate, not only shape. Reviewer's questions for Evo and Menelaos are listed in the notes.

Thirteen friction points reported; fixes applied to the three skills: intake file named; shares of all moments with silent as its own row and no earlier client's numbers in the skill; owner derived from the phase at the bridge; BG editions and the Claude Doc produced on request, EN in the repo; LOW rows at twelve words, silent rows empty, two-digit codes from the map onward, three or four rendered pages accepted; B2B phase rule; single-page mode in the design skill; BE/NV citation form; filter-test section location and scope (taglines outside it).
