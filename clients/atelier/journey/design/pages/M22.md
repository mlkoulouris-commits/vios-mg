# M22 · CRITICAL IMPACT · Farewell ⊐

In-room · Leaving (phase 6) · ~5 sec · Counter or table edge, as she turns to go · **⊐ end-1**, designed as one with M23

Dominant inputs: **brand** and **non-verbal**; BE present (peak-end, framing); concept sets the pressure (a counter, the next guest already there).

## CURRENT STATE

She has paid, read the card, tasted; she has sat ten minutes or is at the counter with the bag in her hand. She stands, gathers, turns toward the door. Her state: "Nobody's hurrying me" moving to "I'll come back." The next guest may already be at the counter. Next: M23, the walk to the door with the brand's attention still on her; then nothing until the card on her desk (M24).

## DO

1. The farewell belongs to the team member who served her. Mid-serve with another guest: a half-turn of the body to her, the line, then back to the guest in hand. Not delegated to whoever is nearest.
2. Trigger: she stands, gathers, lifts the bag from the counter, or turns toward the door. A read, not the till: the farewell is said at leaving, never with the change.
3. Her name if known, then one line. One of two locked lines (below). The same line each visit from the same team member is right; it is a locked line, not a variation.
4. Eyes on her for the line; a small nod as the full stop; resting warm face, intensity 3, to the guest (Bible §9 sign-off).
5. Nothing after the line. No "see you soon", no "have a nice day", no "come again".
6. Groups: once, to the group, with the name of the one who answered the question if known.
7. To-go at the counter: the Note line (M15) and the farewell line are two moments with a beat between them: card in her hand, payment, bag in her hand, then the farewell as she turns.
8. Seated guest leaving while the counter is busy: whoever has the sightline gives the line as she passes the counter; if she is past speaking distance, the eyes and the nod carry it (M23).
9. The room: inherits. The door is the last surface she touches (haptics: weight, soft close; a dependency for the designer brief).

## SAY

**LOCKED** — one line, by name where known:

> EN: "Until the next celebration."
> BG: „До следващото празнуване."

Second locked line, **only after she shared something at M9**:

> EN: "Small wins count."
> BG: „Малките победи се броят."

Registers:

| Register | Line |
| --- | --- |
| Default | Name + "Until the next celebration." |
| She shared a win or a moment | Name + "Small wins count." or the default |
| She said "nothing" at M9 | The default only; never "Small wins count." (it would read as a push to find one) |
| Group | The default, once |
| Regular | Name + the default; the line does not change for familiarity |
| Guest leaving unhappy | No farewell line; see RECOVERY |

Filter test (*Is this processing the guest?*): to her by name, no hurry, nothing sold. Both lines **pass**. "Until the next celebration" names a return without asking for one; no points, no card, no "see you soon".

## DON'T

- Don't say it at the till with the change; don't say it to her back.
- Don't stack lines: "Thanks, until the next celebration, have a lovely evening, come back soon."
- Don't "Thank you for your purchase", "Have a nice day", "Bye bye", or a performed "Ciao".
- Don't use "Small wins count." after "nothing".
- Don't shout it across the room; past conversational distance the nod and the eyes do the work.
- Don't skip it because another guest is being served; the half-turn costs two seconds.
- Don't let a second team member add a second farewell; the second nods only.
- No exclamation energy.

## NON-VERBAL

- **Orientation** (NV C.4): the body turns to her for the line, even mid-task; the hands may be busy, the shoulders are hers for two seconds.
- **Eyes** (NV C.2): on her for the line; the nod closes it. Released only into M23's held attention, not to the till.
- **Face**: resting warm, intensity 3, to the guest; the same face the next guest is watching.
- **Tone** (NV B.1): a notch softer than the question; falling at the end; a goodbye between friends, not a shop's thanks.
- **Volume** (NV B.3): for her; conversational.
- **Timing** (NV E.3): at the leaving trigger, read from her body, never from the payment.
- **Silence** (NV B.4): after the line, nothing from anyone on the floor.
- **Exit geometry** (NV A.4): she is the one exiting; the staff's exit is the slow release of attention in M23, never a turn away at the end of the line.

## BEHAVIORAL ECONOMICS

- **Peak-end** (BE 7): the end is sampled by the remembering self with the weight of the peak; the farewell is the brand's belief said as goodbye and is what she narrates next to the question. The library's own warning applies here: the end is the moment most often under-designed because the next order is calling.
- **Duration neglect** (BE 7): whether she stayed ten minutes or left with a bag, the end is the same length and the same words.
- **Framing** (BE 9): "the next celebration" frames return as her next moment, not as loyalty; the brand gives no call to action and no mechanism.
- **Cognitive ease** (BE 1): one locked line, short, survivable on the hundredth repetition of a summer afternoon.
- **Decision here**: none for her now; the return (M28) is decided later, by what this moment left.

## RECOVERY

| If … | → |
| --- | --- |
| She leaves while the team member is mid-serve | The half-turn and the line as she passes; if she is already at the door, the eyes and the nod (M23). No shouted line. |
| The name is misremembered or uncertain | Leave the name out; the line stands. Never guess a name aloud. |
| She leaves visibly unhappy | No farewell line. If she has complained: "I'm sorry. Menelaos will write to you today." (filter: pass). If she has not spoken: eyes and a nod only. Menelaos informed the same day. R-UPSET. If the complaint touches illness, allergy, injury, staff or legal matters, no draft of any kind; Menelaos takes over (guardrails 11). R-ESC. |
| Two guests leave at once, not together | One farewell each, in order. |
| The farewell was missed and she is out of the door | Nothing. A line called after a guest who has left is worse than none. Noted for the pre-shift. |
| She stops at the door to chat (a regular) | Receive it; keep it to the counter's needs by the body (M23), not by a second line; the farewell is not repeated. |

**State arc:** Finishing → Sent off → Out

**Success metric:** On ten sampled departures per shift, the line is said at the leaving trigger and not at the till, by name where the name is known, one line only, with the team member's shoulders turned to the guest.

**Target emotion:** lighter

**Brand pillar:** welcome, both ways; value Choose happiness; core message block 3 ("you go back into your day feeling seen, lighter")

## HIGH additions

**Brand-coherence test.** Role Peer: a friend's goodbye, not a shop's thanks; the name before the line. Connection statement "let's celebrate it together" closes on "the next celebration". Lover 70: warm, by name. Innocent 30: five words, nothing clever. Holds.

**Story block:** 7, The New World, begins at the door.

**Bookended end (S3-06).** Mirrors M5: the same person where possible, eyes before words, one locked line, the same brevity. The threshold opened plain and certain; the end closes the same way.

**Operational dependencies.** Sightline from the counter to the tables and the door (to confirm). Two on the counter at peak so the half-turn is possible without dropping the guest in hand (operations brief §2). How the team remembers names: Giulia's notebook is a persona accessory; the real method (a names habit, a till note, nothing written about guests without consent) is a Stage 4 and guardrails 12 question. The door's soft close (designer brief revision candidate).
