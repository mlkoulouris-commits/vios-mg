## 3. Owner interview (derived; CONFIRM with Evo)

1. **Love / hate.** Loves: the craft, the moment a guest is noticed, Italian technique done properly. Hates: dessert as a transaction, "Next!", the rushed counter.
2. **Value beyond the plate.** A pause in the day in which someone notices you and what you are celebrating.
3. **One thing to change in the industry.** Service. The category's most common complaint is people, not product.
4. **Why this market is great.** Sofia has no gelato brand with an emotional idea; the tiramisu category is only now emerging.
5. **View on social media.** Real guests, real notes, no posed perfection, no product-only heroes, no pastel kitsch.
6. **Why, aside from money.** Happiness is an attitude; everyday moments deserve to be noticed and celebrated.
7. **How we do business, one sentence.** Fresh every day, the Italian way, no shortcuts, and nothing leaves without its story.
8. **What guests need protecting from.** Being processed; empty mass-market desserts; joyless "healthy" options.
9. **What matters in the world now.** CONFIRM.
10. **Passion.** CONFIRM.

## 4. Purpose, vision, mission, values (locked Oct 2026)

- **Purpose:** To give everyone who believes everyday moments matter a pause to notice and celebrate one, crafted with care, every day. (Who: anyone who believes their everyday moments matter · How: Italian craft and personal attention · Resources: gelato and tiramisu, finest ingredients, a team trained to notice people · Frequency: every guest, every day.)
- **Vision:** To be the name people think of when an everyday moment deserves celebrating, found in our own ateliers and at the tables of partners we choose.
- **Mission:** Every day, we craft fresh gelato and tiramisu the Italian way, with no shortcuts, and make sure each one reaches people with its story, so their moment is noticed and celebrated. Standing commitments: made fresh daily in small batches · authentic Italian technique · every guest greeted personally · nothing leaves without its story · partners only if they serve that story.
- **Values (operating statements):** Choose happiness (never fake it) · Bring the attitude (hire for attitude, train skill) · Notice the moment (ask what you're celebrating) · Stay human (eye contact, names, no scripts; no kiosks).
- **Open:** which values are already true vs aspirational — CONFIRM with Evo.

## 5. Primary audience persona + archetype

**Elena**, 34, creative director, central Sofia, €2.5–3.2k/month, partnered, no children. Pain: mass desserts feel empty, "healthy" feels joyless, cafés rush her. Need: to feel her everyday moments matter. Before: busy, a little drained. After: calm, seen, lighter. ## 6. Competitor map (Oct 2026 audit: Gelato & Latte, Afreddo, Gelateria Naturale, Doppio, Franco's Nuvole)

**Ideas worth adopting:** provenance as proof (own farm, origin); named staff create loyalty; tiramisu as a specialist category; guests police authenticity; packaging as personality.

**Opportunities:** service and warmth is the category's weakest point; no competitor owns an emotional idea ("joy" alone is used by Doppio, not ownable); nobody combines premium gelato and a serious tiramisu collection; nobody makes honesty a visible promise; nobody supplies venues with a story attached.

## 7. Differentiator and positioning

**Differentiator — the celebration ritual:** every order begins with "What are we celebrating today?" and ends with an Atelier Note chosen by chance (ask, choose together, mark it). Onliness: the only gelato and tiramisu atelier where every order becomes a small celebration of your day.

**Positioning (locked in Brand Book v3 §2.4, internal, never copy):** We help people in Sofia who believe their everyday moments matter, but feel processed and rushed everywhere they go, to experience a pause in which their moment is noticed and celebrated, unlike gelato shops that sell product and tiramisu bars that sell novelty; our solution is fresh Italian gelato and tiramisu handed over with a question and a note.

## 8. Brand archetype and Brand Role

Audience desire: "You see me, and you celebrate with me." Brand archetype **Lover 70 / Innocent 30** (Innocent stays light, never cute). Drive: connection that makes ordinary moments feel special. Fear: being ordinary, transactional or fake. **Brand Role: Peer** ("Your day matters to us as much as it does to you, so let's celebrate it together"). Personality expression: Giulia, early 30s, linen in cream/sage/caramel, notebook of guests' names, uses "we", never says "Next!" or "That's our bestseller."

## 9. Contradictions found and what must change upstream

1. **Old differentiator is gone.** The 2025 brand book's primary pillar was tiramisu alla minute made in front of guests. It is discontinued. Any copy, signage, website text or social template that still says "made in front of you", "watch beauty being made", "marble station" must be retired. CHECK: current Instagram bio, website, printed menus, packaging.
2. **Archetype changed** (Lover/Creator → Lover/Innocent) and persona changed (Sofia → Elena). Tone of voice document (Dec 2025: "poetic, sensual, devotional") is heavier than the Oct 2026 direction ("calm, light, sincere; simple sensory language"). Persona builder must write the new voice spec; do not reuse the 2025 tone profile.
3. **Values from seven to four.** The 2025 book's seven values collapse into four operating statements. Staff training material must follow the four.
4. **"Slowness is non-negotiable; during rush we limit capacity, not speed"** (2025) vs Ring Mall counter and B2B growth. Hard case for "Notice the moment" is "a long queue". CONFIRM how the ritual survives a mall queue; otherwise the mall counter is a second brand expression.
5. **Approver.** Brand Book v3 §8.3 left it open. Decided 2026-10-30: Menelaos Koulouris approves everything; Evo informed on brand-level items. Revisit when there are 2–3 ateliers.
6. **AI ambassador vs Stay human.** Decided 2026-10-30: Giulia becomes a disclosed AI digital ambassador for content. Brand Book v3 says "human, in person, never automated or faceless" and "we don't automate the welcome away". Resolution recorded in the Bible: Giulia exists on screen only, always labelled; the counter welcome stays human. Evo to be told explicitly; the book's §3.3 row needs a footnote in the next revision.
7. **Alcohol is on the menu** (Aperitivo line). Guardrails hard line 8 applies: 18+ targeting on any paid post with it, no link to driving, sport or youth. Not mentioned anywhere in the brand book.
