# Choreography & continuities — Stochna Kitchen Brewery

Date 2026-11-01 · DRAFT (pages unlocked; see `schedule.md`) · operational register.

## 1. Continuities

### The peak cluster: M22–M26 protect M24
M22 (conversation takes over, silent) gives the table time to become a table. M23 (reading from a distance) is how staff know when M24 is due without asking "everything OK?". M24 is the one offer. M25 (plates cleared, nothing pushed) keeps the decision to stay unbroken. M26 (sound and light) is the condition for all of it. Designed as one: **between M17 and M30 the only unprompted words a table hears are the M24 offer** (plates named at M19 only if the table looks up).

### The end pair: M30 + M31 as one
The bill is the last transaction; the farewell undoes it. M30 removes everything (no asks, no cards); M31 adds one line. Same person-at-work posture as M07: the evening is bookended by eyes-before-words (S3-06).

### Rules
1. **Offer once.** Every suggestion (M15 anchor, M24 next round) is made once; a no is final until the table asks.
2. **Step back after every line.** Line, beat, turn away (M08, M15, M17, M24, M31).
3. **The voice is the same from the caption to the farewell.** Same plain words online and on the floor; the AI ambassador never appears in the room (`handoff-to-design.md` › v1.1).
4. **Nobody stops working to greet.** The person nearest looks up; that's the welcome (M07, M31).
5. **Nothing is asked of the guest.** No review, rating, card or survey anywhere in phases 3–6.
