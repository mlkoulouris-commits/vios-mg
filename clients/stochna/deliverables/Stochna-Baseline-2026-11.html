7. Reading (five lines)

  1. A dinner house: 84 % of covers between 18:00 and 22:00, Saturday the peak day; the October gyros lunch is the only daypart that moved (114 → 503 covers).
  2. Beer is the volume (five house beers are the top five items; drinks alcohol 31 % of revenue) but food carries the money (62 %); the beer platter and the Black Angus burger are the top food contributors.
  3. Prime cost ran 76–88 % of net revenue over the three months, labour 44–52 % on its own; the September peak is a labour peak, not a sales one.
  4. Seven in ten guests arrive on a booking (702 a month, party of five, 1 % no-shows); Google and the website each bring about a quarter of bookings, Instagram 1 %.
  5. Guests rate it 4.9 from 1,299 reviews and talk about the beer; what they say beyond that, and what the agency's ads cost, is not on file.
