# Registers — Stochna Kitchen Brewery

Date 2026-11-02 · DRAFT · one idea, three readers (section 3.3). Concept for the founder · operational for the manager · floor for the team. Each is redrafted for its reader, never shortened from another. Floor lines read aloud before they reach the playbook.

| Item | Concept (founder) | Operational (manager) | Floor (team) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The threshold (M07) | The Quiet Refuser doesn't stop cooking to greet you; the room says you're in the right place before anyone speaks. | Nearest staff member makes eye contact within 10 s of the door opening, nods, keeps working; the greeting line follows within 20 s. | "Look up. Nod. Keep going." |
| The greeting (M08) | Warmth without ceremony; the first-timer and the regular hear the same thing. | Same person, one line, the booking name after the line. | "Hi. Sit anywhere." |
| The order (M15) | Proper choices feel like care when one person guides without prescribing. | One pairing anchor per menu edition, agreed at pre-shift; the pour offer for the unsure; no upsell. | "One suggestion. Then step back." |
| The signature (M17) | Cook for beer, brew for food, seen in two objects on the table. | Bar and pass land beer and bread within one minute; one sentence; leave. | "Together. One line. Gone." |
| The peak (M24) | The table stays without deciding to; the brand's act at the peak is restraint. | Approach on three signals; offer the paired beer once; accept a no with one word. | "Wait for the three signs. Offer once. 'Sure.'" |
| The bill (M30) | The evening must not end as a transaction. | Bill only when asked, centre of the table, nothing else on the tray, paid within 8 min. | "Only when asked. Nothing on the tray." |
| The farewell (M31) | "See you next time" is a prediction, the bookend of the open door. | Nearest person at work, same posture as M07, one line. | "Look up. 'See you next time.'" |
| The silences | What is absent is as intentional as what is present. | Six moments where staff do nothing; each has a reason on its page. | "Leave them alone. That's the job." |
| Recovery | "We got that wrong. Here's what we're doing instead." | Own in one line, fix, no compensation from the floor; the manager decides afterwards. | "Own it. Fix it. Once." |
| Escalation | Some moments need a named person, not a fluent line. | Allergy, illness, injury, legal, press, harassment: hand to the manager with the facts. | "Not yours. Get the manager." |

**Read-together check:** each floor line, performed, delivers the concept line? Yes for all ten in a desk read; to repeat aloud with two staff members at the first session.
