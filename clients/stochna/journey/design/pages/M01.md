# M01 · HIGH IMPACT · Hearing of it

Digital / word of mouth · days–hours before · Phase 1, deciding · Self-led · Owner: marketing

**Dominant inputs:** brand (story blocks 1–3; Worth the trip) · BE (first impression at a distance) · concept (Instagram, Facebook, GBP; agency holds social, open fact 25) · persona (AI ambassador may speak, `persona-bible.md` § 8).

## CURRENT STATE
A friend says "you have to try this place", or one image crosses a feed. Nothing decided. Next: they check the profile (M02).

## DO
1. Every public image shows a full table mid-evening, beer and food together, the making behind (book § 6.5); no product shots, no empty room.
2. Ambassador posts carry the full disclosure set (Bible § 13), in real footage only.
3. Guests' own photos are reposted only with written permission, credited (guardrails 2).
4. Ads (if any) via `vios-ad-factory`, 18+, `?src=meta_ad` (guardrails 8).

## SAY (caption voice)
EN "Bread's in. Beer's cold. Door's open. Tuesday." · BG „Хлябът е готов. Бирата е студена. Вратата е отворена. Вторник.“ (book § 4.5; bread claim open fact 6)

## DON'T
- No CTA beyond the booking link in the bio; no "book now".
- No superlatives, no "best brewery in Sofia".
- No generated food, room or street (guardrails 5).

## BEHAVIORAL ECONOMICS
BE 17 thin-slicing at a distance; BE 11 social proof only as it really is (real tables, real guests, with permission).

## RECOVERY
- Post performs badly → no boosting to fix it; next week's post is plainer.
- Comment touches a hard-line topic → R-ESC, the approver.

**State arc:** Another bar → One honest image → That might be it.
**Success metric:** saves and shares per post; bookings tagged `?src=instagram` (n shown).
**Target emotion:** curious. **Brand pillar:** Worth the trip · story blocks 1–3.
