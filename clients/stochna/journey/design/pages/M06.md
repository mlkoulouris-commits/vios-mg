# M06 · HIGH IMPACT · The street and the door

Environmental · 1–3 min · Phase 2, the trip · Self-led · Owner: floor

**Dominant inputs:** brand (Place matters; § 1.5 "not picturesque. Neither are we") · non-verbal (the room as channel) · concept (address, sign, window, open fact 1) · BE.

## CURRENT STATE
Walking through an industrial street on purpose, looking for the door. Next: crossing the threshold (M07).

## DO
1. The sign says the name, lit after dark; nothing else on the façade.
2. The window shows the room: blinds up during service, the making in view if the layout allows (CONFIRM floor plan).
3. The door path is clear and lit; no A-frame boards.
4. The street is left as it is.

## DON'T
- No welcome banners, no neon, no chalkboard.
- No dark window on a quiet night: seat the first tables in view.

## NON-VERBAL
NV F.1 lighting register: warm light visible from the street. NV F.4 surface: the sign is a working sign, not a feature.

## BEHAVIORAL ECONOMICS
BE 17 thin-slicing begins at the window; BE 11 social proof: people inside, seen from outside.

## RECOVERY
- Sign out or door hard to find → fixed before the next service; noted at pre-shift.

**State arc:** Is this the right street? → A lit sign, a full window → This is it.
**Success metric:** at observation, guests walk straight to the door without checking their phone.
**Target emotion:** recognition. **Brand pillar:** Place matters.
