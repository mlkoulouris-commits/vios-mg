## Non-negotiables
- Art · Coffee · Aperitivo as one integrated identity. Anything that reads "gallery only" or "café only" is a brand violation.
- The exhibition leads content; the bar follows (§11.5).
- Two voices, one character: the team's "we" for hospitality; the curator's (Desi's) signed voice for the art (§5.4, §11.1).
- The Gallery Series is an untranslated proper noun in every language.

## Messages and story (Brand Book v2.2 §6–8, transcribed 2026-11-01)

Source: Brand Book v2.2 (Oct 2026, art-led revision), sections 6, 7 and 8. Every piece of copy anchors here (`vios-core:vios-guardrails` › Messages and micro-stories). Transcribed, not rewritten; the PDF is the authority.

### Positioning (§6, internal, never copy)

We help curious Sofia adults, who navigate the city as fragments and have never felt fully at home in either the gallery or the hotel bar, to experience contemporary art as part of daily life, held by the rituals they already love, with the same welcome morning and evening. Unlike standalone galleries, hotel bars, ordinary cafés or single-purpose aperitivo rooms, we put contemporary art at the centre, with coffee in the morning and aperitivo in the evening around it: one place, one welcome, all day.

The positioning as a working tool: a VIP area contradicts "one welcome"; closing 3–6 pm contradicts "all day"; evening reservations-only contradicts "one place, one welcome"; a lecture series contradicts "approached, not deciphered"; the bar leading communication contradicts "art at the centre".

### Core messages (§7)

| # | Short name | Tier | What the guest should understand |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Audience | primary | Curious Sofia adults seeking a place that fits the whole of how they live. |
| 2 | Pain point | primary | The city has places to drink, to look at art, to have coffee, but not one place that does all three, with the same identity, the whole day. |
| 3 | Key benefit | primary | A single room with contemporary art at its centre, made daily by coffee and aperitivo, where welcome holds at every hour. |
| 4 | Alternative | primary | Other venues are single-occasion: a morning café, an evening bar, a daytime gallery, a hotel rooftop. The customer assembles their day from fragments. |
| 5 | Differentiator | primary | Contemporary art at the centre, with coffee in the morning and aperitivo in the evening around it. One place, one welcome, all day. |
| 6 | Purpose | secondary | To lower the barrier to contemporary art through the rituals of coffee and aperitivo. |
| 7 | Vision | secondary | Sofia's cultural living room, the place the city returns to. |
| 8 | Mission | secondary | Welcome every guest the same; coffee and aperitivo above the city's standard; art presented to be approached, not deciphered. |
| 9 | Welcome | secondary (value) | The standard does not bend: late, busy, short-staffed. |
| 10 | Ritual | secondary (value) | We do not improvise the moments that define the place. |
| 11 | Discovery | secondary (value) | We open the door to the art with short, clear context, never a lecture; on the floor, the guest finds it and we are nearby if they ask. |
| 12 | Quality | secondary (value) | Standards are not negotiated downward. |

Integrity rule: a piece reinforces at least one, contradicts none. Blocks hold strategy, not copy.

### Story framework (§8; Dilyana = hero, ONE Gallery = guide)

| Block | One line |
| --- | --- |
| 1 Existing world | Dilyana loves Sofia and curates it in fragments: coffee here, drinks there, art somewhere else; always searching, rarely settling. |
| 2 Obstacle | She wants a place with soul, but soul feels scattered; "it's fine" has become her default answer. |
| 3 Call to action | A friend visits: "Show me your Sofia." She has places, but not a place. |
| 4 Meeting the guide | A corner building, warm light, art through the window; she steps in for coffee, no one explains the sculpture, she stays. |
| 5 The challenge | She returns in the evening, talks about the art without expertise, wonders if she is allowed to; the bartender joins with curiosity, no judgement. |
| 6 Transformation | She used to search; now she arrives. "You have to see this place." A centre instead of fragments. |
| 7 New world | "Meet me at ONE." Her place, her ritual, her city, finally whole. |

The brand belief, deployed: *Ritual and art share the same purpose, to make you pause and feel.* It lives inside the story, at M18 (the peak, felt not told) and in the email voice (M26), not at the front of the differentiator.
