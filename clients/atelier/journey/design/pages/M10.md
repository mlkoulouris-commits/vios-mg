# M10 · SILENT · The beat after the question

**Draft — book edition v1.0 unsigned (open-facts 39); observation move not held.**

In-room · Phase 3 The question · ~1–2 sec · Both counters · ○ silent (between two HIGH moments, M09 and M11; the silence a question requires)

## CURRENT STATE

The question has just been asked (M09). She decides whether to say it. M09 did the work; this second lets it land as a question, not a slogan.

## DO

**Nothing.** Where the team member is while not doing it: facing her across the case, eyes on her, face warm and resting (Bible §9), hands still and off the scoop and the till. Hold one to two seconds, longer if she is thinking. Then M11, at her answer.

## DON'T

- Don't fill it ("…or nothing, that's fine too", "haha", "anything at all").
- Don't offer examples of what others celebrate.
- Don't reach for the scoop or the till.
- Don't look at the next guest.
- Don't repeat the question unless she clearly did not hear (then M09 recovery).

Metric: no words between the question and her answer in a sampled hour (x of n orders).
