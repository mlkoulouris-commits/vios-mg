## New patterns (candidates)

### S2-C1 — The trip as a phase for a destination the brand calls a filter

- **When to use:** at the establish-the-arc move, when the brand book says the venue's distance or location is deliberate (a filter, a choice the guest makes), not a drawback.
- **What it produces:** a one-moment phase between deciding and the threshold where the brand's only act is to leave the approach honest and make the door and the window do the work. It holds the question the evening answers ("was it worth it") and gives the threshold its second load.
- **What it asks of the brand:** that it never dresses up the street, apologises for the distance, or hides the door; and that the room is visible from outside.
- **Failure mode:** the phase fills with wayfinding content and signage that turns the approach into marketing.

### S2-C2 — The signature as an object pair for a "made together" brand

- **When to use:** when the differentiator is two things made as one system (beer and food, bread and butter, coffee and pastry) and the book names a byproduct that crosses between them.
- **What it produces:** a signature placed at the first moment the two objects are on the table together, early in the first round, with one plain sentence that names the crossing (the spent grain in the bread came from this morning's brew), followed by a silence.
- **What it asks of the brand:** daily production of both halves and the timing to land them together at every table.
- **Failure mode:** the sentence grows into a speech; or the halves arrive apart and the integration is told, not seen.

### S2-C3 — The peak at the second round for a linger venue

- **When to use:** when the persona "orders in rounds" and the brand's success is "people stay longer without planning to".
- **What it produces:** a peak that is a non-decision: the next round offered once, when the table is ready, tied to what is coming from the kitchen; the table stays without deciding to. The brand's act at the peak is restraint, not a flourish.
- **What it asks of the brand:** that staff read the table from a distance and that the kitchen's pacing is known to the floor.
- **Failure mode:** the peak is designed as a moment of presence (a flourish, a tasting, a visit from the brewer), which replaces the table's conversation with the brand's.

### S2-C4 — The end as two moments designed as one, with a silence after

- **When to use:** when the book treats bill and farewell as one block and names "transactional ending" as the failure.
- **What it produces:** end-1 (the bill, nothing requested) and end-2 (the farewell as a prediction), one standard across both, and a silent walk out so the last words stay last.
- **What it asks of the brand:** that no review, loyalty or rating request exists anywhere in the arc.
- **Failure mode:** one of the two is designed and the other left to the card machine.

## Template refinements (candidates, not applied)

1. **A "conditional" flag.** Five moments in this map happen at some tables only (a constraint questioned, a question about the making, a late friend, something goes wrong, the return). The house form has no column for it; the flag was carried in the moment name. A one-character column, or a convention in the name, would let Stage 3 design a trigger for each.
2. **A "standing" flag.** Four moments run across a phase (the making in view, conversation takes over, reading the table from a distance, room sound and light) rather than at a point in time. Band 2 positions moments in time; standing moments do not sit at a point. Same fix as above.
3. **Share of the visit for pre- and post-visit phases.** The arc table asks for each phase's share; phases outside the visit have no share of the visit. The map wrote "before the visit" and "after the visit" in the column. The template could say that shares are of the in-venue time.
4. **The chapter's "four persona layers" against a book with three transformation layers plus demographics, psychographics and attitudes.** The intake had to decide what counted as a layer. A one-line definition in the skill's Move 0 would remove the guess.
