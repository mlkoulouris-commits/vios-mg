# Handoff to Stage 3 — Atelier del Gelato

From `journey-map.md` and `reasoning-note.md` (2026-11-01, Brand Book v3). Constraints are grouped by moment cluster; each names its source. Stage 3 designs against these; where the map is silent on a mechanic, the design has latitude and says so.

## A. Threshold and the question (M4–M9)

| Constraint | Detail and source |
| --- | --- |
| Acknowledgement within 10 seconds | Every guest, including one joining a queue; eye contact and a smile before any word. v3 §7.6 Welcome standard, §7.3 team standard. |
| Eyes before words, always | The look comes first even when the team member is mid-scoop for someone else; the word can wait, the look cannot. v3 §7.3 "eye contact before words"; Bible §7 habit. |
| The question is asked at every order, after the eyes, before the order | "What are we celebrating today?" / „Какво празнуваме днес?" Not after the order, not with the bill. v3 §5.2 "spoken at every order"; §7.6. |
| The question is in "we" | Never "What are you celebrating?" The Role is Peer; "we" is the Role in one word. v3 §3.2. |
| A beat after the question (M8, silent) | The team member does not fill the pause, does not supply an answer, does not repeat the question. Reasoning Note §9. |
| "Nothing" is a complete answer | No follow-up, no "come on", no joke; move to choosing warmly; the note still comes. v3 §7.6 "No moment shared: never push." |
| Shared joy capped at intensity 3 | A win is lit up with a smile that reaches the eyes, never party energy, never a raised voice. Bible §9 expression palette. |
| Regulars are greeted by name and usual | "Good to see you again." The question is still asked. v3 §7.6 Regulars; §5.4 greeting. |
| Forbidden lines | "Next!", "Anything else?", "That's our bestseller", "Treat yourself", "You deserve it", any exclamation. v3 §3.2, §8.1; Bible §4. |
| Register in the room | BG ти, EN "you" warm. Bible front page. |

Critical dependency to flag: **the queue**. Whether the question survives a Ring Mall queue, and what the shortened form is if it must, is unanswered (diagnosis contradiction 4; note to Evo 2026-10-30 item 3). Stage 3 designs the Rakovski form and leaves a marked gap for the mall.

## Where the design has latitude

- Who draws the Note from the jar, and whether payment comes before or after it (cluster C).
- The exact words of the one true origin line per flavour (within the fact sheet).
- The shortened mall form of the ritual, once Evo answers.
- The to-go end: where the held attention ends when there is no door walk (the counter edge, or the door).
- Whether M6 (the question overheard) is designed at all, or simply protected by not lowering the voice for the queue.

## What Stage 3 must flag back

1. Any script that needs a fact marked CONFIRM (prices, allergen policy, age line, mall hours).
2. Any moment where two HIGH moments collide on one team member (M23 and M5 at peak hour).
3. Any place where the Ring Mall counter cannot perform the Rakovski design.
