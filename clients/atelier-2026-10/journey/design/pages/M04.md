# M04 · HIGH IMPACT · First look inside

Environmental · The threshold (phase 2) · ~3 sec · Arrival · owner: floor (the designer for materials)

Dominant inputs: **non-verbal** (environment) and **brand**; concept (the room as it is); BE (thin-slicing).

## CURRENT STATE

The second she steps in, before the face looks up (M5). The room reads itself to her. "This is real."

## DO

1. Scent (NV F.3): dairy, vanilla, fresh espresso and nothing else; cleaning done and aired before open; no artificial fragrance (v3 §7.7).
2. Sound (NV F.2): Italian acoustic by day, soft jazz in the evening, low enough to talk over; machine noise minimal; noisy tasks in the lull, not at the door's rhythm; no TV, no radio.
3. Sight: the gelato display lit as the visual centre; the tiramisu collection shown with the same care; the Note jars visible at every station, handwriting face out.
4. Touch (NV D.1): the door handle and the counter edge as the first surfaces (materials **to confirm** against v3 §7.2: honed marble, oak and walnut, linen, handmade ceramics, matte brass).
5. Temperature 20–22 °C (NV F.5); warm directed light (NV F.1).
6. The counter at level eyes (around 90 cm, direction; current height to confirm).
7. Staff visible as at M5: relaxed-upright, hands visible, no phone on the counter.

## SAY

Nothing.

## DON'T

- Cleaning-chemical smell; music chosen for the staff; a dark display; jars behind the till; stacked delivery boxes in the sightline; a phone on the counter.

## NON-VERBAL

All six categories at once (S3-08): the room is the thin-slice before the face. Environment F.1–F.5; staff posture C.1; standing geometry A.3 (the counter height is the geometry).

## BEHAVIORAL ECONOMICS

Thin-slicing (BE 17) and affect (BE 5): the judgement forms here and anchors the visit. Cognitive ease (BE 1): a room that explains itself; nothing she can see is announced (S3-03).

## RECOVERY

| If … | → |
| --- | --- |
| The room is wrong at open (smell, sound, light, display, jars) | R-ROOM: fixed before the next guest; pre-shift checklist signed by the opener. |
| A machine must run loud mid-day | Timed between guests where possible; never while a guest is at the door or tasting. |

**State arc:** Stepping in → Reading the room → Toward the counter

**Success metric:** Pre-shift checklist signed daily; a sampled guest reaches the counter without asking where to go or what the place is.

**Target emotion:** at ease

**Brand pillar:** quality; value Crafted with care, quietly
