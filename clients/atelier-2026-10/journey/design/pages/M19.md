# M19 · SILENT · The first taste

In-room · The ten minutes (phase 5) · the first minute · The table, or walking · ○

## CURRENT STATE

She tastes. The answer to "Taste" is in the cup: made this morning, true origin, cool gelato in a warm room.

## DO

Nothing. M11 and M14 did the work: she chose it with a spoon in her hand, it was scooped with care and no commentary. The room holds (M20). Nobody approaches, nobody asks.

Where the team member is while not acting: at the counter, with the next guest or at a quiet task; the table is not looked at.

## DON'T

What would break the silence:

- "How is it?", "Everything all right?", "Good choice".
- A glance held on her first spoonful.
- Clearing anything, wiping the next table, adjusting her chair.
- Music turned up or a machine started as she begins.

**Why silent (Reasoning Note §9):** private activity. A question about the taste asks her to rate what she came to enjoy and pulls her out of the ten minutes.

**Success metric:** On sampled sit-downs, no staff word to the guest in her first minute at the table.
