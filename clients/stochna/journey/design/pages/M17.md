# M17 · CRITICAL IMPACT · First pour and the bread ★

In-room · ~30 sec · Phase 4, the first round · Staff-delivered · **Signature**

**Dominant inputs:** brand (Made together, the tagline in two objects) · concept (bread and beer landing together, CONFIRM open fact 6) · non-verbal (one sentence, then gone) · BE (the differentiator felt, not told).

## CURRENT STATE
The order is in; the table waited briefly (M16, silent). Now the first beer and the bread land. The question under it: is this real making or performance? After: the first sip, left alone (M18).

## DO
1. Beer and bread leave the bar and the pass **together**, carried by one or two people arriving at the same moment. If they can't land together, R-M17.
2. Bread at the centre of the table, glasses to each person (no announcement of who ordered what; ask once if unsure).
3. **One sentence**, said to the table, not to one guest.
4. Then leave. No "enjoy!", no waiting for a reaction.

## SAY (to lock — blocked on open fact 6)
- EN: "The beer's brewed here. The bread's baked here, with the grain from the brew." · BG: „Бирата е варена тук. Хлябът е изпечен тук, със зърното от варенето.“
- Fallback until the spent-grain bread is confirmed: EN "Brewed here. Baked here. They go together." · BG „Варена тук. Изпечен тук. Вървят заедно.“

The book's longer line ("…from this morning's brew") is not used unless the bread is baked daily from that day's brew (claim, guardrails 9).

## DON'T
- No tasting notes, no style lecture.
- No "our signature bread!"; no claim beyond what is confirmed.
- No beer first and bread five minutes later as the default.
- No hovering for the reaction.

## NON-VERBAL
Place, say, step back (NV D.3 handling of objects; NV B.4 silence after the line). Heavy stoneware, real glass (NV D.1, book § 6.4). Eyes to the table for the line only (NV C.2).

## BEHAVIORAL ECONOMICS
BE 2 WYSIATI: the table judges the whole integration from what is in front of it; two objects landing together say "made together" without argument. BE 5 affect: the first taste colours the plates that follow. BE 11 social proof is not used; the object is the proof.

## RECOVERY
- Bread not ready → beer goes first with EN "The bread's coming out of the oven" · BG „Хлябът излиза от фурната“ only if true, then bread within 5 minutes; else the M19 plates carry the signature (fallback named in `handoff-to-design.md`) (R-M17).
- Wrong beer → swapped at once, "Wrong one — here." · „Сбъркахме — ето.“ (R-WRONG).
