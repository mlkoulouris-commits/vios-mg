# M06 · HIGH IMPACT · The question overheard

In-room · The threshold (phase 2) · 0–5 min · Waiting · owner: floor

Dominant inputs: **brand** and **BE** (social proof, the design of the wait); non-verbal (volume, orientation); concept (one or two stations, a queue).

## CURRENT STATE

Seen (M5), waiting behind one or two guests, watching the guest ahead be asked the question and taste. "They ask everyone. It's real."

## DO

1. The ritual for the guest ahead runs at full form: the question, the beat, the tasting, the Note, at conversational volume. Nothing is shortened because someone is waiting.
2. The waiting guest is addressed only by the look (M5). No repeated "I'll be right with you"; no apology to the queue.
3. The wait has content (NV E.2): she can see the display, the jars, the tasting spoons; the team member's stillness with the guest ahead is the content.
4. Past five minutes: the second station opens; with one on, "Thanks for waiting." at her turn (M05 register).
5. Volume neither raised for the queue nor dropped to hide the question.

## SAY

Nothing to her. The guest ahead's lines are M07–M15.

## DON'T

- "Next!"; hurrying the guest ahead; apologising to the queue; performing the question louder for the audience; eyes on the queue while serving.
- A sign or a line that says "please have your order ready".

## NON-VERBAL

Volume (NV B.3) for one person; orientation (NV C.4) stays with the guest in hand; one look to each newcomer (M5) and back; the wait designed (NV E.2), never filled with apology.

## BEHAVIORAL ECONOMICS

Social proof (BE 11): the ritual witnessed is the proof no caption can give. Cognitive ease (BE 1): she learns the form before her turn, so the question does not surprise her into "nothing". Felt time (NV E.2) shortens when the wait has something to watch.

## RECOVERY

| If … | → |
| --- | --- |
| Three or more waiting, one on the counter | R-QUEUE: the look within 10 seconds holds; the tasting shortens; the question and the Note never. Named degradation; staffing is the fix. |
| Ring Mall with a queue barrier | Form **to confirm** (Evo); until then the Rakovski form. |
| A waiting guest leaves | Nothing called after them. |
