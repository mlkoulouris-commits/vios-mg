# Vios Engage

What happens around an engagement, so the four method plugins are a service and not a toolbox. VIOS Practice Part V (production and version control) and Part IV (when an engagement becomes a case). Requires `vios-core`.

| Order | Skill | Does | Writes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **vios-intake** | the six intake moves: conversation, scope against the four stages, pull from the pattern library, workspace, timeline, confirmation | `[client]/README.md` › Intake record, `open-facts.md` |
| 2 | **vios-proposal** | the proposal the founder signs: stages, deliverables, what the client supplies, timeline, price from `setup/rate-card.md` | Claude Doc, ClickUp task |
| 3 | **vios-engagement-setup** | the workspace: folder from the template, the client's five ClickUp lists and the Deliverables tasks seeded from `setup/clickup-engagement.json`, Drive, reviews on the calendar, the month-0 baseline | the client folder and lists |
| 4 | **vios-harvest** | at close: stage-harvest review, library and template contributions, case recommendation, archive, retrospective, change-log entry proposed | `archive/harvest-engagement.md`, `retrospective.md`, `docs/cases.md`, `docs/changelog-proposals.md` |

`references/parts-4-5-examples-and-production.md` holds Parts IV and V verbatim. Prices come only from `setup/rate-card.md`; when it is empty every price is "to confirm". The three layers: the manuscript (Layer 1) changes only on the owner's word; the patterns (Layer 2) grow with every harvest; each engagement's workspace (Layer 3) is its own, archived read-only at close.
