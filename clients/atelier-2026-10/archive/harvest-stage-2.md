# Harvest — Stage 2, Atelier del Gelato (2026-11-01)

Per § 2.2.7. Candidate entries; promote after a second use.

## New patterns (candidates)

**S2-C1 — The counter arc.** When to use: walk-in retail with no bookings and a visit under fifteen minutes. What it produces: an arc short in the room and long outside it (a pre-visit phase on the street, a post-visit phase around an object the guest keeps), with the signature and the threshold inside the same thirty seconds. What it asks of the brand: to accept that the room phase cannot hold more than three HIGH moments, so the end and the pre-visit carry the rest. Failure mode: a restaurant arc with "seating" and "courses" relabelled.

**S2-C2 — The signature before the peak.** When to use: a ritual with a named opening act and a named closing act (ask → mark it). What it produces: the signature on the opening act, the peak on the closing act, and a silence after the peak. What it asks: that the opening act is never used as an upsell line and the closing act never travels with the bill. Failure mode: the two merged into one "the ritual" moment, which Stage 3 then cannot script.

**S2-C3 — The question overheard.** When to use: a counter with a queue, where guests watch the guest ahead. What it produces: a MEDIUM moment in which the brand performs the ritual for the previous guest at full volume and form, so the waiting guest sees it is for everyone. Failure mode: shortening the ritual under queue pressure, which teaches the queue it is optional.

**S2-C4 — The end as two moments on a counter.** When to use: any format where the next guest is already present when the current one leaves. What it produces: end-1 (the line) and end-2 (attention held until out of sight), designed as one and choreographed against the next guest's threshold. Failure mode: a correct line and a turned back.

**S2-C5 — The parallel B2B arc.** When to use: a brand whose Vision puts its ritual on partner tables. What it produces: a thin phase with its own customer, mapped MEDIUM throughout until a persona exists, so the Vision is in the map without a customer being invented. Failure mode: either omitting the line (the map contradicts the Vision) or weighting it HIGH (Stage 3 designs a moment the brand does not perform).

## Template refinements (suggested)

- The house-form table has no column for **owner (marketing / floor)**, which the guardrails' Journey table needs; the bridge step in the skill currently asks for it to be derived by hand. Add an owner column to the per-phase table, or state in the skill that it is filled at the bridge.
- The skill's percentage shares are given "of the inventory"; it should say whether silent moments count in the denominator. This map states shares of all 31 and counts silences separately.
- Walk-in retail would benefit from a reference arc in the toolkit (§ 2.2.4 lists restaurant, fast-casual, bar, hotel, cafe; "counter" or "takeaway" is the closest to a gelateria and is absent).
