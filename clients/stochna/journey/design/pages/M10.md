# M10 · SILENT · Coats and settling ○

In-room · 1–2 min · Phase 3, the threshold

## DO
Nothing. M09 pointed to the table; the table is already laid (M11). The room's first minute is the guests'.

## DON'T (would break the silence)
- No server arriving while coats are still coming off.
- No "can I take your coats?" ceremony; no menu thrust in hand.
- No staff member waiting at the table.

**Metric:** first approach comes after everyone is seated (observation).
